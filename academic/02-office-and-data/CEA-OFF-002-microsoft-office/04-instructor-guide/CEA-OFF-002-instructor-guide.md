## 1. What this course is

Three artefacts, one ability set: a document you are proud to print, a spreadsheet that computes,
a presentation you can deliver aloud. Not software-button training — workplace literacy with Office
as the vehicle. Accelerate Office-confident students with the stretch tasks at the end of each note.

## 2. Preparation (per session)

Machines with Office 2016+; projector; practice files (`GraceStores-inventory.xlsx`,
`GraceStores-sales.xlsx`, sample letters/CVs/certificates); printer ready (S3, S6); PDFs of all
handouts; spare headphones for quiet drilling; check printer toner before S3 and S6 (print runs).

## 3. Delivery model

105 minutes: 0–10 review quiz (flash questions) · 10–25 topic · 25–45 demonstration ·
45–70 guided · 70–95 independent · 95–110 correction/check · 110–120 summary + next session.
Name keys while clicking; never say "just open that" — point at the ribbon tab by name.

## 4. Session notes & failure points

- **S1 Word.** Save As dialog confuses beginners — demo F12 three times. Failure: closing without
  saving; fix by walking the dialog ("Save this? Yes") until automatic.
- **S2 Formatting.** Font addiction is real — cap every document at two fonts and one accent; police
  with a smile. Find and Replace "Replace all" deletes text when the search box was empty — always
  "Find next" first.
- **S3 Professional docs.** Images pasted at screen size look tiny in print — corner-drag while
  counting aloud "hold Shift" maybe unnecessary; footer repeat behaviour surprises everyone once;
  printers: check A4 selected, 100% scale, margins ok.
- **S4 Excel.** `=` forgot = text city; fix immediately. #VALUE! from text-in-number cells —
  teach "the error is information". Autosum (Alt+=) is the fastest win in the course.
- **S5 Practical Excel.** Fence the total cell ($E$10) when computing % of total — draw the fence
  on the board. Sorting by one column scrambling rows = teach Table-convert first, always.
- **S6 PowerPoint.** Walls of text: every student reads slides until forced to speak — drill the
  "cover the slide, say the point" exercise. Delivery grade = clarity and pacing, not applause.

## 5. Assessment guidance

KCs (10%) self-marked; Workbook practicals (25%) — sign each box; spot-check file formats and
locations (PDF where asked). A1 (7.5%) — letter with letterhead layout, correct business formats
(date, address block, salutation, closing). A2 (7.5%) — live SUM, fenced chart data, printed.
Final (40%): all three artefacts in one sitting; silent observation; gate = PDF opens elsewhere +
live formula cell. Show-and-tell (10%) — student explains choices: why this font, why that chart type.
Critical gate failure = reassess within 2 weeks.

## 6. Equity & access

Colour-blind students: charts get patterns/labels not colour alone. Print cost: draft-mode printing
for practice rounds. Under-18 learners per academy policy. Internet: all tasks are offline; Microsoft
Support available for homework.

## 7. Classroom management

Pair beginners with competent peers for S4; cap pair-work at specific tasks, not at doing the work.
Circulation beats demonstration time after minute 45. Silence during KCs.

## 8. Moderation

One observed final per cohort double-marked; compare rubric rows; adjust descriptors at the same
sitting; file moderation sheet with cohort records.

## 9. Knowledge-check keys

KC1: 1) Ribbon/document area/status bar/tabs/zoom (any 3). 2) Save overwrites current file; Save As
creates a named/new copy. 3) PDF. 4) Undo. 5) A word; a line.
KC2: 1) Bold = key emphasis; italics = terms/titles; caps = never for emphasis. 2) Formats of the
selected text. 3) 1.15–1.5 spacing, 6–12pt after paragraphs. 4) Bullets = unordered lists; numbers =
steps/order. 5) One accent max.
KC3: 1) A4, 1 inch (2.54cm). 2) Different First Page / start numbering at second page via
insert-position Options; or footer from page 2. 3) Header; shaded accent. 4) Fidelity everywhere. 5) Name+contact → statement → experience (any sensible order acceptable).
KC4: 1) Many sheets in one file vs one sheet. 2) Column C, row 7. 3) `=`. 4) Currency symbol &
thousands separators & negative-red. 5) `=SUM(E2:E11)`.
KC5: 1) A Table (structured, auto-format). 2) $ before column/row ($E$10). 3) Pie (≤6 slices). 4) Sort reorders rows; filter hides non-matching rows without reordering. 5) Landscape + Fit to
Width = 1 page + print gridlines off (or on for lab) — accept either with reasoning.
KC6: 1) ~6 lines / ~30 words / 24pt+ body. 2) Title, presenter, context. 3) Transition = slide-to-slide
effect; animation = element-to-element. 4) Hidden script for the presenter. 5) F5 = from start;
Shift+F5 = from current.

## 10. Expected outputs

S3: everyone leaves with a printed letter or certificate draft. S5: everyone has a printed chart page.
S6: everyone has delivered aloud at least 60 seconds of their final deck.

## 11. Rubric (0–4)

0 cannot · 1 heavy prompts · 2 completes with errors · 3 completes unaided, correct · 4 unaided,
clean, explains well. Gates: P-gate (PDF elsewhere-opens + live SUM) required regardless of band.
