## 1. Identity

- **Title:** Microsoft Office · **Department:** Office & Data · **Level:** Absolute beginner
- **Duration:** 3 weeks · 6 sessions · 2 sessions/week · 105 min each · **630 guided minutes**
- **Independent practice target:** 3–6 hours · **Fee:** ₦30,000 · **Cohort:** max 15
- **Delivery:** On-site, Windows machines with Microsoft Office (academy licence) or Word/Excel/PowerPoint trial

## 2. Description

The most requested skill in Nigerian job adverts: "computer literate" means able to produce a clean
Word document, a working Excel sheet and a decent PowerPoint. Three weeks take an absolute beginner
from opening the apps to leaving with three finished artefacts — a CV or business document exported
to PDF, a sales/expense sheet with live formulas and a chart, and a presentation delivered aloud.

## 3. Purpose

Word & Excel & PowerPoint competence is the gateway into clerical, administrative, sales-support,
logistics and church/school/NGO office roles, and the daily tool of any small business. Office
skills without practice decay instantly; this course is built around producing finished documents.

## 4. Audience

- Beginners with CEA-OFF-001 or equivalent basic computer confidence
- NYSC corps members, job-seekers and graduates preparing applications
- Office workers, church/school/NGO administrators, SME owners and staff

## 5. Entry requirements

- **CEA-OFF-001 Typing & Computer Basics** or observed equivalent (power on, files, typing ≥ 15 WPM)
- A practice machine at the academy (no laptop needed)
- Software: Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint (any 2016+ or Microsoft 365)

## 6. Learning outcomes

By the end, the student can:

1. Create, format and print a professional Word document (letters, CVs, certificates) and export it to PDF.
2. Build a working spreadsheet: entry, formatting, SUM/AVERAGE/MIN/MAX/COUNT, sorting, filtering, percentages and one chart.
3. Design a 5–7 slide presentation and present it aloud in front of the class.
4. Export, print and share work in the formats employers expect (PDF, printed hard copy, email attachment).

## 9. Practical exercises

Workbook CEA-ACA-WB-OFF-002: typed business letter, CV, formatted report page, expense sheet with
formulas, chart from data, 6-slide deck, print/PDF export checklist.

## 10. Assessment plan (100%)

| Component                                                       | Weight |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Knowledge checks 1–6                                            | 10%    |
| Practical exercises (workbook)                                  | 25%    |
| Assignments A1–A2                                               | 15%    |
| Final practical project (document + spreadsheet + presentation) | 40%    |
| Practical demonstration & explanation (show-and-tell)           | 10%    |

Pass ≥ 50% **and** critical gate: a printed/PDF Word document opened in a different application and a
spreadsheet cell whose formula is live (edit a number, totals change). One reassessment per
component within two weeks; +1 grade boundary max.

## 11. Final project

**"Three artefacts, one sitting"** (brief CEA-ACA-FP-OFF-002): under observation, produce (a) a
formatted CV or business letter exported to PDF; (b) an expense/sales sheet with a live SUM total
and one chart, printed; (c) deliver a 5–7 slide presentation aloud. Marked with CEA-ACA-RUB-OFF-002.

## 12. Completion & certification

Certificate of completion on ≥ 50% overall + critical gate + attendance ≥ 75% (5/6 sessions; missed
session's assignment owed) + final practical passed + feedback form filed. Certificate states
hands-on completion; not an external qualification.

## 13. Further learning

Data Entry (CEA-OFF-003) → Digital Marketing (CEA-BIZ-001) or Web Design (CEA-WEB-001).
Progression: Advanced Excel/spreadsheets and data analytics (CEA-OFF-006, rotating).
