## Brief

In one observed sitting (max 60 minutes):

1. **Document.** Produce either a formatted CV or a formal business letter (choice at sitting start)
   with correct structure, restrained styling, header/footer, exported to PDF and printed.
2. **Spreadsheet.** Build or extend the expense/sales sheet: at least 8 data rows, columns
   date/item/qty/price/total, live SUM, one AVERAGE or MAX/MIN, one chart with title and axis
   labels, printed landscape, fit-to-width.
3. **Presentation.** Deliver 5–7 slides aloud (3–6 minutes) on your chosen theme: CV walk-through,
   Grace Stores online-sales pitch, or a club/department proposal. Speaker notes allowed; reading
   slides aloud is not delivery.

## Conditions

No starter files; notes from this course permitted; no internet; no neighbours. You may ask one
procedural question; answer will be "your choice".

## Marking

CEA-ACA-RUB-OFF-002 bands (P1–P8, keys P2 & P4 gate). Show-and-tell (10%): name your two best
choices and one you would redo — scored on reasoning, never on confidence.

## Submission

Printed artefacts filed in the cohort file; PDF copy to the LMS portfolio slot within 7 days.
Feedback form completed before certificate processing.
