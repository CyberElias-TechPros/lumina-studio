## 1. Welcome

"Computer literate" is the line on every job advert that secretly decides interviews. This course
teaches the three artefacts that line really means: a document that looks professional, a
spreadsheet that actually computes, and a presentation you can deliver aloud. Every session ends
with something printed, saved as PDF or presented.

## 2. Course overview

- **Level:** Absolute beginner (some computer confidence assumed — see entry requirements).
- **Duration:** 3 weeks · 6 sessions · 105 minutes each.
- **Rhythm:** 10 review · 15 topic · 20 demonstration · 25 guided · 25 independent · 15 check · 10 summary.
- **Effort:** Bring a personal goal — a CV, a church flyer, a sales sheet, a proposal deck. We build it.

## 3. Entry requirements & equipment

- CEA-OFF-001 or observed equivalent (power, files, typing).
- Academy machines with Microsoft Office; your own laptop works too (any 2016+ or Microsoft 365).
- Bring documents you actually need: a dull CV, a real price list, a messy roster — real stakes learn faster.

## 4. Learning outcomes

1. Produce a professionally formatted business document from a blank page.
2. Build a working spreadsheet with formulas, sorting and charts.
3. Design and deliver a 5–7 slide presentation from scratch.
4. Export, print and share work in the formats employers expect.

## 8. Practical activities

All workbook tasks use fictional businesses (e.g. "Grace Stores, Port Harcourt") and invented data.
Never type real family or customer records into practice files.

## 9. Assignments

- **A1 (due S3):** Type a one-page business letter for a fictional Port Harcourt business on your
  own letterhead layout; save as PDF; print. (7.5%)
- **A2 (due S5):** Build a mini expense sheet for one fictional shop week with SUM totals and one
  chart; print to PDF. (7.5%)

## 10. Assessment

Knowledge checks 1–6 (10%) · Workbook practicals (25%) · A1–A2 (15%) · Final project (40%) ·
Show-and-tell (10%). Pass ≥ 50% with the critical gate (a real printed Word document opened from
PDF, and a live formula cell). Grades: 80+ A · 70–79 B · 60–69 C · 50–59 D.

## 11. Final project — "Three artefacts"

(a) Formatted CV or letter exported to PDF · (b) expense/sales sheet with live SUM and one chart,
printed · (c) a 5–7 slide presentation delivered aloud. Brief: CEA-ACA-FP-OFF-002. Marked with
CEA-ACA-RUB-OFF-002.

## 12. Rubric summary

Artefacts judged on: correctness (does it do the thing), presentation (does it look like work),
judgement (did they choose sensibly). Full rubric in 11-marking-rubrics/.
