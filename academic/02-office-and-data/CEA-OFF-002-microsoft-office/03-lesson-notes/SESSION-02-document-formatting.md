**1 — Outcomes**
Format text with fonts, sizes, bold/italic/underline, colour and highlight; set alignment, line and
paragraph spacing and indentation; use bullets and numbering; apply borders; use Format Painter;
Find and Replace.

**2 — Prerequisites**
Session 1. You can create, save, reopen a document unaided.

**3 — Introduction**
A document that _reads_ professionally earns trust before a single word is believed. Same text,
same ransom-note styling: rejected. Same text, consistent font, clean spacing, real alignment: read.
Today your pages start looking like work employers pay for.

**4 — Key concepts**
Font family (Calibri/Georgia/Cambria — pick one per document), size hierarchy (body 10–12, headings
larger/bold), emphasis (bold/italic — never all-caps for emphasis), colour restraint (one accent),
alignment (left for body, centre for headings/titles), line spacing (1.0–1.5), paragraph spacing
(6–12pt after), indentation (first-line or block), lists (bullets for unordered, numbers for steps),
borders & page colour (subtle), Format Painter (copy formats), Find and Replace (Ctrl+H).

**5 — Demonstration**
Turn a plain paragraph into a styled page: Title (20pt bold, centred), a subtitle, left-aligned body,
a bulleted requirements list, numbered steps, one accent colour on headings only; demonstrate Format
Painter on two headings; then Find and Replace `&` → `and`; show "change all" danger.

**6 — Guided practice**
Reformat the `about-me.docx` from S1: title, heading hierarchy, one bullet list, one numbered list,
justified body, 6pt paragraph spacing, one accent colour on headings. Swap with a partner: critique
two improvements and one flaw.

**7 — Independent practice**
Format a one-page circular for Grace Stores announcing Saturday-only opening hours: title, date line,
two bullet sections, contact block, border around the whole notice. Print it.

**8 — Real-world example**
A Batawa (Port Harcourt) bakery flyer that mixes 8 fonts loses customers to a competitor with one
clean layout. Design rules: maximum two fonts, one accent colour, white space is free and healthy.

**9 — Knowledge check 2**

1. When do you use bold vs italics vs caps? 2. What does Format Painter copy? 3. Default spacing
   recommendation for body text? 4. Bullets vs numbers? 5. One rule about accent colours.

**10 — Common mistakes**
Font soup; underline for emphasis (it means "link"); all-caps shouting; centred body paragraphs
(books are left-aligned); illogical indentation; replacing with empty string accidentally (Ctrl+H —
check "Replace" not "Replace all" first).

**11 — Summary**
Two fonts max, one accent, left-aligned body, 1.15 spacing, lists where they help, Format Painter
to repeat, Find and Replace with care.

**12 — Assignment**
A1 begins (business letter for a fictional Port Harcourt business on letterhead layout) — draft the
text only today; formatting comes in S3. 5% now, 5% on PDF export next week.

**13 — Reflection**
Compare your S1 plain page with today's. Bring the flyer to S3 — we add tables, images and PDF.
