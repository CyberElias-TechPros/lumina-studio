## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Voluntary show-and-tell of homework forms. Safety briefing for timed tests: accuracy is the gate,
speed is the report — a nervous 98% beats a frazzled 85%.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Test design.** Block A (10 min): accuracy test — 40 lines of mixed fields (no timing pressure,
complete within block). Block B (5 min): speed sprint — type the standard paragraph `THE QUICK BROWN
FOX...` oscillator as fast AND clean as possible. Both blocks marked on raw keystrokes vs source.

**Scoring.** Accuracy % = correct cells ÷ total cells. Count errors, not vibes: compare by eye
side-by-side per row (instructor method), or EXACT helper column for text. Typical trainees score
96–99% in Block A after S3; below 95% means measurement hygiene (verify step) is missing — retrain
on the finger-ruler read-back.

**Speed interpretation.** Report WPM honestly with the accuracy beside it. A pair of numbers
(96% @ 48 WPM) is a respectable professional CV line. Inflated numbers fail recruitment tests and
cost you the job — nobody wins.

**Cleanup brief.** Deliverable 2 of the final project: clean `GraceStores-dump-500.csv` — 500 rows,
messy by construction (mixed locales, duplicates, capitalisation, blanks). Summary sheet with:
rows in/out, duplicates removed, error counts by type, your measured accuracy.

**The report.** One page. Sections: Dataset, What I found (counts), What I did, My measured accuracy
(both tests), What I'll do differently. Honest, numeric, signed.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Timed mock: everyone types a 5-field line set with the finger-ruler verify shown on screen
(instructor's own stopwatch visible). Then instructor opens `accuracy-report-template.xlsx` and
fills it from the mock — the exact shape of the assessed report.

## 45–75 · Assessed final — Blocks A & B

Machines isolated (no neighbours, no internet). Block A paper-based transcription (10 min, 10 pts).
Block B speed sprint (5 min, report-only). Ten minutes rest. Then Block C: cleanup start — continue
on `GraceStores-dump-500.csv` under observation (no time pressure but quality gate applies).

## 75–100 · Cleanup completion & report

Finish the 500-row cleanup as portfolio work; start the one-page report. Instructor circulates with
the yellow-flag protocol. Reports due same day.

## 100–110 · Correction & check

KC4: (1) Accuracy gate and what it means on 500 cells. (2) Why EXACT beats eyes.
(3) Name two report sections besides numbers. (4) You finished Block A with 2 unreadable cells — protocol?
(5) What does unsigned honest reporting beat? Self-mark; sign show-and-tell slot for S4+1 review if booked.

## 110–120 · Summary & next steps

You now hold measured evidence. Portfolio: clean dataset (PDF) + report. Certificate gated on 98%+
accuracy. Alumni pathway: Data Analytics (CEA-DAT-001) if you want queries and charts; Digital
Productivity for Microsoft 365 fluency.

## Answers — KC4

1. ≥98% = max 10 wrong cells in 500 — the recruitment standard. 2) EXACT catches case/trailing-space
   differences eyes gloss over. 3) Any of: dataset description, what I found, what I did, improvements.
2. Yellow-flag, complete, report honestly — never invent. 5) Inflated speed claims that collapse in
   a live recruitment test.
