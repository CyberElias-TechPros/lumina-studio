## Final project

F1–F8 per assessment sheet. Show-and-tell (10): 10 names cause + prevention · 7 cause only ·
5 recount · ≤3 evasive.

## A1 — cleaned register (15%)

15: cleaned register + summary + report, zero errors found on 20-row spot-check · 12: 1–3 spot-check
errors · 8: majors (date locale/phones) unfixed · ≤5: incomplete or fabricated rows (→ reassess).

## Workbook practicals (25%)

All boxes signed → 25; ≤1 missed → 22; 2–3 missed → 17; more → ≤12. Gate-relevant boxes (2.4, 3.4,
4.2–4.4) cannot all be missing.

## Knowledge checks (10%)

Full 2.5 · partial 1.5 · attempt 0.5 · blank 0 per KC.

## Calibration

Accuracy gate is absolute. Do not round 97.6% to 98%. Two assessors agree on gate status at every
touch-point; discrepancies resolved on the physical sheet with the flags visible.
