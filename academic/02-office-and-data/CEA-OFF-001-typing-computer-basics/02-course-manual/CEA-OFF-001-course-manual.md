## 1. Welcome

You have joined this course because computers run modern work, study and business — and you want
to stop standing at the edge of that world. Good. This course does not test your memory and it
does not reward fast hands. It teaches you to sit down, on your own, and get real things done:
a finished folder structure, a found file, a sent email. By the end of four sessions, the machine
will feel ordinary. That is the point.

## 2. Course overview

- **Level:** Absolute beginner. No previous experience is assumed.
- **Duration:** 2 weeks · 4 sessions · 105 minutes each · ~7 guided hours.
- **Delivery:** Instructor demonstration → guided practice → independent practice, every session.
- **Cost in effort:** Bring the practice; the ninety minutes in the lab are only half the course.
  Ten focused minutes of typing drill daily is worth more than a skipped week of classes.
- **Prerequisites:** None. Curiosity and attendance.

## 3. Entry requirements and equipment

- Academy practice machine (no laptop needed to enrol).
- Comfortable clothing; you will be typing.
- Software used in class: Windows, Microsoft Edge or Chrome, File Explorer, Notepad,
  a word processor (Word or Writer), and an email account you will create in Session 4.
- If you own a phone but never a computer, you are the target student, not a special case.

## 4. Learning outcomes

By the end of CEA-OFF-001 you will be able to:

1. Power on/off correctly, log in, and navigate the desktop — taskbar, Start menu, windows,
   applications — without help.
2. Create, rename, move, copy, delete, restore files and folders, and use a USB drive safely.
3. Search the internet effectively and download a file safely.
4. Create and use an email account, including attachments and online forms.
5. Type with correct finger placement at 15+ WPM and 90%+ accuracy (25+ WPM stretch goal).
6. Apply digital safety basics: strong passwords, MFA awareness, scam and phishing recognition.

The full competency map is in Section 7.

| Component                                                 | Weight |

- Minimum pass: **50% overall plus the critical practical gate**: correct power on/off,
  save-and-locate a file, and send an email with attachment — observed, once.
- Reassessment: one attempt per component, within two weeks, capped at one grade boundary.
- Grades: 80–100 A · 70–79 B · 60–69 C · 50–59 D · 0–49 not yet competent.

## 11. Final project

**Unaided independence demonstration** (brief: CEA-ACA-FP-OFF-001). In one observed sitting you will,
with no prompts: create a folder, save a document into it, find a specific file online, download it
safely, attach it to an email you compose yourself, and send it. Then you will _explain_ each step
aloud — what you did and why. Marks use CEA-ACA-RUB-OFF-001. Your result goes into your portfolio;
your certificate is issued only after this is passed.
