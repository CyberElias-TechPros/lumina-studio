## Purpose

Evidence that a beginner who finishes this course can complete ordinary computer tasks alone —
the exact claim printed on the certificate and repeated on the website: _"You finish by performing
a set of computer tasks on your own… No prompts, no help."_

## What you must do (observed once, in one sitting)

Starting from a switched-off machine:

1. Power on the computer and log in.
2. In Documents, create a folder named `CEA-Final`.
3. Open Notepad; type a short note: your name, today's date, and one sentence about what you will
   use this skill for; save it inside `CEA-Final` as `about-me.txt`.
4. Find an **official** document online about computer or internet safety (any Nigerian government,
   education or established-industry source you recognise), and download it.
5. Check the downloaded file's name and extension before opening it.
6. Compose an email to your practice partner: subject `My CEA course project`, body naming the file
   you downloaded, and **attach the file**.
7. Send the email. Confirm it appears in your Sent folder.
8. Close everything, sign out, and shut down correctly.
9. **Explain each step aloud** — what you did and why — as you go (feeds the 10% show-and-tell).

## Conditions

- No notes, no neighbours, no instructor help. The instructor records silently.
- You may ask procedural questions you initiate; the answer will be "your choice".
- Internet must be working for steps 4–7; if it fails, the download step is deferred to a
  same-day fallback slot (USB file provided), and the rest is completed unaided.
- Time allowed: 30 minutes. Most students finish in 15–20.

## Marking

Marked with the project rubric (CEA-ACA-RUB-OFF-001) against nine observable behaviours P1–P9
(see assessments file). Critical: P3 (save into folder), P6 (attachment email sent). Any critical
failure = reassessment within two weeks.

## Submission and records

- The outcome is recorded on the practical assessment sheet; the sheet is filed in the cohort file.
- The `CEA-Final` folder and its files remain yours; they are the seed of your portfolio.
- Passing this project plus the critical gate plus ≥50% overall triggers certificate processing
  per the certification policy.
