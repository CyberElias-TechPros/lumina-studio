## Section 1 — Understanding the Computer

**Activity 1.1 — Name the machine (guided)**
Skill: hardware identification · 10 min
Scenario: Grace Stores (fictional provision shop) bought a new desktop. The owner does not know
its parts.
Instructions: Using the machine in front of you, write the name of each labelled part: (a) power
cable, (b) display cable, (c) system unit, (d) monitor, (e) keyboard, (f) mouse, (g) USB port,
(h) printer port.
Expected output: 8 labels correct.
Quality checklist: [ ] all eight named · [ ] spelled acceptably · [ ] no part confused
Completion: signed ____ Date ____

**Activity 1.2 — The full start-up sequence (guided)**
Skill: correct power procedure · 10 min
Instructions: From completely off, perform: system-unit power → sign in → open Notepad → type
your name → close Notepad → Sign out. Then log in again and list the steps in order.
Expected output: a correctly ordered written sequence.
Completion: signed ____ Date ____

**Activity 1.3 — Desktop detective (independent)**
Skill: desktop orientation · 15 min
Instructions: After logging in, identify and write: two desktop icons, the clock, the Wi-Fi/sound
area, the Start button's exact location, and the name of the currently open window.
Completion: signed ____ Date ____

**Activity 1.4 — Troubleshooting card (scenario)**
Skill: basic fault reporting · 5 min
Scenario: The monitor shows nothing, but the system unit light is on and the fan hums.
Write what you would check first (hint: cable, input source, power button on the monitor) and what
you would never do (force anything off mid-boot).
Completion: signed ____ Date ____

## Section 2 — Keyboard & Mouse

**Activity 2.1 — Mouse agility (timed)**
Skill: click control · 10 min
Instructions: Open Notepad and File Explorer. In 60 seconds: (1) click Start, close it;
(2) right-click the desktop once; (3) double-click Recycle Bin; (4) drag an icon 5 cm;
(5) scroll a window with the wheel. Instructor observes and ticks.
