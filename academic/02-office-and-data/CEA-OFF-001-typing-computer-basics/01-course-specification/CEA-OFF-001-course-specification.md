## 1. Identity

- **Title:** Typing & Computer Basics
- **Department:** Office & Data
- **Level:** Absolute beginner
- **Duration:** 2 weeks · 4 sessions · two sessions per week
- **Guided contact minutes:** 4 × 105 = 420 minutes (~7 hours)
- **Independent practice target:** 2–4 hours (workbook + assignments + touch-typing drills)
- **Delivery:** On-site at 24 Ebony Road, Port Harcourt · instructor-led · practice machines
- **Cohort size:** Max 15 per instructor (machines permitting), min 1
- **Fee:** ₦20,000 (see website for current fees and intake status)

## 2. Course description

The on-ramp course. A complete beginner or a phone-confident learner leaves able to sit down at a
computer, turn it on and off correctly, navigate the desktop, manage files and folders, search and
download safely, create and use an email account with attachments and online forms, and type at a
beginner-useful speed with correct finger placement. Four sessions remove the fear of the machine
and unlock every other course the academy offers.

## 3. Course purpose

CEA's practical courses assume a student can reliably complete basic computer tasks without help.
This course exists to guarantee that baseline in a supervised environment before any other CEA
course begins — for absolute beginners, older learners returning to work or study, and learners
who use phones daily but have never operated a desktop workflow.

## 4. Audience

- Complete beginners of any age who have never used a computer
- Parents and older learners returning to work, business or study
- Students and graduates preparing for the workplace
- Phone-heavy users moving to desktop workflows
- Anyone who is "scared of breaking it" and needs supervised practice

## 5. Entry requirements and equipment

- None academic. Willingness to practise, including at home where a device is available.
- Academy practice machine; no laptop required to enrol.
- Comfortable clothing for typing sessions.
- Software: Windows (academy standard), Microsoft Edge or Chrome, File Explorer, a text editor
  (Notepad), an email account (free, created in class), a word processor (Microsoft Word or
  LibreOffice Writer).

## 6. Learning outcomes

By the end of CEA-OFF-001 the student can:

1. Power a computer on and off correctly, log in, and navigate the desktop (taskbar, Start menu,
   windows, applications) without prompting.
2. Create, rename, move, copy, delete and restore files and folders; use a USB drive safely.
3. Search the internet effectively and download a file safely.
4. Create and use an email account: send/receive, attach and download attachments, complete a
   basic online form.
5. Type using correct home-row finger placement at a minimum of 15 words per minute (WPM) with
   accuracy above 90%; 25+ WPM is the stretch target for drill-compliant students.
6. Apply basic digital safety: strong passwords, MFA awareness, scam and phishing recognition.

## 9. Practical exercises

Guided and independent exercises in the companion workbook (CEA-ACA-WB-OFF-001): folder-structure
build, naming drills, browser and search tasks, safe-download checklist, email send-with-attachment,
form completion, scam-spotting scenarios, and the weekly typing drill log.

## 10. Assessment plan (100%)

| Component                             | Weight | Form                                                    |
| ------------------------------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Knowledge checks (4 × 2.5%)           | 10%    | In-session quizzes 1–4                                  |
| Practical exercises                   | 25%    | Workbook activities, marked                             |
| Assignments (3 × 5%)                  | 15%    | S1→S2, S2→S3, S3→S4 tasks                               |
| Final practical project               | 40%    | Unaided independence demonstration (CEA-ACA-FP-OFF-001) |
| Practical demonstration & explanation | 10%    | "Show and tell": student explains each task aloud       |

Pass = 50% overall **and** mandatory observation of: correct power on/off, file save/locate,
and email with attachment (critical practical gate). One reassessment per component within two weeks
of feedback; reassessment may raise a component by at most one grade boundary.

## 11. Final project

Unaided independence practical (full brief: CEA-ACA-FP-OFF-001): the student performs a defined set
of ordinary computer tasks alone — create a folder, save a document into it, find a file online,
download it safely, attach it to an email, send it — observed without prompting.

## 12. Completion and certification

Certificate of completion (CEA-OFF-001) awarded on: ≥50% overall, the critical practical gate passed,
final practical submitted and observed, attendance ≥ 75% (3 of 4 sessions; the missed session's
assignment must still be completed), and the student feedback form submitted. Certificate number and
verification per the CEA certification policy. The certificate states hands-on completion of the
course; it is not an external qualification.
