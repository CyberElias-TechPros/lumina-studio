## What you will walk out with

A working personal productivity system, built by you, that survives your next job: organised inbox
with filters that work while you sleep, a folder + naming convention that scales, files in the cloud
properly, a shared calendar and a live Google Form with real responses in a Sheet.

## The four sessions

### S1 — Google Workspace Essentials

Docs (writing + comments + suggestions), Sheets (tables + basic formulas), Slides (clean decks),
Drive (upload/folders/sharing/permissions; link vs invite; view/comment/edit) and real-time
collaboration without chaos (who owns the doc, one voice in suggestions, resolving threads).

### S2 — Calendar & Forms

Calendar: events, guests, reminders, time zones, shared calendars. Forms: quizzes vs surveys,
collecting responses, required fields, Forms→Sheets auto-logging, and turning responses into decisions.

### S3 — Email Management

Inbox zero: capture, clarify, decide. Labels and folders, filters and rules (e.g. "receipt → Finance/2026"),
archive vs delete, templates and signatures, unsubscribe vs filter, notifications off by default,
weekend batching. Build your three critical filters live.

### S4 — Files, Naming & Cloud Backup

Folder structures that mirror how you think (Client / Year / Project or Area / Year), naming conventions
(`YYYY-MM-DD-client-doc-vN.ext`), cloud backup strategy (sync vs upload vs backup — they are NOT the
same), recovering lost/overwritten files, and your 15-minute weekly tidy ritual.

## Assessment weighting

KCs 10% · workbook 25% · A1 shared Doc 7.5% · A2 Form pipeline 7.5% · final personal system 40% · show-and-tell 10%.

Gate: restore one file from cloud during the final — live, in front of the assessor.

## Classroom rules

Work in the class sandbox account unless your own account is ready — never bring real client/salary
data into a training account. Naming before saving, every file, every time.

## Bring

Your messiest problem folder listing (names only) — we will triage it in S4.
