## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Folder structures that scale.** Pattern: Area → Project → Year → files. Example:
`Work/GraceStores/2026/` · `School/Exams/2026/`. Three levels deep usually wins; mirrors on Drive and
PC. If you can't decide the folder in 5 seconds, the folders are wrong.

**Naming conventions.** `YYYY-MM-DD-client-document-vN.ext` → `2026-11-16-grace-members-v2.xlsx`.
Date-first sorts chronologically by default. Lowercase, hyphens, no spaces (safe everywhere), no
special characters, meaningful words, no "final" in names — the version number IS finality.

**Cloud backup, three doors — don't confuse them.** (1) **Sync** (Drive for desktop, OneDrive): mirrors
a folder — deleting on one side deletes the other; it is NOT backup. (2) **Upload/Backup** separate
copy with retention — Google Takeout, OneDrive backup, external drive. (3) **Version history** inside
Google docs — un-does edits, not lost disks. You need at least one of (2) or an external drive; sync
alone is a false sense of security.

**Lost file recovery.** Drive search + filters · File ▸ Version history (Google docs) · Trash (30 days)
· ctrl+z-of-last-resort. Lost laptop? Drive web from another machine shows everything synced.

**Weekly 15-minute tidy ritual.** Downloads folder empty (sort to folders or delete), star today's
files, one filename fixed per week, backup spot-checked once per month.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor opens a projected chaos folder (40 files): triages into `Areas/Years`, renames five to

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: the **naming rescue** — 15 messy files handed out (USB/sandbox share); rename all to
convention, sort into a two-level tree, write a 3-line README.txt at root. Instructor spot-checks
two trees per pair.

## 70–95 · Final — your personal system (observed)

Solo (observed, ~25 min): show the assessor (1) your organised folder tree with ≥5 correctly named
files, (2) your cloud backup of one file — restored live to your machine, (3) your inbox with your
three filters, (4) your shared calendar entry for the Weekly Tidy. Assessor checklist P1–P6 applies.

## Answers — KC4

1. Sync mirrors (delete-one-deletes-other); backup keeps an independent retained copy. 2) Default alphabetical sort becomes chronological sort. 3) Area → Year, or Client → Project → Year. 4) Trash, 30 days. 5) Downloads empty, star today's files, fix one filename, monthly backup check.
