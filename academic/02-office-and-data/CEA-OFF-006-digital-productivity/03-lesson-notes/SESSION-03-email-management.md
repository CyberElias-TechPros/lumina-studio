## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Ask for two volunteers to share their inbox counts unvarnished. "By the end of this session you'll have fewer messages and more trust in the ones left."

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Inbox zero, honestly.** Not "all emails answered in 60 seconds" — a decision for every message:
do (≤2 min, now) · delegate · defer (to calendar/task) · archive. The inbox holds new items only.

**Labels & folders.** Label = tag that lives ON the message (a message can carry several); unlike
folders, labels don't move files. Colour labels sparingly (Client/2026, Finance, Actions, Waiting).

**Filters & rules.** Gmail: Settings ▸ Filters. Rule shapes: from:boss@x.com → label Actions +
star · to:me Listserv → archive · subject:receipt → label Finance/2026 + skip inbox · attachment:pdf
from:bank → label Bank. Build three live; a good filter saves hours per year.

**Archive vs delete.** Archive = remove from inbox, searchable forever. Delete = gone. Receipts:
archive, never delete. Identity docs: archive twice. Spam: report + delete.

**Templates, signatures & discipline.** Templates (Settings ▸ Advanced ▸ Templates or snippet) for
your 5 recurring replies; signature with name/role/phone; notifications off for anything except
known-humans; batch checks (09/13/16) instead of every-ping.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor takes a projected live sandbox inbox (400 messages) — builds three filters, bulk-archives
newsletters via search `unsubscribe in:inbox` + select all, sets up a template reply, moves 30 to
Finance/2026 in 20 seconds. Stopwatch visible.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs on sandbox accounts: each builds ≥3 filters (one Actions, one Finance-style, one newsletter-killer),
bulk-archives the sandbox newsletters, creates one reply template. Report inbox counts before/after.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: **Inbox triage plan** — pick your actual pain pattern (too many CC's? newsletters? WhatsApp-to-email?),
write your three filters with exact search queries, your signature, your check-times. Apply to your
real inbox if it's a sandbox-safe account; otherwise implement on the sandbox and list "to port later".

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Inbox is a decision queue, not a warehouse. Next: files, naming, folders, cloud backup — the last myths to bust.

## Answers — KC3

1. Removes from inbox; stays searchable. 2) Label tags the message (multi-label OK); folder moves it (single place). 3) subject:receipt OR subject:invoice (any receipt/invoice terms) → label + archive. 4) Off by default; batch at set times; humans-only push. 5) Repeated identical replies — thank-you, booking confirm, payment received.
