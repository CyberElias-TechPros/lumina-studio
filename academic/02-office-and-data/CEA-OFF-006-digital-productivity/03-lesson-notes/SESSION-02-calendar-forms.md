## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Project a calendar with double-booked 4 PM meetings. "A shared calendar is a social contract. Today we sign it."

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Calendar mechanics.** Create events (title, when, duration, location/link), guests + invites,
reminders (30/10/0 min), time zones (outsiders may live 1–2 hours off), recurring events (weekly
1:1, monthly dues), colour coding by life-area (work/home/church/study).

**Shared calendars.** Create "CEA-2026 Cohort" calendar; subscribe class on phones; booking a slot =
commitment; decline with a reason ("can Thu 2pm instead?") — not silence.

**Google Forms.** New Form → questions (multiple choice, checkbox, short answer, date, paragraph) ·
required fields · sections · confirmation message · link distribution · **Responses ▸ Sheets
(Create spreadsheet)** — every submission autologs one row: timestamp + answers. Forms are the
fastest intake pipeline in the suite.

**From responses to decisions.** Sheet autoloaded → filter/pivot-lite: count by option, average
ratings, export CSV for the boss. Ten responses beat one meeting with opinions.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor builds a live Form: "Class Lunch Order — what do you want?" (choice list + notes +
required name); shares link to room; 8+ submissions land; open linked Sheet; filter counts; agenda
set on the shared calendar for the lunch; calendar invite to cohort.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: build **Course Feedback lite** — 4 questions (rating 1–10, "what helped most", "what confused
you", name optional); wire to Sheet; submit 5 responses from classmates; verify rows appear.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo A2 build: your own Form for a real purpose (choir rehearsal attendance / shop order intake /
study group availability) — must collect ≥5 real responses during class; deliver the linked Sheet
URL + 3 findings in one line each (e.g. "8 of 12 said mornings").

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC2: (1) Two reminders worth setting for a 4 PM meeting. (2) What does the linked Sheet contain?
(3) Why make a question required? (4) One time-zone trap for online meetings. (5) Two Forms questions types.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Calendar = public honesty; Forms = intake without paper. Next: taming the inbox — Week 2 begins.

## Answers — KC2

1. 30 and 10 minutes (or 10 and 0). 2) Timestamp + one row per response with a column per question. 3) Incomplete rows poison averages/counts. 4) Invitees may assume their local time when someone says "4 pm". 5) Any two: multiple choice, checkbox, short answer, date, scale, paragraph.
