## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Show a projected shared Google Doc mid-edit chaos — 4 cursors, 3 comments, 1 unresolved thread.
"This is what we'll tame today."

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**The suite.** Docs (writing, suggestions mode, comments) · Sheets (tables, autofill, SUM) · Slides
(one message per slide, templates) · Drive (your hard drive in the sky: upload, folders, search,
share) · all synced across phone/laptop in real time.

**Sharing & permissions.** Three doors: **Viewer** (read only) · **Commenter** (suggest) · **Editor**
(change). Link-sharing vs named invites. Default to link "view" for outsiders; "edit" only for
owners. "Anyone with the link" is a PUBLIC door — check it like a light switch.

**Real-time collaboration.** Suggestions vs direct edits · comments (@mention persons) · resolving
threads · version history (File ▸ Version history) as the undo-of-last-resort · one doc = one
owner. Never "final-final(2)" — there is one doc, versioned by the system.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor creates `CEA-Class-Brief.gdoc` live: shares to room as Editor; students type their name in
the attendance table simultaneously; instructor shows version history rewind; then switches sharing to
"Commenter" mid-exercise — the class instantly feels the difference.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: A creates a Doc ("Team Lunch Plan"), shares as Commenter; B adds 3 suggestions; A resolves
threads; B opens version history and restores A's earlier draft after a staged "disaster delete".
Swap roles with a new Doc.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: build a personal **Workspace starter**: one Doc (your week), one Sheet (groceries + SUM total),
one Slide (3-line self-intro) in your Drive root folder `CEA-2026/Office`. Share each with
instructor as Commenter.

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC1: (1) Three permission levels. (2) What does "anyone with the link" mean for a Viewer link?
(3) Where does version history live? (4) Suggestions mode vs direct editing. (5) Two reasons Sheets beats typed totals.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Suite + sharing + version history. Next: Calendar and Forms — scheduling and collecting data properly.

## Answers — KC1

1. Viewer / Commenter / Editor. 2) Public-read — anyone who has the URL opens it; treat like a photocopy left on a table. 3) File ▸ Version history (Google auto-saves and versions continuously). 4) Suggestions are proposed edits the owner accepts/rejects; direct edits change the doc immediately. 5) Live recalculation, sort/filter, no re-adding up typed numbers.
