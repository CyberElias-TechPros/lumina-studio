## What you will walk out with

A documented, verified AI workflow for one recurring task of yours — the exact prompts you use, the
checks that keep you honest, and the private line you will not cross — ready to show an employer.

## The four sessions

### S1 — What AI Tools Actually Are

Language models: next-token prediction at scale, trained on text, frozen at a date. What that buys
(fluency, structure, breadth) and what it cannot guarantee (truth, freshness, judgement, privacy).
Choosing between tools; costs of ignoring limits: confident nonsense with citations.

### S2 — Prompt Writing

Role + context + task + constraints + examples + output format. Iterating: ask for critique, ask for
alternatives, constrain length, demand sources. Building a reusable prompt library with your own
tested prompts — the highest-leverage habit in the course.

### S3 — AI-Assisted Research & Work

AI as research accelerator, never as source of truth. Summarising long documents, drafting emails
and reports, brainstorming structures — then the verification loop: every claim checked against a
real source, citation captured, unchecked claims labelled or removed.

### S4 — Responsible Use

Hallucinations (invented citations, wrong numbers), bias (whose voices are absent), privacy
(client data, credentials, trade secrets — never paste), workplace and academic honesty. You will
draft and sign your own one-page personal AI policy.

## Assessment weighting

KCs 10% · workbook 25% · A1 verified AI deliverable 15% · final workflow + policy 40% · show-and-tell 10%.
Gate: verification log on the final — every claim checked.

## Classroom rules

No client data, ever. Outputs are drafts to verify, not answers to copy. Offline-safe alternatives
listed each session in case tools are unreachable.

## Bring

A recurring task you hate (expense reports, meeting minutes, customer letters…) — S2 onwards we build
your workflow on it. Glasses of water; a notebook for prompt drafts.
