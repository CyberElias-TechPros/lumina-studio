## Brief

In one observed sitting (30 minutes) produce **one page**:

1. The recurring task of yours AI will help with (2 lines).
2. Tools you will use, and why (2 lines).
3. Your five library prompts for it (from S2/S3) with parameter placeholders.
4. Your verification steps — which claims you check, against what.
5. Your private line — the data you never share, the disclosure rule you follow at work/study.
6. Your signed personal AI policy (from S4; attach if not on the page).

are rejected; final text claiming facts outside your head/material must carry the verification log.
