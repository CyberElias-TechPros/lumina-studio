## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

A1 verification logs walked through in pairs; two volunteers share one checked fix they're proud of.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Hallucination autopsy.** Invented case law, wrong statute numbers, fake DOIs, "about X kg" numbers
that are simply wrong. Signature smells: over-specific stats, citations with no stable page, universal
claims ("all experts agree"). Countermeasure: demand sources, then open them.

**Bias.** Models mirror their corpus: more Western, English-first, male-coded in technical contexts.
Example catch: a hiring description that "should" match engineering candidates skews against women —
rewrite prompts to demand inclusive drafts and check who is missing.

**Privacy.** Never paste: client data, salaries, credentials, medical/legal specifics, family details,
security questions, unreleased financials. Anonymise ("[CLIENT]", "[SUM ~₦2m]"). Assume anything you
type may be stored, reviewed or used for training — read the tool's terms. Workplaces increasingly
have AI policies; violating them is disciplinary, not "creative".

**Honesty — workplace & academic.** Submitting AI-written work as unaided (exams, contract reports)
is misconduct. Legitimate: AI-assisted drafting you disclose where required and have verified."Friend,
did you write this?" deserves a true answer.

**Your personal AI policy.** One page: tools allowed at work/home · data classes never shared ·
verification rule for external outputs · disclosure rule for study/work · learning stance (use to
understand, not to bypass). Sign, date, file in portfolio.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor red-teams a student's A1 draft live: plants three hallucinated specifics; class finds them.
Then co-writes a model personal policy on the board from class input.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs stress-test policies: swap drafts, attack each other's ("What about my client's invoice data?
Payslip templates? NYSC data?") — patch gaps; sign final version.

## 70–95 · Final project — Documented personal AI workflow

Solo (observed, 30 min): produce **one page** — your recurring task, tools used, the five prompts
from your library, the verification steps, the private line, the honesty rule. This plus A1 form your
final project (40%).

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC4: (1) Three hallucination smells. (2) What must never go into a prompt? (3) Disclosure — when is
AI-written work "dishonest"? (4) What is the model policy minimum outline? (5) One-line summary of
this course.

## 110–120 · Course close

Certificate gate = verification log on final project. Keep your library alive. AI fluency without
verification is just faster error.

## Answers — KC4

1. Over-specific uncheckable stats; unstable citations; universal claims. 2) Private/client data,
   credentials, secrets, anything unreleased. 3) When it is submitted as unaided where independence is
   required (exams, signed reports) or violates workplace policy. 4) Tools allowed · data never shared ·
   verification rule · disclosure rule · learning stance. 5) AI accelerates; you verify — always.

## Homework

None. Live your policy; review it after your first month of use.
