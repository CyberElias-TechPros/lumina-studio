## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Trends and comparisons.** Period-over-period %, moving average (3-month) to strip noise, index to
100 for cross-outlet comparison, mix-shift reporting, top-customer concentration (80/20).

**Correlation vs causation.** r = −1..1 measures co-movement only. Classic trap: sales ↑ and WhatsApp
group messages ↑ together — because both follow weekend footfall, not each other. Spurious patterns from
small n, seasonal artefacts, confounders. The only tools: more n, holdout time windows, a plausible
mechanism. Never write "caused by" in a report without a designed test.

**Sample size honesty.** n<30 → descriptives only, "early signal, not a conclusion"; print n beside every
stat; margins of error widen as n falls; show raw counts beside percentages.

**Analysis checklist (the one-pager):** question · dataset & n · 3 findings with numbers ·
1 action with owner and timeline · 1 limitation · confidence honest (High/Med/Low).
