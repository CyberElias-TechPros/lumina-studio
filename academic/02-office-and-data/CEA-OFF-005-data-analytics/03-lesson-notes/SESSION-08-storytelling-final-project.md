## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Room reset; presentation order drawn; the manager role (instructor) gets a notepad and a red pen.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Structure of a 3-minute finding.** Hook ("Grace Stores lost ₦X in June because…") → the one number →
the chart you would show → the action with an owner and a date → the risk if ignored → the ask.
Never walk through every panel — answer first, support second.

**Visual storytelling.** One slide/panel per finding; walk the eyes left-to-right (conclusion → evidence);
highlight the one bar that matters in the accent colour; annotate on-chart ("stockout, 11 days");
source line bottom-left; end on the ask slide.

**Presenting to a manager.** They interrupt — that's the job; answer in one sentence: "What does it cost
me?" → "₦X/month". "Where did you get it?" → "Source sheet, rows 412–498, n=86." Concede limits fast.

## 25–45 · Final project briefing

In the observed sitting (30 min): finalise dashboard (gate level) · deliver your analysis note aloud
(3–4 min) to the manager role · hand over the printed one-pager. Rubrics apply (P-gate: live slicers +
zero hardcoded numbers + insight-titled panels).

## 45–90 · Presentations (observed)

Each student presents; class watches with the "would you act?" card. Manager role asks the three
standard questions (cost / source / n). Instructor scores P1–P8 sheet.

## 90–110 · Correction & check

KC8: (1) First sentence of a finding pitch. (2) Why action with owner and date. (3) One question a
good manager always asks. (4) What may never leave the room without a source. (5) Honest close line of
an analysis note.

## 110–120 · Course close

Certificates gate on P-gate. Portfolio: dashboard + note + one-pager. Alumni pathway: business
freelancing, digital marketing dashboards, or advanced BI (Power BI/Looker) later.

## Answers — KC8

1. The decision-relevant hook with the number. 2) Undated/ownerless actions don't happen. 3) Cost /
   source / n (any one). 4) Any number put in front of a decision. 5) Limitations + confidence level, signed.
