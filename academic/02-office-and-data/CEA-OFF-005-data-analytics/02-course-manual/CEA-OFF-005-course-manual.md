## What you will walk out with

An interactive Excel or Sheets dashboard built over a real messy dataset — cleaned, pivoted, visualised,
sliced — plus a one-page written analysis stating what the data shows and what a manager should do about it.

## The eight sessions

S1 Data structure & tidy data · S2 Cleaning & validation · S3 Formulas & lookups · S4 Pivot tables & summaries ·
S5 Choosing & building charts · S6 Dashboards (layout, slicers, conditional formatting) · S7 Reading the numbers
(trends, correlation vs causation, sample-size honesty) · S8 Storytelling & final project.

## Assessment weighting

KCs 10% · workbook 25% · A1 cleaning drill 15% · final dashboard + analysis note 40% · show-and-tell 10%.
Gate: the dashboard opens, recalculates on slicer change, and the note recommendations match the data — an unfenced-opinion note fails.

## Classroom rules

Tidy before you trust. Show the work. Correlation is never a confession.
