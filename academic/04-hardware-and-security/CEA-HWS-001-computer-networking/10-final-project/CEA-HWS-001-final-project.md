## Brief

Observed, timed (8 min per card, three cards):

1. **Bench A — physical:** deliverables are diagnosis and fix of a deliberately broken media fault (cable/link). Verify at the far bench, not your own.
2. **Bench B — addressing/DHCP:** deliverable is a bench whose DHCP died or mis-scoped; restore addressing for both test laptops and the printer. Verify connectivity each way.
3. **Bench C — DNS/WAN:** deliverable is internet-down-but-LAN-up (or vice versa); fix with the ladder only; document.

## Conditions

Ladder only; speak your hypothesis before touching; change once at a time; tools allowed: ipconfig/ip, ping, tracert/traceroute, cable tester, router status pages; no config exports from neighbours.

## Marking

CEA-ACA-QA-HWS-001 (P1–P6; gate P3 + P4, absolute). Show-and-tell: how your hypothesis survived contact with reality.

## Submission

Fault sheets + A1 plan to cohort file; portfolio PDF within 7 days; feedback form before certificate.
