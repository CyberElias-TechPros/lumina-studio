## What you will walk out with

A small-office network you planned, wired, addressed, secured and troubleshot — plus the documentation
an IT manager would expect on Monday morning: addressing table, SSID/VLAN map, fault log template.

## The six sessions

S1 Fundamentals (LAN/WAN/internet, layers in plain talk, cables & media) · S2 IP addressing (IPv4,
subnet masks, DHCP vs static, NAT) · S3 Routers & switches (config, ports, VLANs) · S4 Wi-Fi (standards,
bands, SSID/security, placement, guest) · S5 Troubleshooting tools (ping, tracert, DNS, DHCP, status pages)
· S6 Final practical with fault cards.

## Assessment weighting

KCs 10% · workbook 25% · A1 addressing + Wi-Fi plan 15% · final practical 40% · show-and-tell 10%.
Gate: end-to-end connectivity incl. guest isolation + one fault card solved unaided.

## Classroom rules

Lab first, slides after lunch. Label every cable you touch today. No fault card opened before attempting diagnosis.
