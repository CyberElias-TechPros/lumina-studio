## What you will walk out with

A faulted machine diagnosed, fixed, serviced and returned to a documented owner handover — the complete
cycle every repair shop runs daily, understood and practised.

## The eight sessions

S1 Inside the machine (components and failure signatures) · S2 Laptops, safety and identification (ESD,
batteries) · S3 Disassembly and cleaning (thermal paste, dust, cables) · S4 Upgrades (RAM, SSD, compatibility,
cloning) · S5 Power/display/heat faults · S6 Storage/memory/boot/Windows faults · S7 Software servicing
(OS, drivers, backup, accounts) · S8 Service workflow + final practical with a signed customer sheet.

## Assessment weighting

KCs 10% · workbook 25% · A1 diagnosis essays 15% · final practical 40% · show-and-tell 10%.
Gate: safety discipline witnessed throughout.

## Classroom rules

Unplug, prove dead, wrist strap on. Photos before hands. No guessing-swaps — measure first.
