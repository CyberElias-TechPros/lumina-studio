## Brief

One deliberately faulted machine, one hour, solo:

1. **Intake (5 min):** symptom in the customer's words (from the intake card), photo the machine, confirm data-backup signature discussion.
2. **Diagnose (≤35 min):** the ladder — hands on a powered/open machine forbidden; one change at a time; log every step.
3. **Fix + verify (≤10 min):** machine boots or the documented truth of what it needs.
4. **Service (if time):** dust/paste/updates quick pass or deferred in writing with reasons.
5. **Handover (final 5 min):** explain in plain language: what failed, what we did, what to watch, 3-month labour warranty line, care advice.

## Conditions

Course notes allowed; ladder only; tools from bench kit; no phone help; no swapping brain-dead assumptions —
an honest "this bench cannot fix it; board-level work" triage is a competent, passing outcome when documented.

## Marking

CEA-ACA-QA-HWS-002 (P1–P6; gate P4 safety, absolute). Show-and-tell: the fault story — hypothesis vs what the machine made you change your mind about.

## Submission

Job card + service documentation to cohort file; portfolio PDF within 7 days; feedback form before certificate.
