## 1. Identity

- **Title:** Computer Repairs · **Department:** Hardware & Security · **Level:** Beginner
- **Duration:** 4 weeks · 8 sessions · 2/week · 105 min · **840 guided minutes**
- **Fee:** ₦50,000 · **Cohort:** max 12
- **Delivery:** On-site repair lab; donor desktops + laptops, ESD mats, tools kits, spare PSUs/RAMs/SSDs, deliberately faulted machines

## 2. Description

From ESD discipline to a signed customer service report: identify every major component, disassemble and
clean safely, upgrade RAM/storage, diagnose no-power / no-display / heat / boot / storage / Windows
faults methodically, install OS and drivers, and write the documentation a customer pays for.

## 3. Purpose

Computer repair is the most trusted trade in Nigerian digital life — speed matters less than trust and
diagnostics. This course produces the technician who diagnoses instead of guess-swapping parts.

## 4. Audience

Beginners entering the trade · phone/gadget technicians adding laptops · SME IT officers · home fixers going professional.

## 5. Entry requirements

Basic computer use; no repair experience needed; willingness to get hands dusty (literally).

## 6. Learning outcomes

1. Identify every major desktop and laptop component and explain its failure signatures.
2. Work safely: ESD, batteries, PSU, thermal risks; power off, unplug, discharge, verify.
3. Open, clean, service, upgrade and reassemble desktops and laptops.
4. Diagnose systematically: no-power, no-POST/no-display, overheating, storage, RAM, boot, Windows/driver faults.
5. Install OS, drivers, updates; back up; document a complete service.

## 9. Practical exercises

Component scavenger hunt (S1–2) · deep clean day (S3) · clone swap (S4) · fault gauntlet (S5–6) · OS from USB (S7) · full intake-to-return service (S8).

## 10. Assessment

| Component                                                                       | Weight |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Knowledge checks (8 × 5)                                                        | 10%    |
| Workbook practicals                                                             | 25%    |
| Assignment A1 — written diagnosis of three recorded fault logs                  | 15%    |
| Final practical — complete service of a deliberately faulted machine (observed) | 40%    |
| Show-and-tell — fault story: symptom, hypothesis, fix, proof                    | 10%    |

**Critical gate:** safety — no powered hands on an open machine, no bypassed PSU, batteries handled correctly; any safety violation = immediate stop and remediation (gate failure on the final until cleared).

## 11. Critical behaviors

Unplug and prove it dead before hands in · one component at a time with check-back · photos before disassembly · customer data handled like your own · honest "I don't know yet" beats a wrong swap.

## 12. Learning barriers

Force (laptop clips snap); fear of the unknown; parts-swapping before measurement ("RAM shot!" — it was the CMOS battery).

## 13–16. Delivery · QA · Resources · Completion

Lab with donor machines; PSU tester; thermal paste; USB installers; bench PSU. Attendance ≥ 6/8; gate passed; service docs filed; feedback form before certificate.
