## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Safety-card recitation in pairs — one reads the step, other answers the reason.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Opening desktops.** Side panel off, photo of the interior NOW (cable map), remove: GPU first (two screws + PCIe clip),
then RAM, then storage, then coolers; bag and label screws (lid vs board). Cable management: unplug by the plug never
the wire; note the fan headers direction.

**Cleaning.** Compressed air in short bursts OUTDOORS or with catch box; never spin fans with air at full (overspin);
soft brush for heatsink fins; alcohol + lint-free cloth for board/ contacts; repaste CPU: pea-size drop, spread-thin
(or trust pressure mount); clean old paste first with alcohol; fan blades each wiped; PSU vents dusty is the heat culprit usually.

**Laptop maintenance.** Bottom-panel screws out (mind hidden screws under feet), battery first, then fan + heat-pipe inspection
(paste dry = spider-web of crust), repaste if trained on the bench — some models need shell split, bench units only in class.

**Reassembly discipline.** Photos in reverse; connect battery LAST; test POST before case closed; screws pattern (diagonal),
no leftover screws (count!) — count-in count-out is the professional oath.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor deep-cleans a donor desktop end-to-end: photos → strip → air → brush → repaste → reassemble → POST OK + temperature check (BIOS or software reading before/after — record 12°C cooler).

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: deep clean one donor desktop each (rotating benches); fill the cleaning log sheet (fault found: dust dam, cracked fan, CMOS dead); reassemble; boot.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: write the **customer service note** for your cleaned machine: work done, temperatures before/after, recommendations ("repaste due in 18 months", "dust filter every 3 months"). File to workbook.

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC3: (1) First cable discipline. (2) Air bursts — where, why short? (3) Repaste first step. (4) Screw count oath. (5) Boot test timing.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Clean machine, honest temperature. Next: RAM and SSDs — the upgrades customers love.

## Answers — KC3

1. Pull plugs not wires; photos before anything. 2) Short bursts, aimed to blow dust OUT (not deeper in); outdoor or catch box. 3) Scrape/wipe ALL old paste with alcohol to bare metal; degrease. 4) Count out = count in; a loose screw in a PSU/board is a short circuit patiently waiting. 5) Before closing the case — POST check with lid open or at least monitor temp right away.
