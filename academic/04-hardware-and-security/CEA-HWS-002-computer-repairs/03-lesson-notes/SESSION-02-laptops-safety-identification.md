## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Three home-case stories read out — no one touched anything live; applause.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Desktop vs laptop brains.** Same concepts, tinier: soldered CPU (often), SO-DIMM (laptop RAM sticks) vs DIMM,
M.2 or 2.5" SATA, DC barrel/USB-C in from a brick, keyboard ribbon cable to motherboard, display flex cable
(hinges — the #1 cable killer), cooling via heat pipes to a small fan, CMOS rare (battery soldered/detachable).

**ESD safety.** Your body carries static that quietly kills semiconductors. Wrist strap clipped to bare metal
of the case · mat on the bench · handled by edges only · no carpet shoes · components in anti-static bags never
bare laps. One shock you never felt = one dead socket you can't see.

**Power safety.** Unplug before opening (not "off at switch" — the CORD out). PSU internals are off-limits — never open a PSU brick;
if it smells burnt, recycle it. Laptop battery: disconnect FIRST before any board work — a live board + screwdriver bolt = fire risk,
and "the fan still spins" does not mean safe.

**Identification drill.** Real benches: desktop + two laptop donors — name every component from the sheet including flex cables, fan headers, Wi-Fi card, webcam ribbon.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Opening two donors: desktop then laptop (remove battery first on cam), narrating ribbon-cable discipline ("pull the tab"),
photograph for documentation, anti-static handling on every component touched; compare SO-DIMM vs DIMM side by side.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: identification rotation across two benches with the sheet — desktop parts, laptop parts, safety call-outs
(name the live-risk first in each setup). Compare answers with key; misses re-drilled.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: safety checklist card — 15 checks (unplug/strap/discharge/battery/mat/bags…) written from memory, then corrected against master; carry it forever. File to workbook.

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC2: (1) Order: open laptop to touch board — what first? (2) ESD kills how — silently or spectacularly? (3) PSU-brick rule. (4) SO-DIMM vs DIMM. (5) Ribbon cable golden rule.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Small machines, sharp edges. Next: disassembly and the big clean.

## Answers — KC2

1. Remove battery (then mains) and verify dead. 2) Silently — no spark, maybe dead socket. 3) Never open the PSU housing — replace as a unit; recycle burnt units. 4) Laptop is shorter/different key notch; never "desktop bit in the laptop slot" — incompatible. 5) Pull tabs, never the wire; flex the tab up, then the cable.
