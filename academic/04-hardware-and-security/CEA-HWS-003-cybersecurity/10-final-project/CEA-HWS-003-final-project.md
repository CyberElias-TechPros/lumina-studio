## Brief

Your fictional client (Grace Stores, 12-person retail, POS till, WhatsApp catalogue, one shared laptop, customer list, no written policy):

1. **Asset inventory** — name them, size them (sensitivity + criticality, H/M/L).
2. **Threats** — realistic for this business: SWAPPED SIMs, weak POS hygiene, staff phish, stranger on Wi-Fi, WhatsApp impersonation, unbacked customer list.
3. **Weaknesses** — you may not invent multi-million-dollar apparatus; you may honestly assume default router password and no MFA — label assumptions.
4. **Recommendations** — one per weakness: rank, named owner (role is fine), deadline (day 7/14/30), cost band (₦-free/cheap/paid).
5. **30-day checklist** — the boss ticks with evidence.
6. **Residual risks** — three in writing for the owner to accept.
