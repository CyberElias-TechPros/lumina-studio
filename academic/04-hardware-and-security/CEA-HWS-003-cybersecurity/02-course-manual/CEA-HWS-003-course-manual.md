## What you will walk out with

A security assessment report your boss or your own shop can act on: assets, threats, weaknesses, ranked
recommendations, 30-day checklist — plus the habits (MFA, passphrases, phishing radar) that protect your accounts from tonight.

## The eight sessions

S1 Threats/risk/CIA · S2 Authentication vs authorisation, defence in depth · S3 Passwords, managers, MFA
· S4 Phishing & scams (sorting drills) · S5 Networks/Wi-Fi security · S6 Encryption/HTTPS · S7 Incident
response + backups · S8 Policies, risk report writing, final project pitch.

## Assessment weighting

KCs 10% · workbook 25% · A1 home-hardening audit 15% · final assessment 40% · show-and-tell 10%.
Gate: risk-ranked, owner-assigned, time-bound findings.

## Classroom rules

Never test or probe anything outside the lab examples. Click nothing suspicious, ever — even "safely".
Stories welcome, names of real victims anonymised.
