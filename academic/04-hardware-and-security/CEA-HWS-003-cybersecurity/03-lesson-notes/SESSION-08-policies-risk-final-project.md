## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Two verified restores shown; one person found none — that's the lecture.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Policy as professionalism.** Acceptable-use (work machines aren't for cracked games) · password/MFA policy (unique + manager + MFA, enforced) ·
incident reporting duty ("who do I call, in how many minutes") · backup policy (owner, schedule, tested quarterly) · vendor/account lifecycle (leavers' access killed same-day).
Three-page policy beats none.

**The security checklist SME.** 20-line monthly walk: admin passwords · MFA · updates · antivirus health · backup test · user list · Wi-Fi password audit ·
suspicious-message training last done when · POS/till lock screen · e-waste handling.

**The assessment report structure.** Asset inventory → threat list (ranked) → weaknesses (evidence) → recommendations (owner + deadline) → 30-day checklist → residual-risks accepted.
One page per section; board reads it in 4 minutes — that's the test.

**Final project.** Your assigned fictional org (bank branch / clinic / school / church / shop): produce this exact structure from their press-release-bottom facts (name, size, assets) + your eight sessions of judgement.

## 25–45 · Final project (individual writing)

Solo (observed, 30 min): draft the assessment — five sections, every finding ranked H/M/L with one owner + one deadline each. Instructor circulates with challenge questions ("what's the likelihood of THAT?").

## 45–80 · Board pitches

Per student: 3 minutes — "Here are Grace's three worst risks and the 30-day fix list" — challenge cards from room role-played as "the board". Assessor scores P-gate sheet.

## 80–100 · Feedback & wrap

Feedback forms; certificate gate stated; career paths (analyst, GRC, SOC, cloud security bootcamps); one honest rule: keep learning — threats graduate every quarter.

## 100–110 · Correction & check

KC8: (1) Three policy pages that matter. (2) Assessment section order. (3) Every finding needs two tags. (4) Residual risk means. (5) 30-day checklist success definition.

## 110–120 · Course close

Certificate gate: risk-ranked, owner-assigned, time-bound findings tapped on every item. Portfolio: audit (A1), IR card, assessment, workbook.

Acceptable-use, password/MFA, incident-reporting duty (any 3). 2) Assets → threats → weaknesses → recommendations → checklist. 3) Likelihood/impact rating + named owner & date. 4) What remains after controls — boss accepts consciously, in writing. 5) Every item ticked with evidence, not vibes, at day 30.

## Homework
