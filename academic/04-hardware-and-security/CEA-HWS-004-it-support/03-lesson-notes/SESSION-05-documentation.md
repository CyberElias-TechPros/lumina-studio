## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Ticket notes that help the next person — even if that's future you.** Fields: what user said (verbatim one line) · asset/serial · when · what you checked (in order) · what changed · verification result · time spent · next step if it recurs. One story, five lines, no novels.

**Knowledge-base articles.** Problem (searchable title: "Print spooler won't restart") · Symptoms · Cause (one line) · Steps (numbered, copy-paste-proof) · Verify · If-this-fails → escalate-to + what info to attach. A KB article is a gift to yourself at 2am.

**Asset records.** One line per device: asset tag, user, location, config, warranty to, notes ("keyboard replaced 2026-03"). Without this, every audit is archaeology.

**Escalation notes.** L2 needs three things fast: symptom verbatim, steps already tried with results, screenshots/logs — not opinions, evidence. "Escalated to L2 at 11:40 with spooler logs attached; awaiting callback."

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor shows past-ticket disaster ("Printer broken — fixed") vs model entry ("Queue jammed on FIN-PC-07 after batch invoice job; spooler restarted; default printer re-set per user; verified two test pages; recurs weekly — cron task suspected; logged to owner for L2"); class picks apart both on board.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Solo→pairs: upgrade the two worst tickets you can invent/remember into model notes; swap; partner must be able to act on them blind.

## 70–95 · Independent practice (A1)

Solo: **five KB articles** finalised on real bench faults you saw (S4 cards qualify) + asset-line sample for three machines. File to workbook.
