## 1. What this course is

Rehearsal for the hardest calls in any building: scared, angry and in-a-hurry humans with broken machines.
The simulation is the curriculum; the ladder and the calm voice are the tools.

## 2. Preparation

Seeded fault lab (printer with wedged queue, Wi-Fi AP, permission-locked folder, storage-full VM); headsets or phones;
P1–P6 cards; 8 call scripts with escalating difficulty; playbook templates; stopwatch; "you are L1" poster.

## 3. Delivery model

105 min: 0–10 watch-this-good-call · 10–25 teach · 25–45 demo · 45–70 guided role-pairs · 70–95 solo · 95–110 KC · 110–120 close.

## 4. Failure points

- S1: students queue everything P1 — show the payroll scenario and the broken mouse same queue, debate openly.
- S3: remote-support password typing by instructor — never; always the user types.
- S4: printer spooler fear — do it once slowly on the projector; it clicks forever after.
- S6 gate: anyone "fixing" ransomware-scenario = stop, reset scenario, teach isolate-and-escalate; no heroics.

## 5. Assessment guidance

A1 KBs marked on actionability (could a stranger follow it?) — not on length. Final: simulation P1–P6 + playbook completeness;
gate: two faults diagnosed correctly, one with escalation judgment; failure = one reassessment in 2 weeks.

## 6. Equity & access

Hearing-impaired roles: chat-only support scenario option; low-literacy callers encouraged (realistic and humane); no accents mocked — ever.

## 7. Knowledge-check keys

KC1: 1) Report→ack→triage→priority/assign→work→verify-close. 2) e.g. server-down / funny mouse. 3) Timestamped reply stops anxiety bleeding. 4) ASSET — symptom. 5) Any two tone rules.
KC2: 1) Reproduce/isolate/fix/confirm. 2) Cure needs proof of disease. 3) Software, hardware/environment, physical event. 4) User does it at their seat, witnessed. 5) Two failed diagnosed attempts, or security/legal/SLA breach.
KC3: 1) Name, time, mirror. 2) Consent first, narrated clicks, they type secrets. 3) One-time, forced change, logged. 4) "Just", "obviously". 5) First instinct is often wrong under pressure.
KC4: 1) USB/power/toner/queue. 2) services.msc → Print Spooler → Restart. 3) Default printer is per-user. 4) Tuesday announced + rollback ready. 5) Groups (NTFS/share) + account status.
KC5: 1) Symptom, asset, ordered checks, change, verification (+time). 2) Title/symptoms/cause/steps/verify/escalate-if. 3) Symptom, tried+results, logs. 4) Tag, user, location, config, warranty, notes. 5) Feelings are not searchable; logs are.
KC6: 1) Reproduce/isolate/fix/confirm. 2) Slow down, mirror, plan aloud. 3) Verbatim symptom, tried+results, logs. 4) User performs it successfully once. 5) "Just"/"obviously".

## 8. Expected outputs & calibration

S2: ladder recited cold by everyone. S4: spooler cleared by each student once on a bench. S6: two-witness gates; debrief card shared with cohort.

## 9. Rubric (0–4)

0 dangerous/cannot · 1 heavy prompts · 2 completes with missed steps · 3 unaided correct · 4 unaided + raises room's standard. Gate: two faults fixed + one correct escalation.
