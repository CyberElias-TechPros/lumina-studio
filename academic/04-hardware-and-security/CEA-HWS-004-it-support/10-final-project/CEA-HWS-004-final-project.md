## Brief

Observed call simulation (6 minutes per call, rotating roles):

1. Take the seeded call — acknowledge within your own SLA card, mirror the problem in plain words.
2. Run the ladder: reproduce → isolate → fix (smallest change first) → confirm at their seat (their account, their hands).
3. One call (announced by card) is out of your depth — the only correct move is isolation + L2 escalation
   with a complete evidence package (symptom, tried, logs). Guessing is a gate failure.
4. Close every ticket verbally: what changed, what to watch, when to call again.
5. Playbook: clip your five KB articles + cover line into a named booklet and present it.

## Conditions

Notes (your own) permitted; scripts available only as emergency cards; simulated calls only — no real customer data used.
