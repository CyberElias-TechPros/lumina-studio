## What you will walk out with

A ten-fault support playbook written in plain steps — the document real helpdesks run on — plus simulation
hours and the patience muscles that make people trust you with their dying laptop and their pride.

## The six sessions

S1 How support works (triage, SLA, tickets) · S2 Structured troubleshooting (reproduce → isolate → fix → confirm)
· S3 User support (calls, remote control with consent, password resets) · S4 Hardware & software breadth (printers, software, patches) ·
S5 Documentation (ticket notes, KB articles, asset records) · S6 Support simulation (recorded calls + your playbook).

## Assessment weighting

KCs 10% · workbook 25% · A1 knowledge-base ×5 15% · final playbook + simulation 40% · show-and-tell 10%.
Gate: two simulated faults handled, one with correct escalation.

## Classroom rules

Be the calm. Never blame the user — the system failed them somewhere. Everything written down before coffee.
