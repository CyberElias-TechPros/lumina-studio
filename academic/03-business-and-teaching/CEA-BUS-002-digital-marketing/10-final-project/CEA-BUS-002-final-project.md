## Brief

One observed sitting (30 min) + a 3-minute pitch to the class:

1. **Marketing plan** — persona (named) · value proposition (one sentence) · two platforms + WhatsApp flow + Google Business profile steps · why this mix (budget logic).
2. **One-week content calendar** — ≥6 posts, pillars named, hook + CTA per post, three captions fully written.
3. **Three ad concepts** — hook, body, CTA, targeting (geo/interest/age), budget (₦/day × days), objective chosen.
4. **Illustrative report** — filled honestly and labelled ILLUSTRATIVE: spend, reach, leads, cost/result, one next-month change.
5. **Pitch** — 3 minutes; end on the ask.
