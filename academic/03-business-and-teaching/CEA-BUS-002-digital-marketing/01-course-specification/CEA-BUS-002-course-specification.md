## 1. Identity

- **Title:** Digital Marketing · **Department:** Business & Teaching · **Level:** Beginner
- **Duration:** 4 weeks · 8 sessions · 2/week · 105 min · **840 guided minutes**
- **Fee:** ₦40,000 · **Cohort:** max 15
- **Delivery:** On-site; classroom machines with browser; phone marketing accounts optional, personal accounts never required

## 2. Description

A complete campaign, not a theory tour: students build a marketing plan for a real or fictional
business — audience, value proposition, platform strategy, one-week content calendar, ad concepts
with copy and targeting, landing page logic, and a measurement plan with a reporting template.

## 3. Purpose

Most Nigerian SMEs spend on boosting posts without a plan. This course produces the plan — budgeted,
targeted, measured — and the vocabulary to run it with an agency or in-house.

## 4. Audience

SME owners and staff · graduates targeting marketing roles · creators going commercial · community organisers.

## 5. Entry requirements

Basic computer literacy; willingness to analyse real brands; no prior marketing background needed.

## 6. Learning outcomes

1. Write a digital marketing plan: customer, problems, value proposition, channels, budget.
2. Choose platforms by audience behaviour (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, web, email, search).
3. Produce content types with captions and CTAs; build a one-week content calendar and brand voice.
4. Run organic and paid promotion: targeting, objectives, ad creative, landing pages, WhatsApp conversion.
5. Read reach, impressions, engagement, CTR, cost per result; report a campaign honestly.

## 9. Practical exercises

Persona card (S1) · platform matrix (S2) · caption ladder (S3) · one-week calendar (S4, A1) ·
ad concept sprint (S6) · report teardown (S7).

## 10. Assessment

| Component                                                    | Weight |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------ |
| Knowledge checks (8 × 5)                                     | 10%    |
| Workbook practicals                                          | 25%    |
| Assignment A1 — one-week content calendar + three ads        | 15%    |
| Final project — complete campaign plan, presented (observed) | 40%    |
| Show-and-tell — defend your channel mix and budget           | 10%    |

**Critical gate:** the plan must connect spend → objective → metric — a plan with channels but no measurable objective fails; reassess once.

## 11. Critical behaviors

Numbers before opinions; test one variable at a time; CTAs everywhere; report with the same honesty you'd want from your accountant.

## 12. Learning barriers

Boost-and-pray temptation; copying influencers blindly; metrics worship without money sense.

## 13–16. Delivery · QA · Resources · Completion

Live ad-manager screenshots (accounts demo, never personal cards run). Completion: attendance ≥ 6/8; gate passed; campaign plan filed; feedback form before certificate.
