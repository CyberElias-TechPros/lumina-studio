**Do not distribute.** Keys in Section 7.

## 1. What this course is

Campaign craft, end to end: persona → VP → channels → content → ads → numbers → pitch. The class
ante on participation; every student needs a real or vividly fictional business to carry through S1–S8.

## 2. Preparation

Printed persona cards, platform matrix sheets, Canva brand kit templates, Ads Manager screenshot deck
(no live accounts needed), anonymised real campaign report for S7, one-pager report template, print
budget for S8 portfolios.

## 3. Delivery model

105 min: 0–10 board prompt · 10–25 teach · 25–45 demo · 45–70 guided · 70–95 solo/pitch · 95–110 KC · 110–120 close.

## 4. Failure points

- S1: VP written for everybody — force "customers who…" phrasing with a named trigger event.
- S5: students "boost" reflexively — objective must be chosen before the word "ad" leaves their mouth.
- S7: division errors on cost per result — recompute on board twice.
- S8 gate: disconnected spend→objective = reassess; labelled-illustrative is fine, unlabelled is not.

## 5. Assessment guidance

A1: one-week calendar with pillars balanced + 3 full captions; marks on hook strength and CTA specificity.
Final: folder completeness + pitch; client questions standard: cost, audience size, why platform X,
what if budget were halved. Show-and-tell = channel-mix defence.

## 6. Equity & access

No mandatory personal social accounts — fictional businesses permitted; low-data mode (print-first) for all demos.

## 7. Knowledge-check keys

KC1: 1) Same decision, new discovery street; digital = target+measure. 2) Who/what pain/why you/where. 3) Example VP one-liner for chosen business. 4) Any two: profile/photo, pinned post, Google reviews. 5) Specificity breeds writeable copy + screenable creative.
KC2: 1) TikTok. 2) Opt-in only broadcast. 3) Owned over rented. 4) Start with persona's actual hangout; one or two done well. 5) Modelled on your customers — platforms find similar.
KC3: 1) Educate 50/proof 20/entertain 20/promote 10. 2) Stop the scroll ≤3s. 3) One per post. 4) When authentic. 5) Pain + number result + permission.
KC4: 1) Week ahead; batch; 20% fluid. 2) Three adjectives + three "never". 3) Speed + consistency. 4) 5–8 relevant / TikTok keywords. 5) 7 days × pillar × format × hook × CTA × platform + 3 captions.
KC5: 1) Free-slow-compounding vs targeted-measurable. 2) Awareness/consideration/conversion. 3) ₦2–5k/d × 5d; one variable. 4) No objective, no measurement. 5) Click → canned → human ≤15min → D+1 follow.
KC6: 1) Problem-Agitate-Solve. 2) One goal per page. 3) Canned greeting + catalog + 15-min human follow. 4) D+1. 5) 5-second stranger test.
KC7: 1) Uniques vs total views. 2) Spend ÷ results. 3) Weak hook or audience mismatch. 4) Creative fatigue. 5) Point-of-sale "how did you hear?".
KC8: 1) spend → results → cost/result → revenue. 2) Objective/budget, what ran, numbers, worked/didn't, changes, ask. 3) One sentence on cost/results/time. 4) Illustrative ≠ actual. 5) Attributable cause of learning.

## 8. Expected outputs & calibration

S4: A1 calendars complete ≥80% of room; others partial = homework + 1-week extension. S7: cost/result
recomputed aloud to mastery. S8: P-gate agreed by two assessors; pitch judged on clarity not volume.

## 9. Rubric (0–4)

0 cannot · 1 heavy prompts · 2 completes with gaps · 3 unaided, credible · 4 unaided credible + teaches it back. Gate: connected objective→metric.
