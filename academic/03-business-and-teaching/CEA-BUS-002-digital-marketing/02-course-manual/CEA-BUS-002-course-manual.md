## What you will walk out with

A complete, presentable campaign plan for a real or fictional business: audience (named persona), value
proposition, channel mix, one-week calendar, three ad concepts with targeting, and the reporting template
you would hand your boss or client.

## The eight sessions

S1 Fundamentals (customer, problem, value proposition, channels) · S2 Platforms and audiences (who's where,
what for) · S3 Content and storytelling (types, captions, CTAs) · S4 Calendars and brand voice (your A1
one-week calendar) · S5 Organic vs paid (objectives, targeting, budget discipline) · S6 Ads and conversion
(creative, copy, landing pages, WhatsApp flows) · S7 Reading numbers (reach, impressions, CTR, cost/result)
· S8 Reporting and final pitch to "the client".

## Assessment weighting

KCs 10% · workbook 25% · A1 calendar + ads 15% · final campaign 40% · show-and-tell 10%.

## Classroom rules

Bring one brand you love. Real numbers only; if a case study quotes stats, source them or label as illustrative.
No personal ad accounts touched; sandbox screenshots only.
