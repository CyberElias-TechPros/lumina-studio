## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Board: "Marketing is …" — collect 10 raw definitions. Verdict: most say "selling/₦₦" — reality:
marketing makes selling unnecessary because the right person pre-wants the offer.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Traditional vs digital.** Same purchase decision, new streets: discovery happens on feeds/search/WhatsApp
now; measurement is per-impression not per-billboard. Digital wins on targeting + measurement; traditional
wins on local trust still.

**The four questions before any money moves.** WHO is the customer (persona: who, pain, desire, budget,
channel) · WHAT do they struggle with (problem in their words) · WHY you (value proposition: unique,
specific, believable) · WHERE will they meet you (channels: search, social, referral, marketplace).

**Value proposition formula:** For **[audience]** who **[problem]**, we offer **[product]** that delivers
**[specific result]** — unlike **[alternatives]**, we **[differentiator]**. One sentence; must survive a customer's honest "so what?".

**Branding & presence.** Brand = name, look, tone, promise. Presence = every place a prospect can judge you:
profile photo, bio, pinned post, WhatsApp Business reply, Google Business profile, flyers still matter locally.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Projected Grace Stores profile (weak: all-caps bio, no CTA, 40 posts of "Happy New Monday"): rewrite live —
persona first (a working mother in Mile 3 who batch-cooks on Saturdays), one-line VP, three content pillars.
Class votes on before/after.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: pick a real local business they know; write persona + problem + VP in the one-sentence formula;
share; room heckles every generic word ("quality", "best", "professional").

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: **Persona card** — your final-project business (real or fictional): photo/name, age, pain in their words,
trigger event, budget band, where they hang out online. File to workbook.
