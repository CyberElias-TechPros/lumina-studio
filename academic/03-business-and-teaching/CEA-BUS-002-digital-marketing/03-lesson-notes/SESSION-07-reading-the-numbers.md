## 10–25 · Teaching block

**The metric ladder.** Reach (how many saw) · impressions (how many times, count duplicates) · engagement
(likes/comments/shares/saves — quality: saves & shares > likes) · CTR (clicks ÷ impressions) · leads
(messages/forms/calls) · cost per result (spend ÷ results — the truth serum) · ROAS (revenue ÷ spend).

**Vanity vs money.** Likes are free publicity not revenue; follower counts don't pay rent; leads do.
Report in that order: spend → results → cost/result → revenue attributed.

**Reading a report like an owner.** Which ad set died and why · which audience beat expectations · what
CTR says about hook vs offer mismatch (<1% CTR with high reach = weak hook; high CTR low conversion = weak offer/price)
· frequency >3 = creative fatigue · engagement rate falling = audience drift.

**Attribution honesty.** WhatsApp "how did you hear?" field; last-click vs view-through; week-over-week not day-panic.
Small n again — one bad day never a conclusion.

## Answers — KC7

1. Reach = unique viewers; impressions = total views including repeats. 2) Spend ÷ number of results. 3) Weak hook or wrong audience — Creative problem, not budget problem. 4) Same people seeing it again and again — creative fatigue; refresh creative. 5) "How did you hear about us?" at point of sale/chat — simple, human, better than none.
