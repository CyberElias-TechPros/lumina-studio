## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Room rates five projected captions 1–10 for "would tap / would scroll". Debrief: weak hooks die in the first line.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Content jobs.** Educational (how-to, myth-bust) — builds trust · Promotional (offer, launch, price) —
converts — max 1 in 4 posts · Entertaining (relatable, humour) — reaches · Social proof ( testimonials,
behind-scenes) — closes. Ratio guide: educate 50 · proof 20 · entertain 20 · promote 10.

**Storytelling skeleton:** Hook (3 seconds) → tension (the customer's problem in their words) → turn (what we do)
→ proof (result/testimonial/number) → CTA ("DM MENU", "Book on WhatsApp", "Link in bio").

**Captions that convert:** short first line with a reason to tap "more" · benefit-first · one CTA max
· local voice (Pidgin okay when authentic) · line breaks for rhythm · no hashtag soup (5–8 relevant tags
on IG, searchable keywords on TikTok captions).

**CTAs.** Tell them exactly what to do next: "Tap link", "DM PRICE", "Call 0803…", "Saturday delivery? Reply YES". Vague captions produce vague results.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor rewrites a projected flat caption live through three drafts — hook sharpened, story inserted,
CTA swapped vague→specific; room votes at each draft; instructor annotates the winning bones.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: take the persona card business; write 3 captions (one education, one offer, one testimonial-style) with CTAs; swap; red-pen the other's hooks.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: build your **content pillar board** — 3–5 pillars for your final business, 2 example posts each. File to workbook.

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC3: (1) Ideal post-type ratio. (2) Hook — what must it do? (3) One CTA rule. (4) Pidgin in captions — when? (5) Testimonial post — three elements.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Story before sell. Next: calendars, brand voice and your one-week A1 plan.

## Answers — KC3

1. Educate 50 / proof 20 / entertain 20 / promote 10. 2) Stop the scroll in ≤3 seconds; create a reason to read line 2. 3) One CTA per post; multiple CTAs split attention = none chosen. 4) When authentic to the brand and the audience; forced Pidgin reads fake. 5) Pain named, result with number, permissioned quote.
