## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Annoyed-ad/sold-ad pairs debrief: what specifically triggered each reaction. Board lists "3 ad sins that burn money" (generic image, feature-speak, no CTA) and "3 that convert" (named audience, specific offer, urgency honesty).

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Ad creative anatomy.** Visual stops scroll (real product shot beats stock, before/after, human face) · headline
= outcome ("Chinedu's phone back in 90 minutes") · body = one proof point + price if honest · CTA = one action.

**Ad copy formulas.** PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solve: "Dead phone before salary week? Every hour offline costs you… We fix it same-day, ₦8k max, receipt guaranteed.") · Before/After/Bridge · 4U (urgent, unique, useful, ultra-specific). Local flavour welcome when authentic.

**Landing pages.** One page, one goal, one CTA. Above fold: headline = VP, sub = proof, CTA button. Mid: 3 benefits + testimonial/number. Bottom: FAQ, guarantee, contact. WhatsApp-first businesses can skip the page — the chat IS the landing page; then your canned flow must be tight.

**Follow-up money.** 70% of sales happen after the second touch. D+1 "still thinking it over?" · D+3 offer value ("send you our price list") · D+7 close-or-archive politely. Never shame; always door open.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor builds two ad texts live (one generic, one PAS) + a simple landing page skeleton in Canva/site builder screenshots; class picks winner; then simulates WhatsApp canned flow on two phones.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: draft one PAS ad + one landing skeleton (or WhatsApp canned flow) for their final business; peer test: show to neighbour for 5 seconds — "what do we sell, what do you do next?" Any hesitation = rewrite.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: finalise your **ad concept + conversion path** for the portfolio (creative + copy + where the click goes + what happens in the next 15 minutes). File to workbook.

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC6: (1) PAS expansion. (2) One CTA rule on landing pages. (3) WhatsApp-first businesses — what replaces the page? (4) Second-touch timing. (5) The 5-second stranger test.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Creative in, conversion out. Next: the numbers — what counts, what's vanity.

## Answers — KC6

1. Problem–Agitate–Solve. 2) One page, one goal, one CTA — everything else is friction. 3) The canned greeting + catalogue + human follow within 15 minutes. 4) Same day or next morning — D+1. 5) A stranger shown for 5 seconds must say what it is and what to click.
