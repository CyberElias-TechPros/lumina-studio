## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Platform-by-job map.** Facebook = broad SME discovery + groups + Marketplace (30–55 skewing) · Instagram = visual lifestyle, fashion, food (18–40) · TikTok = reach explosion + search for under-30s + now Google searchable · WhatsApp = the Nigerian conversion engine (catalogue, quick replies, broadcast lists — opt-in only) · Website = credibility + capture + long-term asset · Email = owned audience, 25–40x ROI when list is real · Search/Google = intent harvest (people already looking for you).

**Match, don't chase.** Ask: where does THIS persona actually scroll at 9pm? Start with 1–2 platforms done well; spreading thin kills voice consistency.

**Audience mechanics.** Interest targeting, lookalikes, retargeting (warm visitors), geo-radius (5km around shop), language (English + Pidgin ad variants often outperform), age bands tested not assumed.

## Answers — KC2

1. TikTok. 2) Broadcast only to people who opted in; otherwise spam and block risk. 3) You own the list; algorithms can't throttle it. 4) Choose by where the persona actually is, not by fashion. 5) An audience modelled on your best customers — platforms find similar users.
