## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Room reset; pitch order; client role = instructor with the challenge cards.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**The client-ready report template.** One page: objective & budget · what ran (channels, period) · top-line
numbers (spend, reach, leads, sales, cost/result) · what worked (with screenshot) · what didn't · next-month
changes (one variable each) · approval ask. Attachments: ad screenshots, calendar, content samples.

**Presenting.** 3 minutes: hook (headline result) → context (the objective) → story (what we ran) → receipts
(numbers) → lesson (next month) → the ask. No jargon; the client interrupts; every answer lands on cost, results or time.

**Final brief.** Complete plan for a real or fictional local business: persona · VP · two platforms + WhatsApp flow ·
one-week calendar · three ad concepts (hook, body, CTA, targeting, budget) · reporting template filled (illustrative labelled) · 3-minute pitch.

## 25–45 · Final project (individual work)

Solo (observed, 25 min): assemble the campaign folder — every artefact since S1, campaign plan cover page,
report filled, print copies. Instructor files.

## 45–80 · Pitches (observed)

Per student: 3 minutes + client Q&A ("what if CTR drops?", "why not TikTok?", "where does the budget go first?").
Room in client role with challenge cards; honest applause, honest verdicts.

## 80–100 · Show-and-tell & peer debrief

Pairs: swap plans; one strength, one risk each; instructor consolidates class-wide lessons on the board; feedback forms distributed.

## 100–110 · Correction & check

KC8: (1) Report order: spend → ? → ? → revenue. (2) One-page sections. (3) Client may interrupt — your rule. (4) Illustrative numbers label — why mandatory? (5) Next-month change, one variable — why?

## 110–120 · Course close

Certificate gate: objective→metric connected end-to-end. Alumni pathways: social media management (CEA-BUS-004) to run it daily, data analytics to drink from the numbers, business freelancing to sell it as a service.

## Answers — KC8

1. spend → results → cost/result → revenue. 2) Objective/budget · what ran · numbers · what worked · what didn't · next changes · the ask. 3) Answer in one sentence landing on cost, results or time. 4) Honesty with clients; illustrative ≠ actual — misleading is disqualifying. 5) Multi-variable changes teach nothing; the lesson must be attributable.

## Homework

None. Send one campaign plan pitch to a real prospect within 72 hours if brave.
