## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Three volunteers read their hooks; room scores in 3-second pairs (thumbs). Best one dissected.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Content calendar.** Grid of days × pillars; plan a week Sunday-ahead; batch-create; schedule with
native tools (Meta Suite, TikTok scheduler) or upkeep manually with reminders; leave 20% fluid slots for trends/bread.

**Brand voice.** Three adjectives (e.g. warm, plain-speaking, confident); three it-never-says (jargon,
put-downs, hype words); sample sentence in-voice vs out-of-voice. Voice is consistent across captions,
WhatsApp replies, flyers, even invoice footers.

**Visual consistency.** 2–3 brand colours, 1–2 fonts, same logo mark position, consistent photo style
(natural light, real product over stock). Canva brand kit free tier is enough; save templates — never start blank.

**Project brief for A1:** one-week calendar (Mon–Sun) with: date · pillar · post idea · format (reel/carousel/static/story) · caption hook · CTA · platform.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Fri testimonial, Sun closed-notice, Wed Community Saturday", Mon restock alert) — templates made in Canva, scheduled in Meta Business Suite, printed artefact shown.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

## 70–95 · Independent practice (A1)
