## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Organic = earned attention.** Pillars, consistency, community replies, collabs, UGC (user photos with permission),
SEO-ish captions on TikTok/YouTube, Google Business posts. Free but slow; compounding.

**Paid = rented attention with receipts.** Objectives first: Awareness (reach/impressions) · Consideration
(traffic/messages/leads) · Conversion (sales/store visits). Never "boost" without choosing the objective — boosting
reach doesn't buy you customers.

**Targeting ladder:** geo (city/radius) → interest/behaviour → lookalike → retargeting (site/profile engagers).
Budget discipline: start ₦2–5k/day for 5 days, kill losers, scale winners; one variable at a time; lifetime vs daily budget (daily for control).

**WhatsApp conversion path.** Ad → click-to-WhatsApp → canned greeting + catalog link → human follow within 15 min →
"still interested?" 24h later. Measure replies, not just clicks.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

## 95–110 · Correction & check

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Objective → audience → creative → budget — in that order. Next: creative and landing/conversion.

## Answers — KC5

1. Organic = free, slow, compounding; paid = targeted, measurable, immediate-ish. 2) Awareness, consideration, conversion. 3) Small daily test (₦2–5k/d × 5d), kill losers, one variable. 4) No objective, no measurement, no learning — money burned for ego metrics. 5) Ad click → canned greeting → human ≤15min → 24h follow-up.
