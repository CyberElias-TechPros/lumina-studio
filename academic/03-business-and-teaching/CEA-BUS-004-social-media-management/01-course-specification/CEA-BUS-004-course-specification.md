## 1. Identity

- **Title:** Social Media Management · **Department:** Business & Teaching · **Level:** Beginner
- **Duration:** 3 weeks · 6 sessions · 2/week · 105 min · **630 guided minutes**
- **Fee:** ₦30,000 · **Cohort:** max 15
- **Delivery:** On-site; machines with browser; phone for content production practice; sandbox accounts provided

## 2. Description

A full week-in-the-life of a social-media manager, compressed and assessed: optimised profile,
content pillars, a 7-day calendar, real graphics and captions, a week of scheduled posts, comment/DM
handling with templates and escalation, and a short performance report — presented like a client deliverable.

## 3. Purpose

SMEs hire "my niece knows Instagram" daily and fire them weekly. This course produces the trained
operator who plans, batches, posts, converses, de-escalates and reports — with templates and judgment.

## 4. Audience

Graduates seeking the in-demand "social media manager" role · SME owners and staff running their own pages ·
creators going commercial.

## 5. Entry requirements

Comfortable on Instagram/Facebook · basic phone photography useful, taught · no design experience needed (Canva-class tools).

## 6. Learning outcomes

1. Optimise a business profile: bio that converts, image rules, contact wiring, link-in-bio.
2. Build content pillars and a 7-day calendar; batch and schedule posts instead of reactive posting.
3. Produce consistent graphics, photos and short videos; write captions with hooks, CTAs, hashtags.
4. Handle comments, DMs and difficult customers with templates and escalation judgment.
5. Report reach, engagement, growth and clicks client-ready; flag what to fix next.

## 10. Assessment

| Component                                                                     | Weight |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Knowledge checks (6 × 5)                                                      | 10%    |
| Workbook practicals                                                           | 25%    |
| Assignment A1 — optimised profile + 7-day calendar                            | 15%    |
| Final project — mock account managed for one week (simulated across sessions) | 40%    |
| Show-and-tell — weekly report walk-through                                    | 10%    |

**Critical gate:** community handling — a difficult-customer role-play must show acknowledgement + hold + escalate correctly (never delete, never snap); failure = reassess once.

## 11. Critical behaviors

Never argue in public comments; never delete a complaint — respond and move to DM/call; template-beaten improvisation loses; client report weekly without excuses.

## 12. Learning barriers

Posting as personal account (all opinions); inconsistency (3 days on, forgotten 2 weeks); report-hoarding instead of decision-making.

## 13–16. Delivery · QA · Resources · Completion

Sandbox IG/FB pages provided; Canva brand kits; printed comment-response templates. Completion: attendance ≥ 4/6; gate passed; 7-day artefact set filed; feedback form before certificate.
