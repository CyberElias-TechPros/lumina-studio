**Do not distribute.** Keys in Section 7.

## 1. What this course is

Week-in-a-box operations training: profiles, batch production, community, reporting, crisis. The class
works a sandbox client all 6 sessions; assess the behaviours, not the aesthetics.

## 2. Preparation

Sandbox IG/FB pages (one per client); Canva brand kits; seeded devilish comments (3 batches); printed
playbook templates; Meta Business Suite walkthrough screenshots; stopwatch.

## 3. Delivery model

105 min: 0–10 hook · 10–25 teach · 25–45 demo · 45–70 guided/role-play · 70–95 solo/live sandbox · 95–110 KC · 110–120 close.

## 4. Failure points

- S1: bio written like an essay; force the 150-character constraint with a visible counter.
- S4: students delete complaints — enforce "screenshot first, respond, never delete" with veto power in the sandbox.
- S5: engagement-rate arithmetic slips; compute one aloud on the board.
- S6 gate: any "snap back at the customer" = gate failure; acknowledge–hold–escalate every time.

## 5. Assessment guidance

A1: profile build checks (name-field keyword, bio with CTA, pinned post brief, contact wired).
A1 calendar: 7 days, pillar spread, hooks, CTAs, plausible times. Final: mock-week one-pager with ≥2 hard comments
handled correctly; gate behaviour witnessed live or in logs.

## 6. Equity & access

Phones sufficient; no paid tools; fictional client businesses welcome; print assets for low-vision.

## 7. Knowledge-check keys

KC1: 1) Search-indexed keyword field. 2) Who/what/proof/CTA. 3) Mark reads at 32px. 4) Audience pains/competitor gaps/strengths. 5) Best current offer.
KC2: 1) Posts window/stories presence/Reels reach/carousels saves. 2) Hook ≤1–3s. 3) Local+niche+community, rotating. 4) One CTA. 5) Sunday batch ritual, 20% fluid.
KC3: 1) Colours/font/logo placement/templates. 2) Window light. 3) ≤1s spoken+text hook; captions always. 4) DATE-pillar-hook-vN. 5) Most views are muted — captions mandatory.
KC4: 1) SLA because slow reads as guilt/indifference. 2) Acknowledge → apologise-experience → investigate → solve/explain → follow-up. 3) Any two with name + real info. 4) Refund>₦20k / legal / press / repeat offenders. 5) One screenshot undoes 100 posts.
KC5: 1) Interactions ÷ reach × 100. 2) 1–3% is healthy for most local service businesses (watch trends). 3) Saves & shares > comments > likes. 4) No third-party apps with client creds. 5) Period; what ran; numbers (reach/engage/clicks/intent); best/worst + why; next 3 actions.
KC6: 1) 2FA · separate email · printed backup codes. 2) Acknowledge publicly, move to DM, owner looped within the hour if serious. 3) One calm factual reply, then disengage. 4) Minimum privilege — editors can't lock you out or delete assets. 5) Reach · engagement rate · intent DMs/orders + best-post lesson.

## 8. Expected outputs & calibration

S2: A1 due with ≥80% submitted. S4: role-plays witnessed per student. S6: gate witnessed or logged;
two assessors on difficult cases; feedback forms filed before certs.

## 9. Rubric (0–4)

0 cannot · 1 heavy prompts · 2 completes with errors · 3 unaided solid · 4 unaided + catches others' errors. Gate: difficult-customer scenario (acknowledge, investigate, escalate).
