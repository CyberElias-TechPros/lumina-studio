## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Three sanitised comment screenshots on board ("you people are daylight robbers"; "where are you? My order is late"; "fake product!!") — room drafts the first reply line for each, out loud.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Comments are public customer service.** Golden rules: respond within 4 business hours · acknowledge before explain ("Sorry for the stress") · no arguments, ever · receipts where you can ("order #241 was dispatched Tuesday") · move to DM/call for details · public resolution = public trust rebuilt.

**DMs that convert.** Quick replies (templates): price list, delivery info, opening hours, "we're open 6am–8pm", order link, complaint intake ("so sorry — order number?"). Personalise first line ("Hi Adaeze, thanks for asking!") — templates aid, never replace.

**Difficult humans.** Anger ladder: acknowledge → apologise (for experience, not automatic fault) → investigate with order number → solve or explain with care → follow up in 24h. If abuse/threats/illegal claims: do not delete, do not feed — escalate to owner with screenshots; platform reporting if needed.

**Escalation matrix (one page).** You handle: hours, prices, location, reorder help, praise. Owner handles: refunds > ₦20k, legal threats, press, abusive repeat offenders. Reply within SLA either way — silence is the only unforgivable.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor role-plays three live scenarios from the opener with a volunteer as the customer — first attempt is intentionally over-apologetic/defensive, room reconstructs with the ladder; second attempt passes. Then builds one quick-reply template.

## 45–70 · Guided role-play lab

Pairs run four cards (angry delivery, "scammer!", wrong item, silent-where-are-you DM) — two minutes each, swap; adjective rating by partner ("calm 1–5"); debrief quotes that landed.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: write your **response playbook** — 5 quick replies + escalation matrix + one public-resolution example for your client's most likely complaint. File to workbook.

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC4: (1) Four-hour SLA why. (2) Anger ladder, four steps. (3) Two quick-reply examples with personalisation. (4) Escalation thresholds. (5) Public argument cost.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Reputation lives in the replies you didn't script. Next: the numbers — reading reach, engagement and growth like a client.
