## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Format jobs.** Posts (image+caption) = permanent shop window · Stories = daily presence, polls/Q&A, 24h urgency ·
Reels/Shorts = reach machine (9–22s, captioned, hook in 1s, trend audio where tasteful) · Carousels = saves (education in 5 slides)
· Live = events. Most local businesses: 4 posts + 6 stories + 1 reel per week is honest capacity.

**Captions.** Hook line (stop-scroll) · value (short lines, one idea per line) · CTA single action ("DM PRICE", "visit Saturday", "link in bio")
· hashtags 5–8 (IG) local+niche; TikTok = searchable keywords instead; no walls of emoji.

**Hashtag sets.** 3 tiers: local (#PortHarcourtBusiness), niche (#HomeBakingNigeria), community (#Mile3Market).
Save three rotating sets — same tags every post looks copy-paste.

**Scheduling.** Meta Business Suite free scheduler; TikTok native; Notion/Sheets calendar; Sunday plan-Sunday post
ritual; batch creation mornings; leave 20% for ad-hoc breadth. Posted at 8am & 7pm local tests best for Port Harcourt feeds (test, don't worship).

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

("Plantain puff-puff: Saturday combo yes/no/ask-my-wife"), schedules all three into Friday; screen projected.

## 45–70 · Guided practice
