## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Project two business profiles (weak vs strong). Room decides "would you message this?" in 3 seconds. Why?

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**The conversion bio, line by line.** Handle = searchable, real brand name. Name field = keyword ("Grace Stores | Bakery, Mile 3" — Instagram indexes name field for search). Bio = who it's for + what + proof + CTA:
"Fresh bread & pastries daily 6am–8pm, Mile 3 · 🚚 Mile 3 delivery · 👇 Order: link" — 150 characters; escape
cleverness for clarity. Contact buttons (WhatsApp, call) wired and tested. Link = link-in-bio page (Beacons-class) or direct WhatsApp — one tap to action.

**Images.** Logo mark legible at 32px (favicon test) · profile photo consistent across platforms · highlight covers on-brand ·
pinned post = best converter (offer/testimonial).

**Content pillars (3–5).** Derived from audience pains: e.g. Grace Stores — Fresh baking (educational: reheating tips) ·
Community (events, supply stories) · Offers (₦ prices, bundles) · Smiles (behind-scenes, staff, regulars).

**Audience research light.** Ask 20 followers (or profile theirs): age, when they scroll, what they buy, what they complain about;
competitor scan — 3 competitors' best posts; your difference stated in one line.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Live Instagram profile rebuild on projector sandbox: handle/name/bio/contact/highlight/pinned post rewritten; class narrates improvements; before/after screenshotted for the student pack.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: optimise each other's sandbox profile — name field keyword, rewritten bio with CTA, two highlight concepts, pinned post brief; swap screenshots.

## 70–95 · Independent practice (A1 part 1)

Solo: complete **A1 profile build** — your mock client business: all five bio lines written, name-field keyword chosen, contact wiring steps, pinned post idea. File to workbook.

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC1: (1) Name field does what? (2) Bio formula, four elements. (3) Favicon legibility rule. (4) Three pillar sources. (5) One pinned-post job.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

A profile is a landing page wearing a profile picture. Next: formats, captions, hashtags and the 7-day calendar (A1 finish).

## Answers — KC1

1. Searchable keywords — Instagram indexes it; profiles with "bakery Mile 3" surface on that search. 2) Who it's for + what you do + proof + CTA. 3) Mark must read at 32px. 4) Audience pains, competitor gaps, your strengths — any three. 5) Carry your best current offer or proof.
