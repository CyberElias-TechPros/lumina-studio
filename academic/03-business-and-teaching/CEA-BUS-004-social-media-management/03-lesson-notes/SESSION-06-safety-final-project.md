## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

One-line check-ins: "What's your mock week plan?" — room answers in 15 seconds each.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Account security non-negotiables.** 2FA everywhere · separate email for business · no shared passwords over chat · backup codes printed off-site · phishing: Meta never DMs "verify your account" — delete, report, move on · role permissions (admin vs editor) — give staff editor not admin · monthly access audit.

**Crisis protocol (one card in your wallet).** Negative post/spreading complaint: acknowledge fast, don't delete, investigate, respond publicly + move to DM, owner looped within the hour if legal/press/health.
Silence or deletion turns a molehill photogenic.

**Difficult comments playbook.** Troll (bait) → 1 calm factual reply, then stop · legit complaint → ladder from S4 · misinformation about you → correct it with receipts, politely, link FAQ · threats → screenshot, block, report, escalate.

## 25–45 · Project briefing (final)

Mock-week management (over the following week or compressed across S6): run your client's sandbox page — post per calendar, reply to ≥10 seeded comments/DMs (instructor plants some devilish ones), handle one difficulty well (gate), then deliver the S5 one-pager report.

## 45–90 · Live sandbox management

Pairs/instructor seed comments hourly; students manage in turns; flag every tricky decision on the board for debrief; instructor scores gate behaviour live.

## 75–95 · Final report & show-and-tell

Each student: 4-minute walk-through of the week (metrics 4 numbers, one hard comment handled, one lesson, one next move). Q&A via challenge cards.

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC6: (1) Three security non-negotiables. (2) Crisis first move. (3) Troll rule. (4) Editor vs admin — why it matters. (5) Client one-pager — three numbers for the boss.

## 110–120 · Course close

Certificate gate: difficult-customer scenario handled with acknowledgement + escalation. Portfolio: profile build, calendar, asset pack, playbook, one-pager. Next: real clients under supervision — then Business Freelancing (CEA-BUS-001) to package it.
