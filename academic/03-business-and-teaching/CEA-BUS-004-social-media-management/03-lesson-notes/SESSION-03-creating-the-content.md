## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Show three homework photos; room rates "buy-able" quality; name the fix (light, angle, clutter).

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Graphics with Canva.** Brand kit (2 colours, 1 font) → post/story/reel covers from templates —
NEVER blank canvas · logo bottom-corner placement consistent · text ≥ 40pt on mobile · one message per graphic
· export PNG (feed) / MP4 (reel cover) at platform sizes. Licence: free elements marked; don't steal watermarked stock.

**Phone photography for business.** Window light (all day) · one subject, clean background · rule of thirds-ish · shoot horizontal AND vertical (reuse) ·
hold phone steady, tap to focus, exposure finger-slide · edit lightly (crop, brightness, contrast — never heavy filters on food).

**Short video.** Vertical 9:16 · 9–22 seconds · hook spoken + on-screen text in first second · one idea · captions always (70% watched muted) ·
trending audio if it serves the brand, else silence + captions · end card with handle + CTA.

**Batching & asset hygiene.** One morning, 12 posts, two drinks of water; folder structure per client (`Client/2026/Nov/posts/`);
file names `2026-11-16-pillar-hook-v1.png`; everything scheduled not "posted live whenever".

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor produces live: one Canva feed graphic (template + brand colours + logo + price text), one reel cover,
oncell phone food-shot edit (crop/brightness) — class follows along on machines.

## 45–70 · Production morning (guided)

Pairs batch-produce their client's week: 2 graphics + 1 edited photo + 1 caption-cover; swap for a two-minute critique; fix the weakest element.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: **week asset pack** — 6 exportable files per calendar (graphics/photos/covers) organised in a folder with README.txt listing each file → scheduled slot.

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC3: (1) Brand kit contents. (2) Photo light rule. (3) Reel hook length + captions rule. (4) File-naming convention. (5) 70% muted statistic implication.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Batch on Sunday, post with intention. Next: the inbox-side — comments, DMs, difficult humans.

## Answers — KC3

1. 2–3 colours, one font, logo placement rule, template set. 2) Window light, mobile shootable food; never flash-food. 3) ≤1s hook spoken+text; captions mandatory. 4) DATE-pillar-hook-vN. 5) Captions always — most views happen silent.
