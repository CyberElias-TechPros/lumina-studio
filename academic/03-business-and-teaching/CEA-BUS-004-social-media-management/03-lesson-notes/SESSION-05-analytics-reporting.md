## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Room names the vanity metrics (followers, likes) vs money metrics (DMs with price intent, orders linked) — board splits them.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**The client one-pager (weekly).** Period · posts published · reach · impressions · engagement rate (interactions ÷ reach) ·
profile visits → link clicks · DMs with intent · top post + why · bottom post + why · next-week actions (3, named).

**Where the numbers live.** Instagram Insights (Professional dashboard), Facebook Page Insights, Meta Business Suite, TikTok Analytics —
screenshot, don't copy by hand, and don't trust third-party apps with client credentials.

**Interpreting.** Engagement rate: <1% meh, 1–3% healthy, 3–6% strong, >6% shout about it. Reach falling with same output = content or timing drift.
Link clicks < 10/week with big reach = bio/CTA problem. Saves & shares outrank likes; comments outrank both.

**Reporting honestly.** Clients smell success-theatre; bring the dip + the diagnosis ("reach fell 30% Tuesday — we'll test 7pm slot this week"). One decision per slide, not data soup.
