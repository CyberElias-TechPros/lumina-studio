## What you will walk out with

A client-ready social-media starter pack: optimised bio, 7-day calendar, a week of batched graphics and
captions, a comment/DM playbook, and a one-page performance report — the exact deliverables SME clients
pay for month one.

## The six sessions

S1 Profiles & strategy (bio that converts, pillars, audience) · S2 Formats & captions (Reels vs posts vs
stories, CTAs, hashtags, 7-day calendar — A1) · S3 Creating content (Canva graphics, phone photo/video,
batching) · S4 Community (comments, DMs, templates, escalation) · S5 Analytics (reach, engagement, growth,
clicks — the client one-pager) · S6 Safety & final mock week (account security, difficult comments, final report).

## Assessment weighting

KCs 10% · workbook 25% · A1 profile + calendar 15% · mock week 40% · show-and-tell 10%.

## Classroom rules

Sandbox accounts only — never real client credentials in class. Content about this cohort stays in this cohort.
Respect: no mocking local brands by name in critique.
