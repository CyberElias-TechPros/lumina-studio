## Brief

In one observed sitting (25 min):

1. **One-page business plan** — WHO (named archetypes) · three OFFERS with Basic/Standard/Premium prices ·
   acquisition plan naming your first five warm prospects · money logic (breakeven, 50% deposit rule,
   ledger habit) · the one promise you will never break.
2. **Portfolio** — three case studies: problem → approach → result. Honest labels ("practice piece" if not yet paid work). No bare screenshots.
3. **Pitch** — 3 minutes aloud to the class; then defend your price against challenge cards ("why not half?", "who precisely buys this?").

## Conditions

Notes from this course allowed; no internet required; real skill, real numbers; fiction allowed only for practice pieces clearly labelled.
