## What you will walk out with

A one-page freelance business plan — services, packages, prices, target client, acquisition plan — and a
portfolio of three case studies you can send tonight. Plus the scripts for proposals, scope control and
complaint handling.

## The six sessions

**S1 — From Skill to Service.** Services are outcomes clients buy: " logo for a tailor in 7 days, ₦25,000,
two revisions" not "I do graphics". You'll draft three service offers from your skill.

**S2 — Finding Clients & Proposals.** Ethical prospecting (warm network first, then referrals, then cold
with permission), understanding requirements by asking better questions, proposal structure:
understanding → approach → deliverables → timeline → price → terms → next step.

**S3 — Delivering Professional Work.** Expectations, deadlines, revisions (two included, then priced),
communication cadence, complaint handling: acknowledge → investigate → fix or explain → follow up.

**S4 — Pricing & Packaging.** Cost-plus floor, value ceiling, competitor sanity check, packages
(Basic/Standard/Premium), deposits (50% before start, non-refundable once work begins), payment terms,
scope control scripts, when to say no.

**S5 — Records, Money & Reputation.** Invoice ledger, expense tracking, tax-sane basics, repeat-customer
system, referral requests, testimonials with permission, professional reputation as the real compounding asset.

**S6 — Final Project.** One-page plan + three case studies; class pitch; peer pricing challenge ("why is
this ₦30k and not ₦300k?").

## Assessment weighting

KCs 10% · workbook 25% · A1 packages 15% · final plan+portfolio 40% · show-and-tell 10%.

## Classroom rules

No shaming pricing — every number argued in the open. Real examples only: your examples, your real skill.
