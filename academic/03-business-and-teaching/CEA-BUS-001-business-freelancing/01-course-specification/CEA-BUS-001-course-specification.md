## 1. Identity

- **Title:** Business & Freelancing · **Department:** Business & Teaching · **Level:** Beginner
- **Duration:** 3 weeks · 6 sessions · 2/week · 105 min · **630 guided minutes**
- **Fee:** ₦30,000 · **Cohort:** max 15
- **Delivery:** On-site classroom; no internet required; laptop recommended, not mandatory

## 2. Description

Turn one real skill into a sellable service: three service packages with defensible prices, a
portfolio of three work samples as case studies, an ethical client-acquisition plan, a proposal that
gets replies, and the records and manners that keep clients coming back.

## 3. Purpose

Thousands of Nigerians are skilled and broke because nobody taught them the _business_ of the skill.
This course installs the missing half: packaging, pricing, finding, negotiating, delivering, getting
paid, keeping records, surviving complaints.

## 4. Audience

Graduates and NYSC members seeking income · skilled trades people (tailors, caterers, photographers, developers) formalising their hustle · SME founders · side-hustlers wanting structure.

## 5. Entry requirements

One marketable skill (any CEA craft course counts) · basic literacy; no business background required.

## 6. Learning outcomes

1. Convert a skill into three concrete services with named deliverables and timelines.
2. Price a service defensibly (cost-plus, value, competitor, package psychology) and quote packages.
3. Build a three-case-study portfolio that shows capability, not screenshots.
4. Find clients ethically; write proposals with the right questions; close without begging.
5. Manage scope, deadlines, revisions and complaints; keep deposits, records and basic finances clean.

## 9. Practical exercises

Three-services exercise (S1) · proposal rewrite lab (S2) · complaint role-play (S3) ·
package-design studio (S4) · ledger day (S5) · final pitch (S6).

## 10. Assessment

| Component                                                          | Weight |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------ |
| Knowledge checks (6 × 5)                                           | 10%    |
| Workbook practicals                                                | 25%    |
| Assignment A1 — three service packages with prices                 | 15%    |
| Final project — one-page business plan + 3 case studies (observed) | 40%    |
| Show-and-tell — defend your price to the class                     | 10%    |

**Critical gate:** plan passes the "can this be sold tomorrow?" test — a real, specific offer aimed at a real audience with a number attached; failure = reassess once.

## 11. Critical behaviors

Deliver exactly what was agreed; raise scope creep in writing before doing it; chase invoices politely and early; never insult a client — ever; records before feelings.

## 12. Learning barriers

Under-pricing from fear of "no"; vague deliverables ("website for you" with no scope); portfolio screenshots instead of case studies.

## 13–16. Delivery · QA · Resources · Completion

Simulated client inbox during S2–S3. ₦150k practice ledgers. Completion: attendance ≥ 4/6; gate passed; plan + portfolio in LMS; feedback form before certificate.
