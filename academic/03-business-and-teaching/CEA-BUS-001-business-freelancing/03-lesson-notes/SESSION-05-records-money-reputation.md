## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Volunteers read their pricing cards aloud (voluntary). Class asks two pricing challenge questions each —
rehearse S6 pitch.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Records that save you.** One ledger (paper or Sheets): date · client · service · amount · paid Y/N ·
balance · invoice #. Every naira in and out, same day. Invoice numbers sequential. Bank statements
reconciled monthly. 60 days of records = the difference between a business and a hobby in any dispute.

**Basic finances, sane.** Income target ÷ weeks = weekly floor; separate account if possible ("business" purse);
set aside ~10% for tax/tithe/savings depending on conviction; expenses photographed/banked; know your
breakeven month (rent + transport + food + tools ÷ margin).

**Repeat customers & referrals.** Delivery date + 3 days: "How's it working out?" message; 2 weeks: "Any feedback?";
60 days: "Need anything refreshed?" — referral ask woven in. Testimonials with permission: quote + name + first name/org.

**Reputation is the moat.** On-time rate, responsiveness, fixing mistakes unprompted, never ghosting —
your file is your marketing; one bad unresolved complaint undoes ten good jobs in WhatsApp economies.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor shows a projected messy "business book" (crumpled receipts, ₦450k missing, one unpaid since March)
→ rebuilds it properly in a Sheet template live: 12 entries reconciled, one "paid?" discovered, one invoice
re-sent (template shown). Class follows on paper ledgers.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: audit the **sample ledger** (practice file): find the unpaid invoice, the double-counted expense, the
missing deposit — list fixes. Then each drafts a testimonial-request message (their own).

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: build your **personal ledger template** (Sheets or paper) with your income target, breakeven math,
this month's entries (even if zero). File to workbook.

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC5: (1) Six fields every ledger line. (2) Breakeven formula. (3) When to ask for a referral. (4) Testimonial permission — why? (5) One reputational landmine.
Self-mark.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Records → money calm → referrals. Next session is the final: your one-page plan and three case studies.

## Answers — KC5

1. Date · client · service · amount · paid · balance (+invoice #). 2) Fixed monthly costs ÷ margin per job. 3) After successful delivery + follow-up — never during the crisis. 4) Written consent before quoting a client publicly; WhatsApp economies screenshot fast. 5) Ghosting a complaint / missing deadline silently / money into personal purse with no ledger.
