## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Read the screenshot exercise aloud; room rates openers (1–10). Debrief: discovery openers beat "please patronise me".

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Setting expectations up front.** Kick-off message inside 24h: scope restated, timeline with dates,
what you need from them (content, access, feedback windows), revision policy (2 rounds, 7 days to review
each), payment milestones, how you communicate (WhatsApp for day-to-day, email for approvals).

**Deadlines that hold.** Work backwards from delivery; 48h buffer; never promise the impossible;
proactive heads-up at any slip ("Tuesday risk of Wednesday — here's why — new delivery Thursday" beats silence).

**Revisions & scope control.** "Two rounds included" is in the proposal; round 3+ priced before starting.
New request mid-project: "Happy to add that — it's outside scope, so it's ₦X and 2 days; confirm and I'll include it." Written, always.

**Complaints handled well.** Acknowledge fast ("You're right to flag that") · investigate against agreement ·
fix or explain why not · refund/adjust when you're wrong · follow up after. A complaint is a
reputation-building opportunity, not an insult.

**Communication cadence.** Daily micro-update or milestone-only — chosen at kick-off, written where
visible. Silence is the #1 client anxiety.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor role-plays the full lifecycle in fast-forward with a volunteer as client: kick-off message →
mid-project scope creep ("can you also add…") → delivery → the complaint ("the logo is too dark") →
recovery. Room watches for the written-when-it-matters moments; list them on board.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs run complaint role-plays from cards (late delivery / wrong colour / missing file / rude tone) —
acknowledge-investigate-fix-followup 4 steps aloud; swap. Debrief: phrases that de-escalate vs inflame.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: write your **kick-off message template** and your **scope-creep response**, each with two real
blanks you'd fill per client. File to workbook.

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC3: (1) Contents of a 24-hour kick-off. (2) Client can't decide between round 2 options — your script. (3) Scope creep response format. (4) Complaint four steps. (5) Why put communication cadence in writing?
Self-mark.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Agree on paper, deliver on time, hold scope. Next: the money session — pricing, packages, deposits.

## Answers — KC3

1. Scope restated · dates · expectations (their content by when) · revision policy · payment milestones ·
   channel for day-to-day. 2) "To keep us moving, I'll proceed with option A unless I hear otherwise by tomorrow."
2. Happily-add + out-of-scope + price + timeline + confirm-in-writing. 4) Acknowledge · investigate · fix or explain · follow up. 5) Silence breeds anxiety and mistrust; scheduled updates kill both.
