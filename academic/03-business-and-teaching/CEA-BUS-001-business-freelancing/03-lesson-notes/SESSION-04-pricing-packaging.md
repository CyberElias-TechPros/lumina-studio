## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Pricing models.** Cost-plus (materials + hours × rate + margin — your floor, never negotiate below) ·
value-based (what the outcome is worth to the client — your ceiling; a rebrand boosting salon revenue
50% is worth more than logo hours) · market/competitor (sanity check range, not copy).

**Package psychology.** Basic/Standard/Premium — most buyers land on the middle; anchor Premium high to
make Standard feel safe; Basic as proof-of-concept ladder (entry → upsell). Three, not seven.

**Deposits & terms.** 50% deposit before start (non-refundable once work begins — expenses are real),
balance on delivery before files release; for large projects, milestones: 30/40/30. Never start on
"pay later" without a deposit — that's not kindness, that's a donation.

**Scope control.** Every package lists deliverables, revisions, exclusions, timeline. Extras quoted
separately before doing them. Change-order text ready to paste: "Added request… price ₦X, 2 extra days. OK?"

**When to say no.** No deposit → no start. Complaining client before work → decline. Impossible timeline →
counter or decline. Declining well: "I'd love to help; this doesn't fit my schedule/skills — could try XYZ next month."

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor prices the tailored flyer design live on board three ways (cost-plus ₦18k; market ₦25–40k;
value-based for event promoter ₦45k), then packages it: Basic ₦25k (1 flyer, 2 revisions) · Standard ₦40k
(flyer + 2 social sizes + print-ready file, 7 days) · Premium ₦75k (all + brand colour board + express 48h).
Class interrogates: who buys which and why.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: build YOUR three packages for your S1 service — Basic/Standard/Premium with deliverables,
revisions, timeline, deposit split. Swap; partner must find one scope hole in each (challenge it).

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: write your **pricing card** — three packages, deposit line, expiry, exclusions; printable. File to workbook. Be ready to defend it live in S6.

Self-mark; revise your prices once more after this KC.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Money is scope with numbers. Next: records, money discipline and reputation.

## Answers — KC4

1. Floor = cost-plus break-even; ceiling = value of outcome to client. 2) Choice overload; three is the sweet spot; middle is the intended landing. 3) 50%/50%; 30/40/30 for big projects — covers real costs; commitment filter. 4) Added scope + price + extra days + confirmation ask. 5) No-start-without-deposit (any of: no to free work, no to impossible deadline, no to abusive clients).
