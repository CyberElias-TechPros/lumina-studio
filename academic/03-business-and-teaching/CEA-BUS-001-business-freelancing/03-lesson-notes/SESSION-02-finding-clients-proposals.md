## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Homework share: two competitors' offers read aloud; room votes which they'd hire and why.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Ethical prospecting ladder.** (1) Warm circle: friends, family, church, school — announce your offer
once, not daily. (2) Referral requests: deliver well → ask for one name ("who else do you know
struggling with X?"). (3) Cold with permission: WhatsApp groups you own, community boards, Marketplace
listings — where invited. (4) Never scrape personal contacts, never spam.

**Discovery before proposal.** Ask 8 questions before quoting: problem, context, current state,
deadline, budget band, decision process, success metric, hidden constraints. Proposal without discovery
is a lottery ticket.

**Proposal anatomy (one page):** Understanding of the problem · your approach (3 steps) · exact
deliverables · timeline · price · payment terms (deposit) · validity (14 days) · next step ("Reply YES
by Friday and we book Monday 10am").

**Tone.** Professional, specific, short. No "Dear esteemed sir/madam" to a peer; no essay to a CEO.
Mirror the client's register.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor drafts a proposal live from the projected messy client brief ("we lost 3 full-time staff,
need the shop Facebook page revived"): discovery questions first (2 on screen), then the one-page
proposal, then send (simulated).

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: role-play — A is the client with a brief card (event poster / salon rebrand / inventory sheet);
B runs 8 discovery questions aloud, then writes the one-page proposal. Swap. Workbook logs Q-count and proposal time.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: write proposal for your three-services offer applied to a REAL brief from the card deck (hotel
signage, choir logo, church programme printing) — one page, priced, 14-day validity, clear next step.

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC2: (1) First rung of the ethical ladder. (2) Three discovery questions. (3) One-page proposal sections. (4) Why discovery first? (5) WhatsApp pitch — three lines.
Self-mark.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Find, discover, propose, stop talking. Next: delivery — scope, revisions, complaints.

## Answers — KC2

1. Warm circle (announce once, helpfully). 2) Any three: budget band, deadline, decision-maker, success measure, current state. 3) Understanding · approach · deliverables · timeline · price · terms · validity · next step. 4) Proposals without discovery quote wrong scope/price; discovery reveals the real problem. 5) Who you are + what you'd fix for whom + concrete next step ("I'll send a 1-page plan tomorrow").

## Homework (20 min)

Message one real potential client (warm circle) with a discovery-first opener. Screenshot for privacy-safe class review.
