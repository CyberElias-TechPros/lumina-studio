## 1. Identity

- **Title:** Online Teaching · **Department:** Business & Teaching · **Level:** Beginner
- **Duration:** 3 weeks · 6 sessions · 2/week · 105 min · **630 guided minutes**
- **Fee:** ₦30,000 · **Cohort:** max 15
- **Delivery:** On-site; machines with browser; microphone/headset; practice teaching rooms

## 2. Description

Design and deliver a real online class: learning objectives, a lesson plan with materials and
assessment, slides, an assignment/quiz, and a recorded short lesson delivered to peers with structured
feedback — everything Nigerian private tutors and course creators need to charge properly.

## 3. Purpose

Demand for competent online teaching explodes every term. Most tutors teach from improvisation; this
course produces the trained ones — planned, recorded, assessed, and able to give feedback that lands.

## 4. Audience

NYSC members and teachers going online · private tutors formalising · subject experts becoming course
creators · HR trainers preparing internal sessions.

## 5. Entry requirements

Subject competence in something teachable (any subject OK) · basic computer confidence · no teaching experience needed.

## 6. Learning outcomes

1. Write measurable learning objectives (Bloom-ish verbs) and a complete lesson plan (objectives, materials, steps, assessment).
2. Decompose a complex topic into teachable steps and demonstrations.
3. Run an online class: meetings, screen sharing, whiteboard, recording, pacing.
4. Build slides, materials, assignments and quizzes that work without the teacher present.
5. Give feedback that improves performance; deliver a short online lesson with confidence.

## 9. Practical exercises

course map (S5) · final filmed lesson (S6).

## 10. Assessment

| Component                                                  | Weight |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Knowledge checks (6 × 5)                                   | 10%    |
| Workbook practicals                                        | 25%    |
| Assignment A1 — complete lesson plan + slides              | 15%    |
| Final project — recorded 8-minute online lesson (observed) | 40%    |
| Show-and-tell — peer feedback debrief                      | 10%    |

**Critical gate:** the final lesson must contain a stated objective, one check for understanding, and a
run of ≥6 minutes — a chat with a camera is not a lesson; reassess once.

## 11. Critical behaviors

Objective before content; practice beats lecture ≥50% of class minutes; feedback names observable behaviour; recordings honest about gaps.

## 12. Learning barriers

Death-by-slides; monotone pacing; "any questions?" silence mistaken for mastery.

## 13–16. Delivery · QA · Resources · Completion

Two cameras/phones for recording practice. Completion: attendance ≥ 4/6; gate passed; plan + slides + recording filed to portfolio; feedback form before certificate.
