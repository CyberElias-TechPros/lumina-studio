## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Materials pack spot-check: pairs confirm each has title+objective+materials — the submission of a teacher who cares.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Whole-course architecture.** Outcome (one sentence) → modules (3–6 units, each a capability) → lessons
(each module 2–6 lessons) → each lesson: objective + materials + practice + check. Sequence by dependency and
energy (hardest in week 2 not week 1), reviews every 4–6 lessons.

**Lesson structure skeleton (reusable):** Hook (90s) → objective revealed → demo (slow) → guided practice →
independent practice → 1-minute check → exit ticket/next preview. Timing written on the plan, rehearsed.

**Feedback that feeds forward.** SBI model: Situation ("In your demo…") → Behaviour ("…you walked through the paste options fast…") → Impact ("…class lost the thread by step 3") → Feedforward ("Next time narrate the key — Ctrl+V pulls, then slow on the dialog"). Record one of these per student.

**Differentiation light.** Fast finishers: extension task; strugglers: buddy + hint card; absent: recording + exit ticket on return. Plan for all three on every lesson.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor maps a full 4-session mini-course ("WhatsApp Business for your shop") live on the board: outcome,
3 modules, lessons per module, check points, recap placement; then they SBI a projected weak student example ("walked through your demo slides — talk about slide 4").

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: build the course map for their final-lesson TOPIC as if it were a 4-lesson module (outcome, 2 modules × 2 lessons, checks). Present 60s; room names the missing dependency thread.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: write your **course map one-pager** + SBI script lines for the two most likely student mistakes in your topic. File to workbook.
