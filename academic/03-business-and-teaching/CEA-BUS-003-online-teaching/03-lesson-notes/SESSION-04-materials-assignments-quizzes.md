## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Materials that travel without you.** Slides: one idea per slide, big type, image > text, the objective
on slide 2, summary slide last. Handouts: annotated worksheet for guided practice. Templates: started
files students edit (payment sheets, letter skeletons). Everything named conventionally, linked in one message.

**Assignments with teeth.** Named deliverable · clear criteria (pass marks per element) · time expectation
(30/60/120 min) · submission mechanics (where, format, by when) · feedback deadline promised by YOU.
A good assignment is a promise you intend to keep.

**Quizzes that teach.** Google Forms/quiz: 5–10 questions · mostly easy-to-medium with 1 stretch ·
automatic scoring where possible · immediate feedback with one explanatory line per question · mastery threshold 80% else retake hints.

**Student communication.** One message channel (WhatsApp group or LMS announcement), one response SLA
(e.g. questions answered within 24h), FAQ sheet grown from repeated questions, office hours once weekly.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor builds live: one Google Form quiz (5 questions with feedback lines), one assignment brief
(for the Excel-Sum lesson), posts both into a mock WhatsApp group pinned message; projects the linked sheet autolog.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: draft an assignment brief + 5-question quiz for their final lesson; swap; review against the assignment
criteria (named deliverable, criteria, time, where/when, feedback promise); fix.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: complete your **materials pack** — slides outline (title + objective + 8–12 slide bones), assignment
brief, quiz link/file. File to workbook.
