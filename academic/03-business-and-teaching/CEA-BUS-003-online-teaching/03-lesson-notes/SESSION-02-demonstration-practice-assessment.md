## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Demonstration done right.** Slow, narrated, step-visible: name each step AS you do it ("finger rules
the ruler — eyes on source — cell — verify"). Then fast replay. Never demo in silence.

**Guided → independent practice.** I do → we do → you do, in that ratio. Guided: everyone attempts with
hand-holding. Independent: solo with eyes on their own screen. Errors are data — collect them live
("who got a #VALUE!? What was under it?").

**Feedback that improves.** Specific + behavioural + kind + actionable: "Your opening hook named the
problem in 8 seconds — good. Drop the third slide (it repeats slide 1); next time open with the number
₦2.3m." Generic praise ("well done") teaches nothing.

**Assessment as design.** Check for understanding every chunk — one minute: thumbs, 2-question quiz,
exit ticket. Assessment measures the objective, nothing else; if the objective says "types 20 WPM",
assessment must time 20 WPM.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor demos "folding a shirt in 20 seconds" as a model mini-lesson: hook (shirt chaos example) →
slow demo with narration → guided (class mirrors) → independent (timed) → check (30-second exit ticket).
Room debriefs what each phase did to attention.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

score with the feedback formula (specific/behavioural/actionable). Swap.
