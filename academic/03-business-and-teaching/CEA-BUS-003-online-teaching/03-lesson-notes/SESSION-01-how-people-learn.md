## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Ask the room: "Best lesson you ever sat through — why?" Capture answers; pattern: clear goal +
someone responsive + you did things. That's the whole science today.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**The learner, not the lecturer.** Learning = durable change by the learner; teaching arranges
conditions. Attention spans fence at ~15–20 minutes; chunk accordingly.

**Learning objectives that work.** Bloom verbs: after this lesson you will be able to **write / solve /
identify / build** — never "understand" (unobservable). Condition + behaviour + criterion:
"Given a WPS invoice template, type it without looking, ≤ 3 errors, ≤ 10 minutes."

**Lesson planning.** Four questions: Where are they now? Where to? What will they do? How will we know?
The 5E shadow: engage → explain → explore → elaborate → evaluate. For this course: Hook → Demo → Guided
practice → Independent practice → Check.

**Chunking complexity.** Decompose "teach Excel formulas" into: what formulas are, then SUM, then ranges,
then absolute $, then errors — one cognitive stack per chunk, mastered before stacking the next.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor reverse-engineers one of their own classes live: pick topic "finding square roots by hand";
writes the objective; lists the chunk map; spots where students usually drown; rewrites the first chunk.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: take "Baking moi-moi" / "Fixing a phone charging port" / "Excel VLOOKUP" (cards) — write ONE
objective + chunk map ≤5 chunks; swap; challenge each other's verbs ("know? — no. name it observable").

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: **objectives drill sheet** — convert 10 vague aims ("teach my daughter maths") into measurable
objectives; pick YOUR final-lesson topic; write its objective. File to workbook.

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC1: (1) Why "understand Excel" is a broken objective. (2) Structure of a complete objective. (3) Attention
span and chunking. (4) Two questions every lesson plan must answer. (5) The five-step lesson skeleton.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

## Answers — KC1
