## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

A1 sanity check: objectives read aloud; room verifies each check matches its objective.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**The room tech.** Meet/Zoom/Teams: join-5-min-early, camera on (yours), mic discipline, mute protocols,
chat for questions (not hijacking their thread), screen share (window, not whole screen — bedtime notifications off),
whiteboard (draw-think-live), recording (consent! captions! saved where students actually get them).

**Pacing online.** Energy must travel further — bigger voice, bigger gesture, slower transitions.
Eye contact = camera lens not the faces. Check-ins every 10 minutes ("type YES if following" —
never "any questions?" silence).

**Engagement architecture.** Pair rooms (breakouts), polls, chat-answer blitzes, cold-call warmly
("Chiamaka, your typing tip on line 3 — say the rest"), predict-then-reveal before demos.

**Recording etiquette.** Record with consent; keep it short (≤15 min chunks); captions auto-on;
file-naming `2026-11-16-topic-chunk-v1.mp4`; store with the lesson folder, not your phone gallery.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor runs a real (throwaway) Meet room projected: shares window, writes on whiteboard, runs a poll,
breaks class into 2-min breakouts, records 30 seconds, sends the link. Class follows from their machines
logged into their own sandbox Meet.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: run 3-minute micro-room — one teaches anything (drawn from a card: "how to count Naira notes fast");
other joins as the student — screen share + one poll + one question answered on camera. Swap.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: rehearse YOUR A1 lesson in the sandbox room — record yourself for 3 minutes of the opening; watch
back; note one pace and one eye-contact fix. File the video locally (portfolio at S6).

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC3: (1) Camera-eye contact rule. (2) Ten-minute check-in format. (3) Screen-share hygiene. (4) Recording consent + captions. (5) Two breakout-room rules.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Presence online is a craft you can rehearse. Next: materials, assignments, quizzes.

## Answers — KC3

1. Look at the lens, not the faces — reads as eye contact to every student. 2) "Type YES if following / LOST if not" or a 1-question poll. 3) Share the window not the desktop; silence notifications; close personal chat/email. 4) Announce and get consent; captions on; save with lesson files. 5) Small pairs (2–3), one task, timer visible, debrief back in one minute.
