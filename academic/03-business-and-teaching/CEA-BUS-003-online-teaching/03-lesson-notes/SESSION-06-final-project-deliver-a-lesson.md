## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Cameras set; room divided into two "live room" pods; quiet brief: breathe, teach to the eyes, slow down at the demo.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**The brief.** 8-minute recorded lesson on your A1 topic: opens with hook + objective · one slow narrated demo ·
≥2 minutes guided practice with questions · one check for understanding · closing preview/homework. Screen share + board as needed; student role = one volunteer + room holds spot-feedback cards.

**Recording hygiene.** Lens at eye level; light on your face; mic close; phone silent; water nearby; notification banners OFF everywhere; record twice if the first dies — the second take is what they get.

**Feedback loop.** Peer feedback with SBI cards (2 specific + 1 feedforward each); then your own: rewatch 60 seconds of yourself; write the one thing to fix first next time.

## 25–45 · Lesson delivery (observed)

Per student: 8 minutes teaching + 2 minutes Q&A. Assessor scores P1–P6 sheet. Two takes permitted; take 1 failure tolerable if take 2 completes.

## 45–80 · Continued deliveries / rest pod

Alternate pods teach/observe; observers complete SBI cards per teacher; swap at halfway.

## 80–100 · Show-and-tell & feedback debrief

Pairs debrief recordings: strongest 10 seconds + one fix; room consolidates class lessons-learned on the board; improvement plan one-pager per student (3 actions with dates).

## 100–110 · Correction & check

KC6: (1) The 8-minute skeleton timing. (2) Take-two rule. (3) Feedback SBI formula. (4) Improvement plan, three actions example. (5) What a recording beats a live memory for.

## 110–120 · Course close

Certificate gate: objective + one check + ≥6 minutes delivered. Portfolio: plan, slides, assignment+quiz, recording, improvement plan. Keep teaching weekly — teaching decays without practice; join a church/community class.

## Answers — KC6

1. Hook/objective ~1 min · demo ~2 min · guided 2+ min · check ~1 min · close ~1 min. 2) Second take is allowed; first stumbles are normal; ship the better take. 3) Situation, Behaviour, Impact (+ feedforward). 4) e.g. "Narrate mouse clicks aloud by Friday", "Trim slides 9–11 this weekend", "Ask for feedback from one real student within 2 weeks". 5) Objective self-review — you see your own pacing and fillers without defending them.
