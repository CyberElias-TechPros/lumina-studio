## A1 (15%)

Per assessments sheet. Alignment of checks to objective is the discriminating dimension — unaligned checks cap at 10.

## Final (40 + 10)

P1–P6: 12+ → 40 · 9–11 → 32 · 6–8 → 24 · ≤5 reassess. Show-and-tell (10): 10 fix + cause self-diagnosed · 7 fix · 5 squirm · ≤3 none. Gate: absolute.

## Workbook practicals (25%)

All boxes → 25; ≤1 missed → 22; 2–3 missed → 17; more → ≤12. Boxes 2.2, 3.2, 6.1–6.2 must be present.

## Knowledge checks (10%)

Full 2.5 · partial 1.5 · attempt 0.5 · blank 0 each.

## Calibration

Two assessors observe the gate question per recording; borderline "chat with camera" cases discuss against the P1/P4 definitions verbatim, not vibes.
