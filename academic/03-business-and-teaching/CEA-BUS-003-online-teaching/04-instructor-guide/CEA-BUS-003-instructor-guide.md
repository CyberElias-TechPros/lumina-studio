# CEA Instructor Guide — CEA-BUS-003 Online Teaching

**Do not distribute.** Keys in Section 7.

## 1. What this course is

Teaching craft in one 105-minute sitting per skill. The room is a rehearsal studio, not a lecture hall;
every session ends with the student having taught something. Protect psychological safety — first camera-on moments sweat, and that's normal.

## 2. Preparation

Two sandbox Meet rooms; phones/cameras for recording; consent forms; SBI cards; sample lesson videos
(good and bad); printed chunk maps; timers visible.

## 3. Delivery model

105 min: 0–10 warm scenario · 10–25 teach · 25–45 demo · 45–70 guided role-pairs · 70–95 solo/record · 95–110 KC · 110–120 close.

## 4. Failure points

- S1: "Understand/learn/know" objectives slip back — shred any non-observable verb on sight.
- S3: monotone delivery at the demo — model energy; replay their own recording back to them kindly.
- S4: quizzes with 10% trick questions — no tricks in teaching quizzes; stretch question max 1.
- S6 gate: camera-less, objective-less chats fail; allow one re-take; gate = objective + ≥1 check + ≥6 min.

## 5. Assessment guidance

A1: plan completeness and objective-check alignment (15%). Final: P1–P6 lesson rubric; take-two permitted.
Show-and-tell: peer-feedback self-correction reasoning.

## 6. Equity & access

Camera opt-out allowed only for health/cultural reasons — audio+share still teaches; large-type printed plans for low-vision; no public mocking of any recording, ever.

## 7. Knowledge-check keys

KC1: 1) Unobservable, untestable. 2) Condition + Bloom verb + criterion. 3) ~15–20 min attention fencing; chunk. 4) Where they are / where they'll go. 5) Hook → Demo → Guided → Independent → Check.
KC2: 1) I 15–20% / we 30% / you 50%+. 2) Specific + behavioural. 3) Objective-exact 1–2 question end-of-chunk check. 4) "Name three rules — 30s, no notes." 5) Silence ≠ comprehension.
KC3: 1) Lens, not faces. 2) "YES/LOST" type or one-question poll. 3) Window-only share; notifications off; personal apps closed. 4) Consent announced; captions on; stored with lesson files. 5) 2–3 per room; one task; timer; 1-min debrief.
KC4: 1) One idea/slide; image > text; objective slide 2. 2) Named deliverable, criteria, time expectation, mechanics, feedback deadline. 3) 80%. 4) Pin FAQ; fold repeats into it. 5) Their honest, keepable SLA.
KC5: 1) Outcome capability sentence; module capability unit; lesson a teachable sitting. 2) Every 4–6 lessons + module-end. 3) Situation–Behaviour–Impact. 4) e.g. extension tasks, hint cards, buddies, recordings + returns. 5) Hook, objective, demo, guided, independent, check, exit ticket.
KC6: 1) ~1/2/2/1/1/1 min proportions. 2) Second take expected; ship better one. 3) SBI. 4) Any 3 dated actions. 5) Objective self-review without defense.

## 8. Expected outputs & calibration

S2: A1 alignment spot-checks aloud. S4: quiz files exchanged between strangers, marked blind. S6: P-gate witnessed by two assessors; feedback forms before certificates.

## 9. Rubric (0–4)

0 cannot · 1 heavy prompts · 2 completes with gaps · 3 unaided, fluent · 4 unaided fluent + teaches it forward. Gate: filled lesson rubric objective + checked understanding.
