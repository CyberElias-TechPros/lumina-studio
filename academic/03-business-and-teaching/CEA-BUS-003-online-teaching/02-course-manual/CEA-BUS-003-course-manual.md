## What you will walk out with

An evidence pack for charging for what you know: a complete lesson plan, slides, an assignment or quiz,
an 8-minute recorded online lesson delivered to peers, and a feedback-based improvement plan.

## The six sessions

S1 How people learn (objectives, lesson planning, chunking) · S2 Demonstration, practice, feedback,
assessment (your A1 lesson plan) · S3 Running an online classroom (Meet/Zoom, screen share, whiteboard,
recording) · S4 Materials and quizzes · S5 Course and lesson structure (modules, sequencing, feedback scripts) ·
S6 Final — deliver and record an 8-minute lesson; peer feedback.

## Assessment weighting

KCs 10% · workbook 25% · A1 lesson plan 15% · final lesson 40% · show-and-tell 10%.

## Classroom rules

Bring your own teachable topic (we will build your plan in-class). Recordings stay in cohort storage; consent forms signed at the door.
