## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Whitespace screenshots compared — the room votes the best teacher and names the technique (usually: one big image + one sentence).

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Section by section (the greatest hits).**

- Navigation: logo + 4–5 links + CTA ("Book now", "WhatsApp") — sticky on scroll is fine.
- Hero: who you are + what you do + ONE action — never two buttons fighting.
- About: why trust you — years, place, proof; one B&W portrait is classier than a wedding wall.
- Services: 3–6 cards with icon, name, one line, "from ₦…".
- Gallery: real work, real captions — never stock strangers drinking champagne.
- Contact: address, tel: links, WhatsApp deep link, Google Maps embed, 3-field form, business hours.
- Footer: logo small, nav repeat, socials, RC/CAC if a company, "© 2026".
  **Cards.** Consistent: radius, padding, image ratio (4:3 or 1:1), button style; hover lift `translateY(-2px)`.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Live: build Mrs. Obi's hero + services (3 cards) + contact (WhatsApp deep link `https://wa.me/234…` prefilled "Hello! I'd like a quote") + footer with CAC line; timer 30 minutes; addresses copy-paste reality of Lagos clients.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: build their gallery (6 real/CC0 images + captions) + contact section with tel:, wa.me deep link, map embed, 3-field form.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: **full section set** — your homepage assembled: nav, hero, about, services, gallery, contact, footer — all sections present, all links real or placeholder-flagged. Screenshot top-to-bottom to workbook.

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC5: (1) Hero law. (2) WhatsApp deep link format. (3) Gallery authenticity rule. (4) Contact trinity (tel / wa.me / map). (5) Footer trust signals.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Pages are done; now make them survive small screens without tears. S6: responsive.

## Answers — KC5

1. One line of who + one CTA — never two. 2) wa.me/234XXXXXXXXXX?text=Hello. 3) Real work or nothing — stock breaks trust. 4) Call · message · find-us. 5) CAC/RC, socials, copyright, repeat nav.
