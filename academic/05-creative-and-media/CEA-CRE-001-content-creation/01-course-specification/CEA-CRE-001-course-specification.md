## 1. Identity

- **Title:** Content Creation · **Department:** Creative & Media · **Level:** Beginner
- **Duration:** 3 weeks · 6 sessions · 2/week · 105 min · **630 guided minutes**
- **Fee:** ₦30,000 · **Cohort:** max 15
- **Delivery:** On-site; phones required; ring light, tripod, mics; editing laptops with CapCut-class free editors

## 2. Description

One finished short-form video — the full pipeline: idea, hook-driven script, phone shoot, clean edit with
captions, thumbnail and title, deliberate publish, first-week analytics review — plus copyright/privacy discipline.

## 3. Purpose

Attention is the scarcest resource on Nigerian feeds. This course trains creators who earn it honestly and keep it strategically.

## 4. Audience

Aspiring creators · SME marketers doing their own pages · students · podcasters/videographers scaling down to Shorts/Reels/TikTok.

## 5. Entry requirements

A smartphone; willingness to be on camera off-duty; basic computer use.

## 6. Learning outcomes

1. Generate ideas and write hook-first scripts; choose short vs long form deliberately.
2. Shoot stable, lit, well-framed phone video; record clean audio.
3. Edit: trim, transitions, text, music (licensed), captions; pace to rhythm.
4. Package (thumbnail, title, description) and publish deliberately with a calendar.
5. Read first-week analytics; respect copyright and privacy; iterate.

## 9. Practical exercises

Script lab (S1) · lighting ratio walk (S2) · caption ladder (S3) · thumbnail bake-off (S4) · analytics teardown (S5) · screening + critique (S6).

## 10. Assessment

| Component                                                                     | Weight |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Knowledge checks (6 × 5)                                                      | 10%    |
| Workbook practicals                                                           | 25%    |
| Assignment A1 — packaged publish-ready video (unpublished until final checks) | 15%    |
| Final project — complete video + first-week plan, produced live (observed)    | 40%    |
| Show-and-tell — screen your video, defend your hook                           | 10%    |

**Critical gate:** copyright-clean — every music/visual element either owned, licensed or royalty-free with source named; unlicensed audio in the final = reassess once.

## 11. Critical behaviors

Hook in 1 second · audio before visuals · captions always · no one on camera without consent · credits and sources named.

## 12. Learning barriers

On-camera freeze (use voiceover fallback); editing rabbit holes; comparison-shamescrolling.

## 13–16. Delivery · QA · Resources · Completion

Loaner ring lights/mics; licensed music pack; projector screening. Attendance ≥ 4/6; gate passed; video filed; feedback form before certificate.
