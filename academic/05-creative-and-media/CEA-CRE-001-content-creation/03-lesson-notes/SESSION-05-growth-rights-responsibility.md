## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Live URLs shared; first 24h numbers read out; class notes the wild variance without panic (one bad post ≠ dead account).

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Engagement loops.** Reply to first 50 comments in hour one · pin one comment question · respond to DMs · collaboration swaps (stitch/duet where relevant) ·
analytics: retention curve > impressions; find WHERE viewers drop and reshoot better hooks next time.
**Copyright.** Music (platform libraries = safe; anything else = takedown/monetisation loss/account strike) · images (Unsplash/Pexels or your own) · other people's footage (permission or transformative + fair-dealing awareness — when in doubt, don't) · logos/trademarks in product shots — mostly fine for review content, never for implying endorsement.
**Privacy.** Consent on camera (spoken "it's okay to use this" helps) · location safety (never film home address) · minors (parental consent, face-blur option) ·
medical/legal advice disclaimers where relevant.
**Responsible creation.** No fake before/afters · sponsored must be labelled (#ad) · honest about errors (your correction video builds more trust than the silence) · no punch-down humour.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Projected analytics page of the practice video from S4 (instructor's): retention curve dissected ("drop at 0:07 — the hook took too long; shorten next time") · music claim warning screenshot · comment replies demonstrated.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs audit each other's A1 package against the rights checklist (music licence source named? image rights? consent obtained? captions true?) — sign off each other's workbook audit page.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: your **week-one review note** — for YOUR live video: numbers so far, drop point found, one fix for next video, rights statement signed. File to workbook.

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC5: (1) Retention > impressions why. (2) Safe music sources. (3) Minor on camera rule. (4) #ad placement. (5) Correction video virtue.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

The publish button starts the lesson, not the celebration. Next: final screening.

## Answers — KC5

1. Retention tells WHY watchers stay; impressions only how many arrived. 2) Platform libraries / YouTube Audio Library / Pixabay — everything else needs a licence. 3) Parental consent; blur if unsure. 4) Clear, near the offer, and in caption. 5) Public corrections compound credibility faster than perfection does.
