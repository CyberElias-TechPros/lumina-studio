## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Board: "One video you watched twice this month — why?" Collect: relatability, curiosity, usefulness, beauty.
All four are scriptable skills, not luck.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Content types.** Educational (how-to/myth) · entertainment (skit/relatable) · inspirational (journey/turn) ·
promotional (offer/product) · documentary (process/day-in-life). Pick per post, not per mood.

**Pillars.** 3–5 repeatable themes from YOUR lane (a baker: bakes, kitchen fails, sourcing, ₦-pricing). Pillars = the algorithm's memory of you + your own sustainable treadmill.

**Audience first.** One sentence: "For [who] who [pain], I make [what] so they [outcome]." Example: "For busy salary earners who want hot food at 9pm, I post 90-second one-pot recipes so they stop eating noodles every night."

**Hooks (the first 1 second).** Question hook · number hook ("3 mistakes every phone photographer makes") · bold claim ("Never buy shade cloth for your cream") · pattern interrupt (silence → shout) · result-first ("Here is the receipt"). Write five, pick one, never open with "hey guys, welcome back".

**Scripts.** 30–60 seconds = 75–150 spoken words. Structure: hook → 1 problem → 3 steps/parts → CTA ("save this", "comment X"). Read aloud; cut anything a 10-year-old can't follow.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Live: instructor picks "fixing generator pull-start" topic; writes audience sentence; brainstorms 6 hooks aloud; class votes for the sharpest; script in six lines; word count shown.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: build 5 content ideas for one pillar each + full scripts for the best two; swap; partner must retell the hook in ≤5 words for each to survive.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: **five ideas + two full scripts** for YOUR channel — filed to workbook (feeds A1/S6).

## 95–110 · Correction & check
