## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Room re-dressed for screening; phones silenced; popcorn spirit optional; rights statement signatures ready.

## 10–25 · Teaching block (briefing)

**Final deliverables.** (1) The video (≥45s, captions, licensed music, exported 1080×1920 or platform spec).
(2) The screening defence (2 minutes): topic choice, hook reasoning, light/audio story, one thing you'd change.
(3) The rights declaration (signed) — every asset owned/licensed/cleared.
(4) The week-one plan: post time, pinned comment, reply cadence, metric you'll watch.

**Critique protocol.** Work first, face never. "I felt… at 0:12 when…" comments only; two stars + one wish per video; applause level equal for all — growth is the curve, not the room.

## 25–80 · Screenings (observed)

Three rounds: screen → 2-minute defence → room two-stars-one-wish each → assessor scores P1–P6. Rights declarations collected.

feedback forms; alumni pathways — video-editing (CEA-CRE-004) for craft, social-media management (CEA-BUS-004) for day-to-day.
