## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Watch one student take (volunteer); room deletes the boring middle together by controller-pointing.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**The edit decisions, in order.** (1) CHOOSE the right take(s) — kill darlings now. (2) TRIM to the bone — first frame speaks to the last; dead air, throat-clears, "umm" gone. (3) PACE to the hook — short first cut, get to value before 3 seconds. (4) LAYER — b-roll over your talking head where it carries (hands, product, result). (5) TRANSITIONS — mostly none; cuts and jump-cuts are your friends; one wipe per friendship only.
**Text & captions.** Caption EVERYTHING (muted majority); 3–6 words per line; one line up, one down; highlight key word in accent colour; auto-caption then FIX the mangling.
**Music.** Licensed class pack / platform audio library ONLY; duck to −18dB under voice; royalty-free sources (YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay). Unlicensed = fire risk, and the gate in S6.
**Voice cleanup.** Noise reduction (light), normalise loudness to −14 LUFS-ish, cut hmms aggressively.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Live edit in CapCut/Premiere-class editor projected: import take → rough cut → tighten hook → jump-cuts → auto captions + manual fix (name "Obinna"!) → music at −18dB → export 1080×1920.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs dock each other's footage: rough cut of their S2 takes; partner enforces 3-second value check and caption readability check (phone 50% brightness, arm's length).

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: **full edit of your A1 video** — ≥60s, captions, music licensed, −14 LUFS; export to phone; watch twice for typos (deleted letters). File to workbook (project file + export).

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC3: (1) Five edit steps in order. (2) 3-second value check. (3) Music level under voice. (4) Auto-caption golden rule. (5) One transition joke — and still no abuse.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Cutting is the craft; captions are the courtesy. Next: packaging — the thumbnail and title duel.

## Answers — KC3

1. Choose → trim → pace → layer → transitions. 2) Value must arrive by 3 seconds or the viewer is gone. 3) ~−18 dB under voice. 4) Auto-then-fix: every line proofed, names especially. 5) One wipe per friendship — cuts and jump-cuts do 95% of the job.
