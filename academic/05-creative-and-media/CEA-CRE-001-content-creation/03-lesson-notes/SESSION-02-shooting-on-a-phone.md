## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Voice memos played; the two bravest get room verdicts on hook-strength in 3 seconds.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Stability first.** Two hands at chest height; elbows in; walk like a waiter (knees soft); or tripod/stack of books — shake = deleted in the edit (ruined footage teaches fast).
**Framing.** Eyes on upper third (interview) · talking-head center with headroom · rule of thirds for product · clean background hunt before record (bin the laundry) · vertical for Reels/TikTok/Shorts; landscape for YouTube long-form.
**Light.** Facing window > side window > overhead (never) · golden hour outdoors · ring light 45° above eye · no mixed temps (window + yellow bulb = mud) · ISO lies: slight grain OK, blur is fatal.
**Audio.** Phone mic 20cm from mouth, chest-mount or plug-in mic if you own one · quiet room (generator off!) · record 5s room tone for the editor · unusable audio = unusable video — re-shoot, don't "fix in post".
**Talking to camera.** Look at the LENS not the screen · say the hook 3 times until it flows · breathe out before line · smile with eyes · 30-second takes, many of them.
**Screen recording** for tutorials (built-in recorders fine) — enable Do Not Disturb, portrait or landscape consistency.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Live shoot with a volunteer: bad take (shake, backlight, whisper) → diagnose why → better take (window, still, clear voice) → room scores both; audio waveform on laptop projected.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs shoot each other: 3 takes of the S1 hook (15s) — stability, framing, audio each pass; partner flags first unusable thing noticed; best take chosen on screen.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: shoot **your A1 raw footage** (60–90s of the chosen script, ≥3 takes) + 5s room tone. File to SD-less phone gallery, renamed `2026-11-16-topic-take1.mp4`.

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC2: (1) Stability triangle. (2) Light placement hierarchy. (3) Audio rule #1. (4) Lens-vs-screen eye rule. (5) Mixed-temperature mud — fix.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Good audio + okay video beats great video + bad audio. Next: the edit — cutting boring out.

## Answers — KC2

1. Two hands/elbows in or tripod. 2) Face window > side > overhead avoid. 3) 20cm from mouth, quiet, room tone. 4) Lens = eye contact for the viewer. 5) One light source only — kill the bulb or close the blind.
