## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Thumbnails on the projector at 50% — class votes the legible one; dissect why.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Thumbnails.** 3 words max, one readable face or object, contrast against the platform UI, test at 50% scale on phone; never lie with the thumbnail (click-bait burns trust — retention tanks, ranking follows).

**Titles & descriptions.** Title = benefit/curiosity gap with the searchable keyword ("One-pot plantain egusi — ₦1,800 for 4" beats "Cooking great food at home"); description repeats keyword once + link + credits + hashtags.

**Captions (the post caption).** Hook again, value line, CTA ("save for Saturday"), 5 relevant hashtags, tag location if local.

**Calendars & deliberate posting.** Batch posts 1 week ahead; post at tested times (test Sat 7pm vs Mon 8pm); schedule inside platform; pinned comment with context ("recipe printed below").
**Platform specs.** 1080×1920, ≤90s for Shorts/Reels/TikTok; safe zones (avoid top 10%/bottom 20% for captions — UI blocks them).

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Instructor packages a finished practice video end-to-end projected: two thumbnail candidates compared → title A/B debated → caption + hashtags → pinned comment → schedule for Sat 7pm; show the upload settings screens (comments on, visibility, audience = not-for-kids unless it is).

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs package their edited video: two thumbnails, two titles, caption, tags, schedule pick; swap; partner names the honest-but-better title; upload settings screenshotted for workbook.

## 70–95 · Independent practice (A1 check-in)

Solo: **A1 publish-ready folder** — video export + project file + thumbnail(s) + title/desc/tags + source log (music!). Instructor checkpoint-signs A1 readiness.

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC4: (1) Thumbnail test size. (2) Title formula elements. (3) Safe zone bottom %. (4) Pinned comment job. (5) Not-for-kids default when?

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Packaging converts clicks to trust. Next: growth, rights — the adult table.

## Answers — KC4

1. Phone at 50%, arm's length. 2) Benefit + curiosity gap + keyword. 3) Bottom ~20% (platform UI covers). 4) Extra context/CTA held by the eye of the read-through. 5) Always unless the content is knowingly for kids — mislabeling carries platform penalties.
