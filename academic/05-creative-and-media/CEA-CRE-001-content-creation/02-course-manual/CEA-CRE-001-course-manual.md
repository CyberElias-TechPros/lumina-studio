## What you will walk out with

A finished, published short-form video of yours — scripted with a real hook, shot deliberately, edited with
captions, titled for search, posted with a plan, and reviewed against numbers in week one.

## The six sessions

S1 Ideas and scripts (pillars, hooks, short vs long) · S2 Shooting (phone, light, audio, framing) ·
S3 Editing (trim, transitions, music, captions) · S4 Packaging (thumbnails, titles, captions, calendar) ·
S5 Growth and rights (analytics, copyright, privacy) · S6 Final project — screen your video, defend every frame.

## Assessment weighting

KCs 10% · workbook 25% · A1 publish-ready package 15% · final video 40% · show-and-tell 10%.
Gate: all assets original, licensed or public-domain-cleared.

## Classroom rules

Consent before any filming of people. No copyrighted music — class pack only. Wins and misses screen together; critique the work, never the face.
