**Do not distribute.** Keys in Section 7.

## 1. What this course is

Confidence-through-production. Every student leaves having shipped one real video; the two feelings
that matter are "I did that" and "I see the mistake I'll fix next time". Protect screening dignity absolutely.

## 2. Preparation

Ring light, tripod, 2 lavalier mics + adapters for common phones, 2 laptops with CapCut-class editors,
projector, licensed class music pack loaded, consent cards, rights declarations printed.

## 3. Delivery model

105 min: 0–10 watch-a-good-clip together · 10–25 teach · 25–45 demo · 45–70 guided/shoot · 70–95 solo/edit · 95–110 KC · 110–120 close.

## 4. Failure points

- S1: hook-shame spirals — hooks are written before performed; five on paper first, always.
- S2: audio ignored because "camera looks fine" — play both takes back-to-back; the room refuses to watch the bad one twice.
- S3: transition addicts — one-wipe rule enforced with a kitchen timer.
- S6 gate: unlicensed music discovered AT screening = stop, mute, reassess path offered; never "just this once".

## 5. Assessment guidance

A1: folder completeness + project file opens. Final: Rights declaration verified line-by-line; P1–P6 witnessed;
week-one plan realistic (no "I'll post daily forever").

## 6. Equity & access

Phones with cracked screens provided; low-data export option; voiceover alternative for camera-frozen students; large-type cue cards for scripts.

## 7. Knowledge-check keys

KC1: 1) Edu/entertain/inspire/promote/doc. 2) Treadmill + algorithm memory. 3) Stop scroll ≤1s. 4) ~110 words. 5) "For X who Y, I make Z so they W."
KC2: 1) Two-hands elbows-in/tripod. 2) Window facing > side > overhead-avoid. 3) 20cm + quiet + room tone. 4) Lens = eye contact. 5) One source light only.
KC3: 1) Choose→trim→pace→layer→transitions. 2) Value by 3s. 3) ~−18dB. 4) Auto-then-fix. 5) One wipe per friendship; cuts do the work.
KC4: 1) Phone at 50%, arm's length. 2) Benefit + gap + keyword. 3) Bottom ~20%. 4) Context/CTA pinned by the creator's own hand. 5) Always unless knowingly kid-directed.
KC5: 1) Retention = why they stay. 2) Platform libraries/YAL/Pixabay. 3) Parental consent or blur. 4) Clear in text near offer. 5) Public correction compounds trust.
KC6: 1) Video, defence, rights, week-one plan. 2) "I felt X at time Y when Z." 3) Rights-clean. 4) Why a stranger stops at 0:00. 5) Feed weekly; craft courses.

## 8. Expected outputs & calibration

S2: A/V quality pairs show measurable lift across takes. S4: A1 folders ticked before S5. S6: two-assessor rights check; every screening applause-equity enforced.

## 9. Rubric (0–4)

0 unusable · 1 heavy prompts · 2 watchable, amateur mistakes · 3 clean and honest · 4 would be proud to pin it. Gate: rights-clean declaration.
