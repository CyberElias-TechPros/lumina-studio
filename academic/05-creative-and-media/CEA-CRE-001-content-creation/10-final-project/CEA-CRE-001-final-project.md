## Brief

Observed screening + defence:

1. **The video** — ≥45 seconds, 1080×1920 (or platform spec), captions, licensed audio, exported from your editor — screened to the class.
2. **The defence** — 2 minutes: why this topic (audience sentence), why this hook (the 1-second test), light and audio decisions, the drop point you'd fix next time.
3. **The rights declaration** — signed list: every music track, every image/clip, every human on camera — source or consent attached.
4. **Week-one plan** — post datetime, pinned comment, reply cadence, the one metric you'll report back.

## Conditions

Own topic choice; notes permitted; no re-editing after the screen starts; every human visible heard agreed (spoken or written consent).

## Marking

CEA-ACA-QA-CRE-001 (P1–P6; gate P4 declaration line-by-line, absolute). Show-and-tell: your hook under friendly fire.

## Submission

Video + project file + declaration + week-one plan to cohort file; portfolio link within 7 days; feedback form before certificate.
