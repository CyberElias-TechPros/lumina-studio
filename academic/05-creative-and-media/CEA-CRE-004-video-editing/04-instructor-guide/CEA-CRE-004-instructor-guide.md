**Do not distribute.** Keys in Section 7.

## 1. What this course is

Cinema-in-a-box discipline: structure, rhythm, mix, captions, ship. The class runs hot on deadlines;
machines matter less than eyes — headphones on, timelines versioned, WA uploads verified.

## 2. Preparation

CapCut/Premiere-class lab install; class music pack (licensed) on shared drive; three raw shooting packs
(cookout, tailor fitting, church choir — variety); loudness meter app; projector + screen; export checklist laminated.

## 3. Delivery model

105 min: 0–10 watch-one-clip · 10–25 teach · 25–45 demo · 45–70 guided cuts · 70–95 solo · 95–110 KC · 110–120 close.

## 4. Failure points

- S1: assembly never stops growing — enforce the v-naming ritual and 10-minute triage timer per dump.
- S2: J/L ratios over-cooked (everything dialogue-driven) — one action-driven scene mandatory in each piece.
- S3: −18dB music becomes −18dB everything — peaks get checked on palm-eyeball meter before export.
- S6 gate: portrait 1080×1920 tagged as 16:9 — the stretch-test rebound is brutal; watch-back catches it.

## 5. Assessment guidance

A1: listen before you look — LUFS read-out, clean noise floor, vocal-first mix. Final: two exports pass the
watch-back with captions legible and skin honest; gate = watched-back, on a different device than edited on.

## 6. Equity & access

Colour-blind-safe accent choices for captions; loudness/vibration alerts for hard-of-hearing seats; loaner USB mics; no paid plugins required.

## 7. Knowledge-check keys

KC1: 1) hook ≤2s → develop → payoff. 2) ≤1s. 3) keep/maybe/kill. 4) No trimming past assembly. 5) First frame that compels.
KC2: 1) Cut mid-motion. 2) J = audio leads · L = outlives. 3) Beat-aligned with variation. 4) Ripple closes gap · lift leaves gap. 5) ~zero dead air (≤150ms).
KC3: 1) −14 LUFS social (−16 YouTube). 2) Underwater robotics — back off denoise. 3) −18 to −22dB, ducked. 4) Seam replacement of silence. 5) Takedown/demonetisation risk.
KC4: 1) ~80% muted watchers. 2) 3–6 words, 2 lines max, 1–3s. 3) ≤3s, first entrance. 4) Bottom 20% UI zone. 5) Auto-then-proof, always.
KC5: 1) Hard cut. 2) Time/place shift. 3) Shape-echo across the cut. 4) 0.4s 90→100% + fade. 5) Thumbnail legibility survives forwards.
KC6: 1) WB and exposure first — paint repair later. 2) Orange band ±10. 3) 8–12Mbps. 4) −14 LUFS. 5) 25% (captions) and 75% (mix) both.

## 8. Expected outputs & calibration

S2: before/after v1→v2 diffs screened pairs. S4: captions retell test passed. S6: every export watch-back witnessed by assessor.

## 9. Rubric (0–4)

0 unusable · 1 heavy prompts · 2 functional, rough edges · 3 clean, shipped, correct specs · 4 portfolio-grade under pressure. Gate: platform-spec watch-back.
