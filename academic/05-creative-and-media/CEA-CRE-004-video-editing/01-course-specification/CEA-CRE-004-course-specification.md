## 1. Identity

- **Title:** Video Editing · **Department:** Creative & Media · **Level:** Beginner
- **Duration:** 3 weeks · 6 sessions · 2/week · 105 min · **630 guided minutes**
- **Fee:** ₦40,000 · **Cohort:** max 12 (machines heavy)
- **Delivery:** On-site lab; mid-range PCs with CapCut/Premiere-class editors; headphones per seat

## 2. Description

Two finished videos — a ≤60s vertical short and a 3–6-minute horizontal piece — mixed, captioned,
colour-corrected and exported to platform specs; the montage of an editor's head before the hand touches a timeline.

## 3. Purpose

Every business handing video to "the boy with the phone" deserves a product that holds attention. This course trains the editor behind it.

## 4. Audience

Beginners targeting short-form editing gigs · creators producing their own channels · SME media staff · church/school AV teams.

## 5. Entry requirements

Basic computer use; comfort with files/folders; no prior editing needed.

## 6. Learning outcomes

1. Structure stories that hold attention (hook, promise, payoff); select footage ruthlessly.
2. Cut on action, to rhythm, with J/L cuts; kill dead air without mercy.
3. Clean and mix audio to broadcast usable (noise reduction, levels, ducking, SFX, voiceover).
4. Caption, lower-third, brand-consistency; transition restraint; basic motion graphics.
5. Colour-correct then lightly grade; export to IG/TikTok/YouTube/WhatsApp specs.

## 9. Practical exercises

## 10. Assessment

| Component                                                   | Weight |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Knowledge checks (6 × 5)                                    | 10%    |
| Workbook practicals                                         | 25%    |
| Assignment A1 — mixed audio for one raw clip + wave map     | 15%    |
| Final project — two finished videos to platform specs       | 40%    |
| Show-and-tell — screen your piece, name the three best cuts | 10%    |

**Critical gate:** delivery specs — vertical 1080×1920 ≤60s AND horizontal 1920×1080 platform-passed (no stretch, audio −14 LUFS-ish, captions present); failure = reassess once.

## 11. Critical behaviors

Structure before cuts · audio first-class · captions never optional · exports watched back once at 50% volume before shipping · credits kept.

## 12. Learning barriers

Timeline rabbit holes; plugin hunting instead of cutting; "cinematic" grading abuse.

## 13–16. Delivery · QA · Resources · Completion

Licensed music pack; three sample raw shoots; loudness meter taught; printer for the export checklist. Attendance ≥ 4/6; gate passed; two videos filed; feedback form before certificate.
