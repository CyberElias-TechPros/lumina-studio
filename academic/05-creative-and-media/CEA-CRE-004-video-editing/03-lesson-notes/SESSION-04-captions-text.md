## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Captions = base floor.** 80% watches muted in-transit. Auto-generate then PROOF every line — names butchered ("Temilade" becomes "Thomas") are brand wounds.
**Caption craft.** 3–6 words per line; one line up, one line down max; 1–3 seconds per line; highlight key word in accent; font = your brand faced or platform default, 28–42pt equivalent; position above bottom 20% (UI-free zone).
**Lower thirds.** Name/role for first speaker entrance ONLY; 3 seconds; brand colour bar optional; never linger like a TV chyron.
**Titles/end cards.** One hook card (3 words) OR none; end card = CTA + handle ("Save for Sunday", "@gracestores"); 1.5s max.
**Accessibility law of the feed.** Deaf viewers, busy markets, mosque/church quiet zones — captions are reach, not decoration.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Live caption pass on your vertical v2: auto-generate → fix names/fillers → restyle font/size/position out of the UI zone → highlight two power-words → export with burned-in + sidecar .srt optional; classroom reads captions aloud for sense.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: caption each other's horizontal piece; partner reads ONLY captions (audio off) and must retell the content accurately; fix any line failing the retell test.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: **caption + lower-third pass** on both pieces; export new versions; verify on phone at 50% brightness. File to workbook.
