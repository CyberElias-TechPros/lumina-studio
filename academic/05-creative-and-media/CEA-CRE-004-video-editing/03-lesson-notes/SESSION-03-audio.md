## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Two students play their v2 before/after cuts; room rates "human" vs "radio ad" out loud.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Dialogue first.** Target −16 LUFS YouTube / −14 social. Peaks never red. Normalize every clip to match mouths and rooms — mixed rooms are a third wall nobody can watch through.
**Noise reduction, sanely.** Sample the noise, subtract gently (over-denoised = underwater); room tone 5s for patching silence seams.
**Music & ducking.** Licensed pack only; music under voice at −18dB to −22dB; sidechain or manual keyframes; end of song fades, not chops; no copyrighted afrobeats bed — class pack lives for this.
**SFX.** Whooshes/pops/clicks for graphic entrances — one per section, then silence; silence is classier than constant whoosh.
**Voiceover.** Record tail close-mic; punch-ins free; room echoed? closet recording trick (clothes are free acoustic foam).

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Live rescue of a "recorded beside the generator" clip projected: denoise −35% → normalise → low-cut 80Hz rumble → music bed slid to −20dB → sidechain dip under voiceover → final −14 LUFS read-out.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: **A1 audio rescue** — each fixes the same raw clip; compare LUFS and artefacts (underwater alarms); swap headphones; verdict.

## 70–95 · Independent practice (A1 submission)

Solo: complete **A1** — rescued voice clip + music bed + −14 LUFS export + two-line wave-map walkthrough (what was wrong, what you did). File to workbook.

## 95–110 · Correction & check
