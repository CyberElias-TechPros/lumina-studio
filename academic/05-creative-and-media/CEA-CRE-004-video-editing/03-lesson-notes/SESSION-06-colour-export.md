## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Exports at thumbnail size screened; the weakest text gets the rewrite now, in class.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Correct FIRST, grade second.** Correction: exposure, white balance (skin honest), contrast baseline, saturation +5.
Grade: gentle warm push (+5 temp) or teal/orange for "cinematic" — subtlety is the grade; heavy = Instagram filter cosplay.
**Lumetri-class basics.** Exposure/WB → contrast/highlights/shadows/whites/blacks → saturation/vibrance → HSL skin band protect (orange hue ±10) → vignette −8 max → done.
**Export specs.** H.264/H.265 · vertical 1080×1920 ≤60s @ 8–12Mbps · horizontal 1920×1080 @ 10–16Mbps · audio AAC 320kbps, −14 LUFS · YouTube/IG/TikTok/WhatsApp each watch-back tested after upload-processing (profiles differ!).

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Projected grade of one horizontal hero shot: correct → +8 warm → orange-HSL protect → vignette → before/after side-by-side; then export ×4 presets live; watch-backs at 25% volume (captions carry) and 75% (mix carries).

## 45–80 · Final project exports (observed)

Solo: export BOTH pieces to platform specs; run checklist (res, bitrate, LUFS, captions, filename
`2026-11-16-topic-vertical.mp4`); watch both once; sign declaration (original+licensed assets only).

## 80–105 · Show-and-tell & close

Per student: screen vertical piece; three best cuts named aloud (with timecodes); one question from room; assessor scores P1–P6; feedback forms; certificates gate restated.

## 100–110 · Correction & check

KC6: (1) Correct before grade why. (2) Skin HSL band. (3) Vertical bitrate. (4) LUFS target social. (5) Watch-back rule.

## 110–120 · Course close

Certificate gate: both exports pass watched-back platform check. Portfolio: the two videos + project files. Keep cut folders — every client becomes a sequel.

## Answers — KC6

1. Grading a broken white balance = painting a cracked wall. 2) Orange band ±10 protects skin from teal/orange crush. 3) 8–12 Mbps. 4) −14 LUFS-ish. 5) Once at 25% (captions) + once at 75% (mix) — both.
