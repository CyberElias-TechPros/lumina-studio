## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Cut on action.** Cut mid-motion (hand reaches → door opens) — the brain fills the gap; cutting on static reads like a slide show.
**Rhythm & pacing.** Vary shot length (wide→medium→close pattern), "one new idea per beat"; music's tempo gives you permission to cut on-beat; silence is a strong beat before the payoff.
**J-cut / L-cut.** Audio leads picture (J: voice starts before we see speaker) or lingers after (L: face reacts after line ends) — dialogue feels human; violence feels synced.
**Dead air.** "Umm", breath, pause between sentence and answer — cut all without mercy in short-form; natural pauses live in long-form WITH intent.
**Trimming tool discipline.** Ripple delete to keep sync; slip vs slide awareness; always work on a DUPLICATE sequence versioned `v1-assembly, v2-rough, v3-fine`.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Projected timeline: assembly with visible dead air; instructor J/L's the dialogue exchange; cuts on-action establishing → close-up; ripple-deletes 9 silences; plays before/after; uses ripple vs lift explain on screen.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

## 70–95 · Independent practice
