## What you will walk out with

Two finished pieces — a vertical ≤60-second piece and a 3–6-minute horizontal film — mixed, captioned,
graded gently and exported exactly where they'll live. Plus the edit-order discipline pros charge for.

## The six sessions

S1 Story & assembly (hook → payoff; footage triage; assembly edit) · S2 Cutting craft (action cuts, rhythm, J/L cuts, dead air) ·
S3 Audio (levels, noise, ducking, SFX, voiceover) · S4 Captions & text (styling, lower thirds, end cards) ·
S5 Transitions & graphics (restraint, motion, brand) · S6 Colour & export (correction → gentle grade → platform specs).

## Assessment weighting

KCs 10% · workbook 25% · A1 audio rescue 15% · final two videos 40% · show-and-tell 10%.
Gate: both exports pass platform checks watched-back.

## Classroom rules

Audio before beauty. Cut before effect. Watch your whole export once before asking for approval — with captions on.
