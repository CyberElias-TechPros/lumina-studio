## What you will walk out with

A mini brand package (logo, flyer, social post, business card) delivered the studio way: brief received,
drafts shown, corrections handled, exports in the right formats — plus the eye to know a broken layout at forty paces.

## The eight sessions

S1 Seeing (layout, alignment, balance, contrast, hierarchy, white space) · S2 Colour & type (theory, pairing, hierarchy) ·
S3 The tool I (interface, templates, elements) · S4 The tool II (layers, transparency, brand kit) · S5 Flyers & social posts ·
S6 Posters, cards, invitations, certificates, simple logos · S7 Client workflow (brief, corrections, exports) · S8 Final package + critique.

## Assessment weighting

KCs 10% · workbook 25% · A1 flyer + social set 15% · final package 40% · show-and-tell 10%.
Gate: export spec compliance (print + web trees).

## Classroom rules

Eyes before hands. No AI-generated brand kits submitted as your own design work without disclosure; corrections in writing; the client is always right — argue with reasons, not ego.
