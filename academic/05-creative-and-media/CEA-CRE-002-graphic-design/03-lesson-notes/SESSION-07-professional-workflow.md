## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Confessions read; room laughs, forgives, and teaches the ten-second fix out loud together.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**The brief is law.** WHO the audience, WHAT the deliverable(s), WHERE it lives (print/web), WHEN (deadline), HOW MANY revisions (two), WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE (one measurable ask — "parent sign-ups", "Saturday traffic"). Brief missing any = you ask BEFORE designing, not after.
**Corrections protocol.** Client writes them (or you paraphrase back in writing: "so: price bigger, venue smaller, logo mono — yes?") · two rounds included, round three priced · never argue taste with taste ("I prefer blue" is not a design reason — ask WHAT problem blue solves) · keep every version (v1/v2/vFINAL-real).
**File organisation & exports.** `ProjectName/brief.pdf, working/, export/print/, export/web/` · PDF-Print w/ marks, PNG transparent-logo, JPG social < 1MB, fonts embedded/flattened honesty — label what needs licensed fonts · naming `2026-11-16-client-artefact-vN.ext`.
**Delivery & sign-off.** Send exports + a one-page "what I did & why" note; get written approval ("approved for print" text); invoice AFTER sign-off, not before.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Projected live brief intake for Grace Stores rebrand REEL — instructor sends paraphrase-back email; v1 draft shown; two staged "client corrections" (price too small; venue date illegible); v2 engine; sign-off captured; export naming; invoice text; the whole 20 minutes is gold.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs role-play brief→v1: A is the client with a card (hair-salon launch: audience students; deliverable IG + flyer A5; two corrections staged); B does it live in the tool; both swap after sign-off captured in text.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: **brief-to-export gauntlet** — supplied 15-minute micro-brief; complete the full pipeline incl. paraphrase-back message + versioned exports + approval text file. File to workbook.

## 95–110 · Correction & check

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Brief, work, correct, deliver — the loop that keeps clients calling back. Next: final package.

## Answers — KC7
