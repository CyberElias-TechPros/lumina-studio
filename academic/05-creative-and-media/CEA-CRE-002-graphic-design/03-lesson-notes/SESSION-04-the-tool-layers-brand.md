## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Layers, depth, order.** Every element is a layer; stack order decides who sits on whom (text behind shape? wrong).
Group related items (logo lock-up = icon+wordmark grouped) · lock backgrounds before editing layers above · transparency for watermarks/duotone images (30–70%) · grouping = move as one, alignment controls live here.

**Cropping & frames.** Mask images into circles/rounded rects for consistency (all staff headshots same crop!) · background removal (tool or manual eraser) for product shots · replace rather than stretch (never stretch a man sideways).

**Brand kits operational.** Save once: hex colours, fonts, logo variants (light/dark/mono), spacing/grid; share kit with team; keep "do not stretch / recolour / re-set in Comic Sans" one-pager in the folder — brand decay is a team sport without it.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Live: build Grace Stores mini kit projected — logo lock-up group, mono variants, background removal on a bread photo (clean), duotone transparency overlay headline, saved brand folder; export 4 variants; folder tree shown.

## Answers — KC4

1. Later = nearer; text must sit above its shape. 2) Move/scale as one unit; alignment per group. 3) 30–70% keeps texture without legibility death. 4) Product shots on white/no background; headshots into frames. 5) Stops well-meaning recolouring — one page, taped in the folder, wins.
