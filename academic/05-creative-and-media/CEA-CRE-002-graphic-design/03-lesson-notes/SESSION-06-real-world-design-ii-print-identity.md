## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Five-answer flyers screened; the best QR gets phone-scans from the room in real time.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Print spec essentials.** 300 DPI at final size (a 1080px photo up-stretched = mush) · CMYK mindset (screens RGB lie bright; expect saturated-print shift) · bleed 3mm + safe margin 5mm · sizes: business card 90×54mm, flyer A5/A4, poster A3, invitation double-fold logic · PDF-Print export with marks = the print shop's love language.
**Business cards.** Name 1st, role 2nd, one contact priority, logo mono on reverse, no 7 fonts, no foil-fake-gradient; hand-feel matters — greyscale + one spot colour wins.
**Certificates & invitations.** Border discipline, centred stack or 1-2-column layout; seal + signature lines; cert names = who EXACTLY (test print the longest name!).
**Simple logos (honest scope).** Wordmark + symbol; 2 colours max; survives one-colour black print (photocopy test); 32px favicon-readable; NEVER clipart+gradient+bevel; disclose honestly if weak (everyone's first logo is).

## 45–70 · Guided practice

## 70–95 · Independent practice

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC6: (1) DPI for print. (2) Bleed vs safe margin. (3) Card size mm. (4) One-colour logo survival. (5) Longest-name test.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Print forgives no arrogance. Next: the client room — briefs, corrections, exports done professionally.

## Answers — KC6

1. 300 DPI at placed size. 2) Bleed 3mm (cut zone) vs safe margin 5mm (nothing critical outside it). 3) 90×54mm. 4) If it survives pure black, it survives everything. 5) Overflow must be caught BEFORE print, not at the ceremony.
