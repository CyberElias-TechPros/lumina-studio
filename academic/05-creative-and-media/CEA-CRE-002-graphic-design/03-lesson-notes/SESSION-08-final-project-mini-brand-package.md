## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

## 10–25 · Teaching block (briefing)

**The package (Grace Stores, assigned).** (1) Logo — wordmark+symbol, two variants (dark/light), PNG transparent + PDF.
(2) Flyer (A5) — print-PDF with bleed. (3) Social post — 1080×1080 PNG < 1MB. (4) Business card — 90×54mm, bleed.
(5) The defence — 3 minutes: brief understanding, hierarchy choices, one compromise you made and why, one you refused.
**Presentation protocol.** Work first; critique the decisions: "the eye hits X first — correct?" Room may ask one reason-question each; no taste-verdicts, only reason-verdicts.

## 25–80 · Screening & critique (observed)
