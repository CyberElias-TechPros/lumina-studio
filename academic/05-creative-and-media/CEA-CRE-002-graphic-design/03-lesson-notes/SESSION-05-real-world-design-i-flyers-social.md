## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Logo-abuse photos screened (blurred); room rioted — the drama heals the seeing muscle.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**The flyer contract.** What, where, when, how much, CTA — five answers ≤ one eye-scan (2 seconds). Church/event flyers local reality: info density acceptable if hierarchy bulletproof; WhatsApp statuses = dark/light contrast (feeds are bright); "broadcast graphics" portrait 1080×1920 same rules.
**Social formats.** IG feed 1080×1080 (safe zone 90px) · carousel 1080×1350 · story/reel cover 1080×1920 (bottom 250px text-free — UI) · Facebook 1200×630 link cards · WhatsApp JPG < 1MB for speed.
**Photo honesty.** Real products over stock — always; if stock, license; no fake before/afters; no price lies.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Live build: Grace Stores "Saturday Combo" flyer (A5) → IG post crop → WhatsApp status crop → print-PDF export; whiteboard the hierarchy (combo photo 60%, price 30%, CTA 10% arrow).

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs produce ONE event flyer from a shared brief (sungbo-egusi night: venue, date, price, CTA, QR-to-WhatsApp) in 35 minutes, printer-tested; swap with bench-mate; rubric-score hierarchy in one minute each.

## 70–95 · Independent practice (A1 build)

Solo: **A1 flyer** — from the supplied brief card (choice of 3: shop promo, church event, school WhatsApp announcement); exports print-PDF + web-PNG in `print/` and `web/` trees. Signed checklist filed.

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC5: (1) Flyer five-answers. (2) IG story safe text rule. (3) WhatsApp JPG size logic. (4) Real-photo rule. (5) QR-on-flyer scan test — you did it?

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

Every artefact is a promise with pixels. Next: print and identity — posters, cards, certificates, logos.

## Answers — KC5

1. What, where, when, how much, CTA. 2) Bottom ~250px free — platform UI sits there. 3) <1MB — networks here matter; slow images get skipped. 4) Real products > stock; licence or shoot your own. 5) Scan from across the desk with a real camera — untested QR = dead print.
