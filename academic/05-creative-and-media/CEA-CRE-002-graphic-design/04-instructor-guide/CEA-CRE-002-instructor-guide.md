**Do not distribute.** Keys in Section 7.

## 1. What this course is

Eye-hand-honesty training wearing a software course costume. The tool is free; the eyes are the tuition.
Every critique must name a visual reason — enforce this ruthlessly from week 1 or the room drowns in "I like it".

## 2. Preparation

Wall posters: hierarchy ladder, 60-30-10 swatch, type pairing cards, print-spec card, export tree poster;
colour wheels, A4 printouts of 15 real flyers per bench; test printer loaded; font-pairing flash cards; Grace Stores brief printed ×20.

## 3. Delivery model

105 min: 0–10 flyer-on-screen verdict · 10–25 teach (wall cards) · 25–45 demo · 45–70 guided/critique · 70–95 solo · 95–110 KC · 110–120 close.

## 4. Failure points

- S1: "It's ugly" with no reason — halt and demand the noun (alignment? contrast? white space?).
- S3: templates worshipped — re-run template-to-original until hands know the purge rhythm.
- S5: 3-colour-total ignored ("but this yellow is SO bright!") — 60-30-10 poster gets pointed at, wordlessly.
- S6: print spec hand-waved — every card goes through bleed verification ON PAPER before sign-off.
- S8 gate: export checklist signed only when the tree opens cleanly — never by vibe.

## 5. Assessment guidance

A1: print PDF with bleed opens in Acrobat with marks; web PNG ≤1MB at 1080px. Final: P1–P6, export-spec absolute.
Show-and-tell: reasoning density, not volume.

## 6. Equity & access

Colour-blind students: icon+label contrast checks; greyscale previews of every artefact; left-handed mouse setups; large-print briefs.

## 7. Knowledge-check keys

KC1: 1) Where does the eye land 1/2/3? 2) Symmetric formality / asymmetric energy. 3) Silence that lets the message land. 4) Size, weight, dark/light, warm/cool. 5) Unreadable at 3m = decoration.
KC2: 1) 60 main / 30 support / 10 accent. 2) Opposites vs neighbours. 3) Two families (display+text). 4) ≥9pt print / ≥16px web. 5) Script never carries body text.
KC3: 1) Start near target then purge theirs. 2) Any three dims. 3) PDF Print 300dpi + marks. 4) PNG transparency/graphic vs JPG photo compression. 5) Transparent background or boxed-white logo.
KC4: 1) Upper = nearer; text over its shape. 2) Move/scale/align as one. 3) 30–70% keeps texture legible. 4) Product/headshot frames. 5) Stops well-meaning brand-recolouring amateurs.
KC5: 1) What/where/when/money/CTA. 2) Bottom 250px free. 3) <1MB. 4) Real licensed subjects or owned shots. 5) Scan test off a printout, phone camera.
KC6: 1) 300 DPI. 2) Bleed 3 / safe 5. 3) 90×54. 4) Black-photocopy survival. 5) Longest-name overflow proof.
KC7: 1) Audience, deliverable, placement, deadline, revisions, success. 2) Verbal corrections drift; writing stops "I never said that". 3) Outcome reasons over colour opinions. 4) brief.pdf / working / export-print / export-web. 5) Payment after approval kills endless creep.
KC8: 1) Logo, flyer, social, card, defence. 2) Reason vs taste. 3) Any honest trade-off named. 4) Export spec: bleed + embedded + pixels. 5) Ten-minute daily poster autopsy.

## 8. Expected outputs & calibration

S1: every student tags ≥2 layouts right unprompted by S2. S4: Grace Stores kit exists per student before S5. S8: two-assessor file-tree check.

## 9. Rubric (0–4)

0 unsafe/unreadable · 1 heavy prompts · 2 competent basics w/ errors · 3 clean, on-brief, spec-true · 4 would be shown in a portfolio. Gate: export spec compliance.
