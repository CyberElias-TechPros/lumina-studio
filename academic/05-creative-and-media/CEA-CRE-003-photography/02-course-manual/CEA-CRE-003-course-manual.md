## What you will walk out with

Two assignment sets — a six-frame product/food set and a six-frame event/portrait set — edited,
exported, and defended frame-by-frame: what light, why this crop, why you left it alone after the first fix.

## The four sessions

S1 Composition & framing (thirds, lines, negative space, perspective) · S2 Light (direction, quality,
golden hour, DIY reflectors) · S3 Phone control (exposure, focus lock, white balance, HDR trap, raw) ·
S4 Editing (colour, contrast, clarity, batch consistency, export sizes, final portfolio).

## Assessment weighting

KCs 10% · workbook 25% · A1 product set 15% · final 12-frame + notes 40% · show-and-tell 10%.
Gate: every frame shot by you this term.

## Classroom rules

Consent before portraits. No filters pretending to be skill. Screens at 100% brightness for honest edits.
