## 1. Identity

- **Title:** Photography · **Department:** Creative & Media · **Level:** Absolute beginner
- **Duration:** 2 weeks · 4 sessions · 2/week · 105 min · **420 guided minutes**
- **Fee:** ₦30,000 · **Cohort:** max 15
- **Delivery:** On-site; phones required; reflectors/diffusers, clamp lamps, sample backdrops

## 2. Description

Twelve edited photographs across two assignments — one product/food set, one event/portrait set —
each with a short note on the decisions behind it: composition, light, exposure, edit restraint.

## 3. Purpose

Every SME, church programme, and wedding lives or dies on photos lately. This course trains eyes that see light and decisions before they touch an edit dial.

## 4. Audience

Absolute beginners · small business owners shooting their own stock · church/school media teams · aspiring event shooters.

## 5. Entry requirements

A phone with a working camera; curiosity; no art background.

## 6. Learning outcomes

1. Compose deliberately — thirds, lines, negative space, perspective — and know when to break the rules.
2. Read and use natural/artificial light; master the golden hour and window light.
3. Control exposure, focus, white balance on a phone; understand HDR's trap.
4. Edit for colour, contrast and clarity; batch-consistent; export for web and print.

## 9. Practical exercises

## 10. Assessment

| Component                                                       | Weight |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Knowledge checks (4 × 5)                                        | 10%    |
| Workbook practicals                                             | 25%    |
| Assignment A1 — product set (6 frames, edited)                  | 15%    |
| Final project — two assignment sets, 12 frames + decision notes | 40%    |
| Show-and-tell — your favourite frame and why                    | 10%    |

**Critical gate:** authenticity — no stolen/filtered stock submitted as student work; all frames must be shot by the student this term; failure = reassess once.

## 11. Critical behaviors

Clean the lens first · shoot, then cull ruthlessly · edit less than you want to · credit every collaborator.

## 12. Learning barriers

Auto-everything passivity; over-editing "HDR crunch"; fear of asking subjects to wait 10 seconds for light.

## 13–16. Delivery · QA · Resources · Completion

Loaner reflectors; model-release cards for portrait assignments; licensed backdrops. Attendance ≥ 3/4; gate passed; sets filed; feedback form before certificate.
