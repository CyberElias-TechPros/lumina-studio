## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Exposure by finger.** Tap-to-focus sets focus AND exposure point; hold to LOCK both (AE/AF lock); then slide exposure up/down manually — dark subject in bright room needs tapping the SUBJECT not the wall.
**White balance.** Auto usually fine; mixed light makes skin grey-orange — lock AWB if phone allows or fix in edit (S4); outside-midday shade is blue — warm it deliberately.
**HDR.** Merges exposures — good for high contrast LANDSCAPES, terrible for faces (waxy) and moving kids (ghosts) — learn its off switch on purpose.
**Formats & raw.** JPEG fine; raw (where available — most midrange+ phones) preserves edits like a pro RAW; file sizes 3–5× — use for finals, not breakfast.
**Stability.** Two hands, elbows in, lean on wall or lay phone on table/books — night shots breathe, nobody wins from long-exposure handshake portraits; timer 2s beats your finger-press-shake every time.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Projected phone screen: room with a black bag on white table — tap wall (bag = silhouette) vs tap bag (wall = blown) — class verdict · lock + slide ritual · HDR-face waxy reveal · timer vs trembling hand at 1/8s · raw vs jpeg crop-zoom test.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: each runs the tap-wall/tap-subject duel + one AE/AF lock scene each; swap phones; partner verifies the lock icon visible before exposure slid.

## 70–95 · Independent practice
