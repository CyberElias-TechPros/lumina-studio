## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Direction & quality.** FRONT light (flat, friendly, safe) · SIDE light (texture, drama, portraits) · BACK light (rim/silhouette — gorgeous, tricky exposure).
Hard light (direct sun, bare bulb) = crisp shadows, harsh skin · soft light (overcast, north window, diffused) = forgiving, premium product-look.
**Golden hour.** 45–60 min after sunrise / before sunset; warm, directional, kind; shadows long; every professional's free flex.
**DIY reflectors & diffusers.** White foam board / bedsheet as fill · translucent shower curtain as softener ·
oilcloth-lined rice sack (classic) bounces cool shadow warm · tinfoil scrunched on card for punch (careful).
**Indoors & artificial.** North-facing window = free softbox; avoid ceiling bulb (raccoon eyes); one strong side lamp + reflector beats three weak lamps; mixed colour temperatures — pick one master or fix in edit (S4).

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Live: portrait at the window — front/side/back versions; student face; exposure drifts; reflector raised fills shadow; diffuser over lamp jaw-drop; class votes frames as light changes.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: each shoots the other three ways (front/side/back) at the window; then same with reflector; compare exports; instructor photographs the comparison grid.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: **light diary** — 6 photos of one object (a spoon) taken hour-by-hour at home tonight; note window direction per frame. File to workbook (time-lapse of light).
