## 0–10 · Review & warm-up

Two projected photos (same subject): one dead-centre-dull, one off-thirds-alive. Room votes; name what changed.

## 10–25 · Teaching block

**Rule of thirds.** Grid 3×3; eyes/horizon/product sit on intersections, never dead-centre (unless symmetrical design is the point — doors, churches).
**Leading lines.** Roads, queues, mops of hair, gospel-chord drum kits — let lines walk the viewer's eye INTO the subject.
**Negative space.** Empty sky/wall/cloth on purpose; luxury looks like emptiness; product + 60% emptiness converts ₦ more than clutter.
**Perspective & angle.** Shoot at the subject's eye level (unless smallness/largeness is the story); straighten before everything; verticals vertical — tilted horizon kills more photos than bad light.
**Clean the frame.** Bin the laundry, step left, ask the aunty to move her bucket — the background is half the photo; half the room's garbage walks in the backdoor otherwise.

## 25–45 · Guided demonstration

Projected live shoot of a cup: dead-centre → thirds → leading-spoon version → negative-space version → tilted (no!) → fixed; room votes per version; instructor narrates WHY each works.

## 45–70 · Guided practice

Pairs: **thirds treasure hunt** — 12 framed subjects on the tables; shoot each from assigned rule-card (thirds/lines/negative/angle); compare against reference sheet.

## 70–95 · Independent practice

Solo: **your 5-frame study** of anything in the room, one rule each, straightened, exported small. File to workbook.

## 95–110 · Correction & check

KC1: (1) Thirds grid placement. (2) Leading lines job. (3) Negative space conversion use. (4) Verticals vertical why. (5) Two pre-shoot checks every time.

## 110–120 · Summary & next session

The frame is a decision. Next: the light that makes or breaks it.

## Answers — KC1

1. Eyes/horizon/product on intersections. 2) Walk the eye to the subject. 3) Premium feel + product focus in adverts. 4) Human brains read tilted horizons as mistakes even when unnoticed consciously. 5) Lens wiped + background scanned + horizon levelled.
