## The question this answers

You said: _"the lesser prices are for the theoretical aspects of the courses like
a crash course, while the bigger amounts are for full packages."_ Understood —
and the market data below says that is exactly how Port Harcourt is priced. It
also shows the current website only advertises the **cheap end** of every course,
while describing the **full-package outcomes** (build a real website, security
assessment, campaign, etc.). That mismatch is fixable, and fixing it is worth
money.

## What this means for CEA

Current catalogue: **₦20,000–₦60,000 for 2–6 week courses** (22 courses), plus
diplomas at **₦150,000–₦320,000 for 3–6 months**.

1. **The cheap catalogue is below market for its promises.** At ₦50,000 for Web
   Design (4 weeks) you are below Brandwone's _Basic_ (₦55,000 / 3 weeks) and
   nowhere near a portfolio-building package. If those courses are the
   _theoretical / crash_ versions, the price is right — but the current website
   copy promises the full-package outcomes.
2. **The full-package band is where the market pays.** ₦100,000–₦300,000 is
   normal for a project-based short course; ₦430,000–₦537,000 for 16-week
   professional tracks (Loctech). CEA's diplomas already sit correctly at
   ₦150,000–₦320,000, so the missing piece is a **short full-package tier**.
3. **Your differentiators against these competitors are real and cheap**:
   central Port Harcourt classroom (Ebony Road/Rumuola), small classes, one
   instructor who is also the operator, an actual deliverable per course,
   verifiable certificates, and Paystack/bank payment. Say those loudly at the
   full-package price point.
4. **Nobody at the cheap end publishes outcomes** — they sell "training".
   Publish _what you leave with_ (the deliverable) and the two tiers separate
   themselves in the visitor's head without a discount war.

## Recommended structure (two tiers per course, one career track)

Name the tiers so no one can confuse them:

| Tier | Name on the site                       | What it includes                                                                                                          | Price logic                                                         |
| ---- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A    | **Crash Course — Theory & Demo**       | 1–2 sessions/week, taught + demonstrated, class notes, attendance certificate. No project assessment, no portfolio piece. | Keep today's ₦20,000–₦60,000 band.                                  |
| B    | **Full Package — Practice to Project** | Same syllabus + guided lab, a graded deliverable you keep, portfolio review, certificate of completion.                   | ₦100,000–₦250,000 (below Loctech/Myteacher, above Brandwone Basic). |
| C    | **Professional Diploma** (unchanged)   | 3–6 months, 3 days/week, cohort, career modules.                                                                          | ₦150,000–₦320,000 (already published).                              |
