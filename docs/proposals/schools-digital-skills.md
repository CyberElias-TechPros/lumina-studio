## 1. Introduction

The world is changing rapidly, and digital skills are becoming increasingly
important in education, employment, business and everyday life. While academic
education remains essential, students also need practical skills that prepare them
for the digital world they are growing into.

Many students use phones and computers every day, but using technology for
entertainment is different from using technology to create, solve problems, work
and eventually earn.

Cyber Elias Academy proposes a practical Digital Skills Programme for primary and
secondary schools, designed to expose students to useful digital skills early. The
programme allows students to learn one practical digital skill per term, through
structured lessons delivered on agreed days each week **inside the school**.

> _"Every student should have the opportunity to leave school with more than
> academic certificates — they should also have practical skills they can use in
> the real world."_

## 2. About Cyber Elias Academy

Cyber Elias Academy is a technology training and skills-development organisation
focused on making digital education practical, accessible and easy to understand.
Our approach is built on the belief that students should not only learn about
technology but use it to create things and solve real problems — progressing from
basic digital literacy to career-relevant skills.

Registered in Nigeria as **Cyber Elias Academy Ltd (RC 8413776)**. Certificates
are issued by the academy and are publicly verifiable; they are not a university
degree or a government licence.

## 3. The problem we are addressing

Many young people complete their education with limited practical exposure to
digital tools. Students may know how to use social media, watch videos or play
games, but may not know how to:

- Create a professional document or a presentation
- Analyse information using spreadsheets
- Design a simple graphic or build a website
- Create digital content or use AI responsibly
- Protect themselves online
- Solve basic technology problems
- Turn a digital skill into a service
- Build a simple digital project

There is therefore an opportunity for schools to introduce practical digital
education earlier and more systematically.

## 4. Our proposed solution

A term-based programme where participating students learn **one focused skill per
academic term**. Instead of many unrelated topics, each term has a clear learning
objective. Students attend classes on agreed days each week and progressively
develop their abilities.

**The learning model: LEARN → PRACTICE → CREATE → PRESENT → CERTIFY**

Students do not simply attend classes — they practise, then complete a practical
project, present it, and receive a certificate.

## 5. Programme objectives

1. Introduce students to practical digital skills.
2. Improve students' confidence with computers and technology.
3. Develop creativity and problem-solving ability.
4. Introduce students to modern technology and AI.
5. Encourage responsible and safe use of the internet.
6. Help students discover areas of technology they may pursue professionally.
7. Give students practical projects they can showcase.
8. Introduce entrepreneurship and digital income concepts at an appropriate age.
9. Prepare students for further technology education.
10. Help the school provide relevant, future-oriented skills.

## 7. Term-by-term pathway

The school may choose different combinations depending on age and interests. A
three-year example:

|            | Year 1                      | Year 2            | Year 3                         |
| ---------- | --------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **Term 1** | Computer & Digital Literacy | Web Design        | Coding & Programming           |
| **Term 2** | Microsoft Office            | Digital Marketing | AI & Digital Productivity      |
| **Term 3** | Graphic Design              | Content Creation  | Freelancing & Entrepreneurship |

This creates a progressive pathway rather than random computer classes.

## 8. Project-based learning

Every term ends with something the student made, so parents, teachers and
management can see what was learned:

| Skill area        | Practical project                                |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Graphic Design    | A school event flyer                             |
| Microsoft Office  | A formatted report and a presentation            |
| Excel / Data      | A simple personal or school budget               |
| Web Design        | A basic website                                  |
| Coding            | A simple interactive project or game             |
| Content Creation  | A short educational video                        |
| Digital Marketing | A promotional campaign for an imaginary business |
| Entrepreneurship  | A business idea, developed and presented         |

## 9. Delivery

Cyber Elias Academy will agree a timetable with the school. A possible structure:

- **Frequency:** 1–2 practical sessions per week
- **Duration:** 60–120 minutes per session, depending on age group and timetable
- **When:** during designated school periods, after school hours, during ICT
  periods, or as an extracurricular programme — whichever suits the school
- **Where:** the school's computer room/lab, or a CEA arrangement where the school
  has no lab (discussed case by case)

## 10. Assessment

Assessment is based on **practical performance**, not attendance alone:

- Class exercises · practical assignments · projects · presentations · skill
  demonstrations · an end-of-term practical assessment

The question that matters: _**"Can the student actually use the skill?"**_

## 11. Certification and reporting

Students who complete their selected course receive a **Cyber Elias Academy
Certificate of Completion**, which carries a code verifiable at
**cea.ng/certificates/verify**.

The school also receives:

- Student participation records
- A course completion report
- Practical project records (with photographs, where permission is given)
- An overall end-of-term programme report

## 12. Digital Skills Passport

Each completed course is added to the student's **Digital Skills Passport** — a
visible, printable record of the practical skills they have developed through
their time at the school, and a document they can carry into further study or
work.

**Student Digital Skills Passport** ✓ Computer Literacy ✓ Microsoft Office
✓ Graphic Design ✓ Web Design ✓ Coding ✓ Digital Marketing ✓ AI Literacy
✓ Cyber Safety ✓ Entrepreneurship

## 18. Long-term vision

A student should be able to progress through this journey:

> "I don't know how to use a computer." → "I can use a computer." →
> "I can create something with technology." → "I can solve a problem using
> technology." → "I have a skill that can create opportunities for me."

That is the journey we want to help schools create.

## 19. Conclusion

Education should prepare students not only for examinations but for the world they
will enter after school. Technology will continue to influence almost every
industry, profession and business. Introducing practical digital skills early
gives students the opportunity to explore technology, discover their interests and
develop useful abilities while they are still in school.

students with structured, practical and affordable digital-skills education.
